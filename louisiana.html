Louisiana ID Laws: What You Need to Know

Louisiana issues driver's licenses and identification cards through the Office of Motor Vehicles. This guide explains Louisiana ID requirements, REAL ID documents, residency, voter identification, renewal, replacement, fees and rules for non-U.S. citizens.

Louisiana driver's license requirements

Louisiana's standard personal driver's license is a Class E license. First-time applicants must provide the required identity documents, Social Security information and proof of residency and complete the applicable knowledge, vision and road-skills requirements.

The OMV says a Class E license generally expires on the applicant's birthday approximately six years after issuance. The current basic license fee is $32.25 for applicants under age 70, with a lower base fee for applicants age 70 and older. Certain field offices may add a service fee of up to $8. citeturn4search1

Teen drivers

Louisiana uses a graduated licensing process for younger drivers. Students taking driver's education must obtain a Temporary Instruction Permit before participating in the behind-the-wheel portion of instruction and before the road-skills test. The exact permit and license requirements depend on age and the applicant's stage in the graduated system.

New Louisiana residents

A person moving to Louisiana must transfer an out-of-state driver's license within 30 days of establishing domicile. The OMV requires a primary document, Social Security number, proof of residency and the photo license from the previous state, or an official driving record when the photo license is unavailable. The out-of-state license is surrendered when the Louisiana license is issued. citeturn4search12

Louisiana State ID without a driver's license

Louisiana calls its non-driver credential a Special Identification Card. Any Louisiana resident can obtain one, subject to the applicable identity-document requirements. The card clearly states that it does not authorize the holder to operate a motor vehicle. citeturn2search1

Louisiana's current system issues six-year ID cards to people age 16 and older. Residents age 15 and younger can receive either a two-year or six-year card under the statutory program. citeturn4search3

Holding an ID and driver's license

Louisiana's identification-card rules distinguish the Special ID from a driver's license. The OMV determines whether an applicant is eligible for a particular credential based on the person's existing credentials and circumstances.

Free Special ID for voters

Louisiana waives the Special ID fee for a person who does not have a Louisiana driver's license when the applicant presents a voter registration card or certificate. The Secretary of State also confirms that registered voters can obtain a free Special ID for voting. citeturn2search0turn0search1

Documents needed for a Louisiana ID

Louisiana's OMV uses an identification-document process that varies depending on whether the applicant is obtaining a standard credential, REAL ID, first-time credential or credential based on temporary lawful status.

Identity and date of birth

First-time applicants generally provide an original or certified identity document. Common qualifying documents include a certified birth certificate, valid U.S. passport or qualifying citizenship or immigration documents.

Louisiana requires original or certified copies for many credential transactions. Photocopies should not be assumed to be acceptable unless the OMV's current checklist specifically permits them.

Social Security information

A Social Security number is collected for Louisiana driver's licenses. Applicants who are not eligible for an SSN must follow the OMV's process for obtaining an SSN denial letter from the Social Security Administration. The current OMV site specifically directs applicants who are ineligible for an SSN to obtain that denial letter. citeturn4search8

Name changes

Louisiana requires the name change to be recorded with the Social Security Administration before an OMV name-change transaction. Applicants then bring original documentation supporting the change, such as a certified marriage license, divorce decree or qualifying court document. citeturn4search0

Proof of Louisiana residency

Proof of Louisiana residence is required for driver's license and identification-card transactions. A first-time REAL ID application generally requires two proofs of residence.

Acceptable residency evidence can include utility bills, financial statements, lease or mortgage documents, insurance records, government mail and other documents showing the applicant's name and Louisiana residential address. The exact acceptable-document list depends on the transaction.

Changing your address

Louisiana law requires a driver to notify OMV of an address change within 10 days. The address-change transaction requires the current driver's license, proof of residency and proof of insurance for any vehicle the applicant owns. The current OMV FAQ lists a $17 license transaction fee, with a local fee of up to $8 potentially applying. citeturn4search0

Louisiana REAL ID requirements

A Louisiana REAL ID-compliant driver's license or identification card displays a gold circle with a star cutout in the upper-right corner. Louisiana also issues non-REAL-ID credentials that remain usable for ordinary state purposes. citeturn4search18turn5search2

REAL ID document categories: Louisiana's OMV REAL ID guidance calls for proof of identity, lawful status, date of birth, Social Security information and two proofs of residence. The applicant must meet the OMV's current document checklist for the particular situation. citeturn4search18

Since May 7, 2025, a REAL ID or another federally accepted identity document is required for covered federal purposes such as boarding federally regulated commercial aircraft, accessing certain federal facilities and entering nuclear power plants. A passport or another TSA-accepted document can be used instead. citeturn2search6

Getting REAL ID for the first time

If a Louisiana resident does not currently have a REAL ID credential, the applicant must visit an OMV field office or authorized Public Tag Agent location with the required documents. Online renewal of a non-REAL-ID credential does not automatically add the REAL ID indicator. citeturn5search0

Louisiana voter identification requirements

Louisiana voters must identify themselves before voting by giving their name and address and presenting an accepted form of photo identification. The Secretary of State lists a Louisiana driver's license, Louisiana Special ID, LA Wallet digital driver's license, U.S. military ID with the voter's name and picture or another generally recognized picture ID containing the voter's name and signature. citeturn0search0turn0search1

If you do not have photo ID

A voter without an available photo ID can complete a Voter Identification Affidavit after answering identifying questions. The Secretary of State notes that a voter using the affidavit remains subject to challenge under state law. citeturn0search4

Free Special ID

All registered Louisiana voters can obtain a free Special ID from OMV by presenting their voter registration information card. If the card has been misplaced, the voter can obtain or print voter-registration information through the Louisiana Voter Portal. citeturn0search1

Voter registration and identification

Louisiana's voter-registration system can use a Louisiana driver's license or Special ID number for online registration. If neither has been issued, the voter can use the paper process and provide the required Social Security or alternative identification information. citeturn0search2

Renewing a Louisiana ID or driver's license

Eligible Louisiana residents can renew a driver's license or ID online, through LA Wallet or by mail when they meet the OMV's eligibility rules. OMV says eligible residents can generally renew online up to 90 days before expiration, while field-office or Public Tag Agent renewal can be completed earlier. citeturn5search2

A person is generally not eligible for online or mail renewal when the previous renewal was also completed remotely, the credential has been expired for 12 months or more, the credential was cancelled or lost, the driver's license has a suspension or flag or the applicant is within the age-based restrictions listed by OMV. citeturn5search2

For an ID card, Louisiana's current system issues six-year cards to people age 16 and older. Qualified citizens can renew online, at an OMV field office or at a Public Tag Agent. citeturn4search3

Replacing a lost, stolen or damaged Louisiana ID

Louisiana provides duplicate driver's license and identification-card services through OMV. Online duplicate services are available for eligible customers through the current OMV platform. A person who has lost a credential may be ineligible for an online renewal and may need an OMV transaction in person. citeturn5search1turn5search2

If the replacement also involves a name or address change, the applicant must complete the applicable change transaction and provide supporting documents. Address changes cannot be made through the online ID-card renewal process. citeturn5search0

Name and address changes

Louisiana requires the name change to be updated with the Social Security Administration before changing the name on a driver's license. The OMV then requires original documentation supporting the legal change. citeturn4search0

A driver's license address change must be reported within 10 days. The applicant must appear in person with the driver's license, proof of residency and proof of insurance for vehicles owned by the applicant. citeturn4search0

Louisiana's online ID-card renewal system does not allow changes to personal information, including address. A customer who needs to make a change must visit an OMV location. citeturn5search0

Louisiana IDs for non-U.S. citizens

Louisiana issues driver's licenses and identification cards to eligible non-U.S. citizens who meet the OMV's identity, lawful-presence, Social Security and residency requirements.

Temporary lawful-status applicants may receive credentials with expiration periods tied to their authorized period of stay. The OMV has a separate policy for immigrant and non-immigrant issuance and can require immigration documents and additional verification. citeturn5search1

Applicants without a Social Security number

People who are not eligible for a Social Security number must obtain an SSN denial letter from the Social Security Administration before completing the Louisiana credential transaction. citeturn4search8

Counterfeit, altered and borrowed identification

Louisiana credentials are issued by OMV after the applicant's identity and required documentation are verified. A counterfeit credential, an altered genuine credential or another person's genuine license or ID does not become a valid credential for the person presenting it.

For general educational background on what a fake ID is and how it differs from a genuine government-issued credential, see What Is a Fake ID? Definition, Types and Legal Facts. Louisiana law and official OMV guidance remain the controlling sources for Louisiana-specific requirements.

Louisiana ID law FAQs

Can I get a Louisiana ID without a driver's license?

Yes. Louisiana residents can obtain a Special Identification Card. It is an identification credential and does not authorize driving.

How many residency documents do I need for a Louisiana REAL ID?

Louisiana's REAL ID checklist generally requires two proofs of residence, along with identity, lawful status, date of birth and Social Security information.

Do I need a REAL ID to drive in Louisiana?

No. A non-REAL-ID Louisiana credential remains valid for ordinary state purposes. REAL ID matters for covered federal purposes.

Does Louisiana require photo ID to vote?

Yes. Louisiana voters present an accepted photo ID or can complete a Voter Identification Affidavit if no photo ID is available.

Can I get a free Louisiana ID for voting?

Yes. Registered voters can obtain a free Louisiana Special ID by presenting their voter registration information card to OMV.

Can non-U.S. citizens get a Louisiana ID?

Eligible non-U.S. citizens can receive Louisiana credentials when they meet the identity, lawful-presence, Social Security and residency requirements.

Can I renew my Louisiana ID online?

Eligible customers can renew an ID online. The online process cannot be used to change personal information, and a first-time REAL ID upgrade requires an in-person document check.

Georgia ID Laws: What You Need to Know

Georgia's Department of Driver Services issues driver's licenses, permits and state identification cards. This guide covers Georgia's document requirements, residency rules, REAL ID, voter identification, renewal, replacement, fees and requirements for non-U.S. citizens.

Georgia driver's license requirements

Georgia's ordinary non-commercial driver's license is a Class C license. New drivers must meet the age-specific permit, testing and supervised-driving requirements that apply to them.

For a new or transferred Georgia credential, DDS requires documentation that verifies identity, residential address and U.S. citizenship or lawful status. The applicant must also provide the full Social Security number through the required online form.

New Georgia residents

A person who establishes residency in Georgia and has a driver's license from another state must transfer the credential through DDS. The out-of-state license, permit or ID generally must be surrendered. If the previous credential was lost or stolen, DDS requires a driving record or motor vehicle report from the issuing state, subject to its current date requirements.

Teen drivers

Georgia uses graduated licensing for young drivers. The learner's permit, Class D provisional license and Class C license have different age, testing, education and supervised-driving requirements.

Georgia state ID without a driver's license

Georgia DDS allows eligible residents to apply for a state identification card. The basic eligibility rule says a person who is old enough to sign their name or make a mark indicating a legal signature can apply.

If you have a valid driver's license or ID from another U.S. state, you generally must surrender that credential before Georgia will issue a Georgia ID. A valid foreign driver's license does not have to be surrendered.

A Georgia driver can also hold an ID card after surrendering the driver's license. DDS says applicants can apply for an ID when a Georgia or out-of-state driver's license is valid, suspended, revoked, canceled or expired, subject to the surrender rules.

Documents needed for a Georgia ID

Georgia DDS requires documents that verify identity, residential address and U.S. citizenship or lawful presence. The standard REAL ID document list is used for Georgia credentials.

Identity

For U.S. citizens, one acceptable identity document can be a certified U.S. birth certificate, unexpired U.S. passport or passport card, Consular Report of Birth Abroad, certificate of naturalization or certificate of citizenship.

If the identity document does not show your current legal name, DDS requires documentation covering the name-change history, such as a certified marriage document, divorce decree, court order or adoption document.

Social Security number

Your full Social Security number must be provided on Georgia's License/ID/Permit Form. If DDS cannot verify it electronically, you may need to bring acceptable documentation showing the full number.

Document language

Georgia DDS states that documents must be in English. Applicants should use the current DDS document list for the exact credential and immigration category involved.

Proof of Georgia residency

Georgia requires two documents showing Georgia residency for a REAL ID-compliant license or ID.

DDS gives examples such as utility or phone bills dated within the last six months that show the applicant's name and street address. Other acceptable documents can include lease agreements, mortgage documents, bank statements, insurance documents and government correspondence.

The address documents are intended to establish where the applicant lives in Georgia. A document that does not meet DDS's current name, address or date requirements may be rejected even if it is otherwise an official record.

Georgia REAL ID requirements

Georgia calls its REAL ID program Secure ID. DDS says all Georgia driver's licenses and identification cards issued after 2012 are REAL ID-compliant and show either a gold or black star in the upper-right corner.

For applicants who need a REAL ID or are transferring an out-of-state or foreign credential, DDS requires original or certified documents establishing identity, Social Security information and Georgia residency. Non-U.S. citizens must also establish lawful status.

Federal use

REAL ID-compliant Georgia credentials can be used for federal purposes covered by the REAL ID rules, including boarding federally regulated commercial aircraft and entering covered federal facilities.

Georgia also notes that only one physical credential can carry the REAL ID star at a time. For example, if your Georgia driver's license has the star, your physical Georgia ID card will not also be REAL ID compliant.

Georgia voter identification rules

Georgia requires photo identification when voting either in person or absentee.

Voting in person

Georgia accepts seven forms of photo identification for in-person voting:

  • Any valid state or federal government-issued photo ID, including a free Georgia ID
  • Georgia driver's license, even if expired
  • Student ID from a Georgia public college or university
  • Valid employee photo ID issued by a qualifying government entity
  • Valid U.S. passport
  • Valid U.S. military photo ID
  • Valid tribal photo ID containing a photograph

If you do not have an acceptable photo ID, Georgia provides a free Voter ID Card through county registrar offices or DDS offices.

Absentee voting

For an absentee ballot application, voters generally provide the number from a driver's license or state-issued ID. If neither is available, Georgia allows a copy or photo of another accepted identification document. The state also lists certain documents showing the voter's name and address, such as a current utility bill, bank statement, paycheck, government check or other government document.

Renewing a Georgia ID

Georgia DDS offers online renewal for eligible customers. To renew an ID online, the applicant must generally be a Georgia resident, at least 18 and a U.S. citizen, and the current document must be a REAL ID credential with the gold or black star.

Online renewal is not available when the applicant needs to change identifying information or when other DDS restrictions apply. Applicants who hold a REAL ID generally do not need to resubmit REAL ID documents unless their name or address has changed or another identity-verification service is requested.

If a driver's license has been expired for two years or more, DDS requires additional testing before a new license can be issued.

Replacing a lost, stolen or damaged Georgia ID

Georgia DDS provides replacement services for licenses and ID cards. The exact process depends on whether you are replacing a credential without changing information or also updating your name, address or other identifying information.

If your out-of-state license or ID was lost or stolen before you transferred to Georgia, DDS requires a certified driving record or motor vehicle report from the state that issued it. DDS says the report must meet its current date requirement.

If a credential was stolen, replacing the card is only one part of protecting your identity. Review accounts and records for suspicious activity if personal information was exposed.

Name and address changes

Georgia DDS requires documentation for legal name changes. If the name on the identity document differs from your current legal name, you must provide the documents needed to establish the complete name-change history.

For address changes, DDS requires proof when the address on the credential is updated. REAL ID holders generally do not need to repeat the complete document process unless their name or address changes or another identity-verification service is requested.

Georgia IDs for non-U.S. citizens

Georgia issues driver's licenses and identification cards to eligible non-U.S. citizens who can establish identity, lawful status and Georgia residency.

Non-U.S. citizens must present valid immigration documents in person at a DDS Customer Service Center. DDS lists different combinations of foreign passports, visas, I-94 records, I-797 notices, Employment Authorization Documents and other federal documents depending on immigration category.

Georgia requires the name on a driver's license to exactly match the original immigration documents. DDS also states that a non-U.S. citizen who establishes Georgia residency must obtain a Georgia driver's license within 30 days when required to drive in the state.

Social Security exceptions

Applicants who are not eligible for a Social Security number may need to complete the DDS Certification of Social Security Denial Status Form. Non-citizens who have DHS work authorization must apply for an SSN.

Temporary extension

DDS provides a one-time 120-day extension for certain existing non-citizen customers whose Georgia license, permit or ID has expired or is expiring within 30 days and who can show that they have requested an extension of lawful stay using the required I-797C documentation.

Georgia ID fees

Georgia DDS maintains its current license, permit and ID fee schedule separately from the document requirements. The amount depends on the credential and transaction, so this page does not hard-code a fee that may change without notice.

Georgia does provide a free Voter ID Card through DDS or a county registrar's office for eligible registered voters who need one for voting.

Before paying for an ID, renewal or replacement, check the current Georgia DDS Fees and Terms page.

Counterfeit, altered and borrowed identification

A Georgia driver's license or ID is tied to the applicant's verified identity, lawful status and state records. A counterfeit credential, an altered genuine credential or someone else's genuine credential is not equivalent to a valid credential issued to you.

Georgia's DDS document-verification process is designed around original or certified source documents and identity checks. Georgia ID law FAQs

Can I get a Georgia ID without a driver's license?

Yes. Georgia DDS issues state identification cards separately from driver's licenses. A person must meet the applicable identity, residency and lawful-presence requirements.

How many proof-of-residency documents does Georgia require?

Two documents proving Georgia residency are required for a REAL ID-compliant license or ID. DDS examples include utility or phone bills showing the applicant's name and street address.

Are Georgia IDs REAL ID compliant?

Georgia says all driver's licenses and IDs issued after 2012 are REAL ID-compliant and have a gold or black star in the upper-right corner.

Does Georgia require ID to vote?

Yes. Georgia requires photo identification for in-person and absentee voting. The state provides a free voter ID card for eligible voters who need one.

Can non-U.S. citizens get a Georgia ID?

Yes, eligible non-U.S. citizens can obtain a Georgia driver's license or ID when they establish identity, lawful status and Georgia residency.

Can I use an expired Georgia driver's license to vote?

Yes. Georgia lists a Georgia driver's license as an acceptable in-person voting ID even if it is expired.

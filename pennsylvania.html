Pennsylvania ID Laws: What You Need to Know

Pennsylvania has separate requirements for standard driver's licenses and photo ID cards, REAL ID and voting identification. This guide explains the documents, residency rules, fees, renewals, replacements and special requirements that apply to Pennsylvania residents.

Last reviewed: September 26, 2026

Pennsylvania driver's license requirements

Applicants for a Pennsylvania driver's license must establish identity, Social Security information and Pennsylvania residency. PennDOT's current first-license guidance requires two proofs of residency for applicants age 18 or older. Applicants age 16 or 17 do not need those two residency proofs at the initial licensing stage. citeturn1search6

First Pennsylvania license

Applicants complete the applicable learner's permit and licensing process, including the required testing. PennDOT's current fee schedule lists the initial permit and four-year license at $45.50. citeturn1search1

Proof of identity

PennDOT accepts specific identity documents listed on its driver-license forms and document guides. If the applicant's current name differs from the identity document, original legal name-change documentation such as a marriage certificate or court order may be required. citeturn1search6turn1search2

Social Security number

A Social Security card is among the documents PennDOT accepts for licensing. Applicants who do not have a Social Security card should follow PennDOT's current alternatives and eligibility instructions rather than relying on an unofficial document list. citeturn1search6

Pennsylvania photo ID cards

Pennsylvania offers PennDOT photo identification cards to residents who need official identification but do not hold a driver's license. A photo ID card is available to individuals age 10 and older. citeturn1search11

Applicants under 18

Applicants under 18 do not need to provide proof of residency when a parent, guardian or person in loco parentis accompanies them to the Driver License Center. The accompanying adult must satisfy PennDOT's applicable requirements. citeturn1search11

Applicants age 18 and older

Adult applicants must provide proof of identity and Pennsylvania residency. PennDOT's general documentation rules require two proofs of residency for adult driver's-license and photo-ID applications. citeturn1search2

Current ID fees

The current fee schedule lists $43.50 for an initial photo ID, $44.50 for renewal and $42.50 for replacement. citeturn1search1

Documents needed for a Pennsylvania ID

PennDOT maintains separate acceptable-document lists for U.S. citizens and non-U.S. citizens. Applicants should use the current PennDOT list because the accepted documents depend on the credential and the applicant's status. citeturn1search2

Identity

Common identity documents include an eligible U.S. birth certificate or valid U.S. passport. For REAL ID, PennDOT requires an original or certified copy. Photocopies are not accepted. citeturn1search10

Social Security

For REAL ID, PennDOT accepts a Social Security card, W-2, SSA-1099, non-SSA-1099 or pay stub showing the applicant's full nine-digit SSN and current legal name. citeturn1search10

Legal name changes

If the applicant's current legal name differs from the identity document, PennDOT can require certified marriage certificates, a court order or an amended birth certificate, depending on the circumstances. citeturn1search12

Proof of Pennsylvania residency

PennDOT generally requires two proofs of Pennsylvania residency for adult driver's-license and photo-ID applications. If an applicant has no bills in their own name, PennDOT provides a process involving the person they reside with plus a second document showing the applicant's name and address. citeturn1search2

Examples

  • Current Pennsylvania driver's license or photo ID
  • Pennsylvania vehicle registration card
  • Auto insurance card
  • Utility bill showing name and address
  • W-2 or pay stub
  • Tax record
  • Lease or mortgage document

Physical address

PennDOT requires a Pennsylvania address for driver's licenses and photo IDs. A P.O. Box by itself is not a substitute for the required Pennsylvania residence address. citeturn1search16

Pennsylvania REAL ID requirements

REAL ID is optional in Pennsylvania. A REAL ID has a gold star in the upper-right corner and can be used for covered federal purposes such as domestic air travel and entry to certain federal facilities. A standard Pennsylvania credential remains valid for driving and other state purposes. citeturn0search0turn0search1

REAL ID checklist

  • One proof of identity and lawful status
  • One proof of Social Security number
  • Two proofs of Pennsylvania residency
  • Proof of legal name change, if applicable

PennDOT requires originals or certified copies. Photocopies are not accepted. citeturn1search10

One-time REAL ID fee

The first REAL ID costs a one-time $30 REAL ID fee plus the applicable standard renewal fee. PennDOT states that once a customer has a REAL ID, future driver's-license renewals automatically remain REAL ID-compliant unless the customer opts out, with no additional REAL ID fee. citeturn1search12

Applying before renewal

You do not have to wait for your license to expire. If PennDOT has not already verified your documents, you generally need to visit a Driver License Center so the documents can be reviewed and imaged. Same-day REAL ID Centers can issue the credential the same day. citeturn0search1turn1search12

Non-U.S. citizens

Additional documentation can be required based on immigration status. PennDOT's REAL ID document list identifies the lawful-status documents accepted for non-U.S. citizens. citeturn0search1turn1search10

Pennsylvania voter identification requirements

Pennsylvania does not require every voter to show ID at the polls. The identification requirement applies when a voter is voting for the first time in a particular election district or polling place. Returning voters generally do not need to show ID unless otherwise noted in the poll book. citeturn1search0turn1search5

Accepted photo ID

  • Pennsylvania driver's license or PennDOT ID card
  • ID issued by a Commonwealth agency
  • ID issued by the U.S. Government
  • U.S. passport
  • U.S. Armed Forces ID
  • Student ID, including an electronic or digital student ID
  • Employee ID

For first-time voters at a polling place, an acceptable photo ID must be valid under the State Election Board's current rules. citeturn1search3

Non-photo ID

A first-time voter who does not have an accepted photo ID can instead use an approved non-photo document showing the voter's name and address. Examples include a county voter registration confirmation, government-issued non-photo ID, firearm permit, current utility bill, bank statement, paycheck or government check. citeturn1search0turn1search3

If identification is required but unavailable

A registered voter who is required to show ID but cannot provide it may be issued a provisional ballot. County election officials determine whether the provisional ballot can be counted under the applicable rules. citeturn1search9

Mail-in and absentee voting

Pennsylvania uses separate identification requirements for mail-in and absentee ballot applications. Registered voters generally provide a valid Pennsylvania driver's license or PennDOT ID number, the last four digits of an SSN or qualifying alternative identification. Certain UOCAVA and VAEH voters are exempt from the ID requirement. citeturn1search4turn1search7

Renewing a Pennsylvania driver's license or ID

Pennsylvania non-commercial driver's licenses are generally issued for four years, with a two-year renewal option for eligible drivers age 65 and older. The current standard renewal fee is $39.50 for four years and $27.50 for the two-year age-65-plus renewal. citeturn1search1

REAL ID renewal

After the first REAL ID is issued, future driver's-license renewals automatically include the REAL ID designation unless the customer chooses to opt out. No additional REAL ID fee is charged on later renewals. citeturn1search12

Renewal and address updates

PennDOT provides online and mail renewal options for eligible customers. A non-commercial driver's-license address change has no PennDOT fee. citeturn1search1

Replacing a lost, stolen or damaged Pennsylvania ID

PennDOT offers duplicate and replacement services for driver's licenses and photo IDs. The current fee schedule lists $42.50 for a duplicate or replacement driver's license and $42.50 for replacement of a photo ID card. citeturn1search1

Camera card

A duplicate camera card for a license or ID is currently $5. A replacement license or ID transaction is separate from the camera-card fee. citeturn1search1

Lost documents

If the credential was stolen or identity information may have been compromised, consider reporting the incident to the appropriate authorities and monitor accounts for possible identity misuse. PennDOT's replacement process is the official route for obtaining a new state credential.

Name and address changes

Name changes

PennDOT requires legal documentation connecting the former and current names when the name on the credential needs to change. Examples include a certified marriage certificate, court order or amended birth certificate. citeturn1search12

Address changes

Pennsylvania provides a non-commercial driver's-license change-of-address service with no PennDOT fee. Photo ID address changes are also listed as no-fee transactions. citeturn1search1

Pennsylvania IDs for non-U.S. citizens

PennDOT maintains separate documentation rules for non-U.S. citizens. Applicants must establish identity, Pennsylvania residency and lawful status using the documents applicable to their immigration category. citeturn1search2

REAL ID

Non-U.S. citizens applying for REAL ID may need additional immigration documents. PennDOT's REAL ID checklist includes qualifying permanent-resident, employment-authorization and foreign-passport documentation. citeturn1search10

Social Security number

A person who does not have a Social Security number may need an original SSA ineligibility letter when applying for REAL ID if their visa status makes them ineligible for an SSN. citeturn1search10

Pennsylvania ID law FAQs

Is REAL ID mandatory in Pennsylvania?

No. REAL ID is optional. A standard Pennsylvania license or ID remains available, but another federally accepted document is needed for covered federal purposes when a REAL ID is not used.

How many residency documents do I need for Pennsylvania REAL ID?

You need two documents proving your Pennsylvania residency.

Do I need two proofs of residency for a Pennsylvania driver's license?

Adults generally need two proofs of Pennsylvania residency. PennDOT's first-license guidance says applicants age 16 or 17 do not need the two residency proofs at that stage.

Does Pennsylvania require photo ID to vote?

Not for every voter. ID is required when voting for the first time in a particular election district or polling place. Approved photo and non-photo forms are available.

What if I do not have an acceptable ID when voting?

If identification is required and you cannot provide it, you may be issued a provisional ballot. Election officials determine whether the ballot can be counted.

How much does a Pennsylvania photo ID cost?

The current initial photo ID fee is $43.50. Renewal is $44.50 and replacement is $42.50.

How much does Pennsylvania REAL ID cost?

The first REAL ID has a one-time $30 fee plus the applicable renewal fee. Future driver's-license renewals do not include another REAL ID fee.

Can non-U.S. citizens get a Pennsylvania ID?

Yes, qualifying applicants can obtain Pennsylvania credentials by providing the identity, residency, Social Security and lawful-status documentation required for their circumstances.

Wyoming ID Laws: What You Need to Know

Wyoming ID laws cover driver's licenses, state identification cards, REAL ID, proof of identity and residency, voter identification, renewal, replacement and fees. This guide explains the main requirements in plain language and links to official Wyoming sources.

Wyoming Driver ServicesWYDOT Driver Services issues Wyoming driver's licenses, permits and identification cards.

Wyoming ID requirements at a glance

Identity

Applicants generally provide one acceptable proof of identity and one proof of legal presence using original or certified documents.

Residency

Two documents showing your name and current physical Wyoming residential address are generally required.

Social Security

Wyoming requires your SSN on the application. Proof of the number is recommended but not required in every case.

REAL ID

Wyoming says all driver's licenses and ID cards issued by Driver Services are REAL ID compliant, with a star in the upper-right corner.

Important: Wyoming moved to statewide REAL ID compliance years ago. Current credentials issued by WYDOT meet federal REAL ID standards, although some older pre-2010 cards may still be in circulation.

Wyoming driver's license requirements

Wyoming issues Class C driver's licenses for ordinary non-commercial driving, along with commercial licenses, learner permits, motorcycle privileges and restricted credentials.

New Wyoming residents

Wyoming gives new residents one year from the time they establish residency to obtain a Wyoming driver's license. You must apply in person at a Wyoming driver exam station.

If your current license is from Georgia, Massachusetts, Michigan, Tennessee or Wisconsin, or if you hold a commercial driver's license, Wyoming says you must apply for a Wyoming license once you establish residency rather than using the general one-year window.

Applying for a first Wyoming license

First-time applicants appear in person, provide the required documents, complete a vision screening and complete the applicable written and driving tests. Applicants transferring an out-of-state license generally surrender that credential when the Wyoming license is issued.

Young drivers

Wyoming uses graduated licensing for younger drivers. Learner permits and intermediate licenses are part of the process before full driving privileges, with age and supervised-driving requirements applying to younger applicants.

Wyoming state ID card requirements

Wyoming identification cards may be issued to residents of any age. Adult cards are horizontal while cards for people under 21 are vertical.

A Wyoming ID card costs $10 and is generally issued with an eight-year expiration period. A person cannot hold both a Wyoming driver's license and a Wyoming ID card at the same time.

For a new ID card, applicants provide the same core identity, legal-presence, residency and Social Security documentation required under Wyoming's document rules. Temporary-status applicants can receive an ID whose expiration matches the applicable immigration document.

Proof of identity, legal presence and residency

Proof of legal presence

U.S. citizens can generally use one state-certified birth certificate, valid unexpired U.S. passport or passport card, Consular Report of Birth Abroad, Certificate of Citizenship or Certificate of Naturalization.

Wyoming requires original or certified documents. Photocopies, notarized copies and online printouts are not accepted for these core documents.

Proof of identity

One proof of identity can include a U.S. state or territory driver's license, state ID card, certified birth certificate, valid U.S. passport or other government-issued identification.

Proof of Wyoming residency

Two proofs of residency are required. Each document must contain your name and current physical Wyoming residential address. A P.O. Box, private mailbox or mail-drop address cannot be used as the physical residence.

Wyoming's current document guide says residency documents generally must be no more than 45 days old and cannot come from the same entity. Examples include a utility bill, lease, mortgage document, insurance record, bank or credit-card statement, Wyoming voter registration, Wyoming vehicle registration, government mail, paycheck, tax document, school record and Wyoming residency affidavit.

Social Security number

Wyoming requires your Social Security number for the application. A Social Security card, W-2 or government document showing the full number can be used as proof, although WYDOT says proof of the number is recommended rather than required in every application.

Name changes

If your current name differs from your identity or legal-presence documents, you must provide the legal document connecting the names. Marriage certificates, divorce decrees and court orders are common examples.

Wyoming REAL ID requirements

Wyoming says all driver's licenses and identification cards issued by Driver Services are REAL ID compliant. Current credentials have a star in the upper-right corner. Wyoming changed from a gold star to a black star in 2019 while retaining REAL ID compliance.

Because Wyoming credentials are already REAL ID compliant, residents generally do not need to choose between a separate standard and REAL ID credential when applying for a current license or ID.

A REAL ID-compliant Wyoming credential can be used for federal purposes covered by the REAL ID rules, including domestic commercial air travel. A U.S. passport or another TSA-accepted document can also be used.

Older cards: WYDOT says some ID cards issued before January 1, 2010 may still be in circulation. People holding those older cards are encouraged to renew at a Driver Services office to ensure REAL ID compliance.

Wyoming voter ID requirements

Wyoming voters voting in person must show an acceptable form of identification. The Wyoming Secretary of State lists a Wyoming driver's license or ID, tribal ID, valid U.S. passport, U.S. military card, out-of-state driver's license or ID, qualifying Wyoming student IDs, Medicare or Medicaid cards and a Wyoming concealed firearm permit among the accepted forms.

Wyoming voters who do not have one of the listed documents can obtain a free Wyoming identification card from a local Driver Services office. Voters who vote by mail do not need to provide an additional form of identification with the absentee ballot.

Voter registration identification

Wyoming's current voter-registration rules also require proof of U.S. citizenship and Wyoming residency. Citizenship can be shown with documents such as a valid Wyoming license or ID that does not indicate non-citizenship, a U.S. passport, certificate of citizenship or naturalization, certain tribal IDs, military records or a certified U.S. birth certificate.

Residency can be shown with a Wyoming license or ID, passport, qualifying government or school documents, utility or bank records, a lease, property tax record, Wyoming vehicle registration or other approved documents. The Secretary of State also provides an attestation process when an eligible voter does not have the specified residency documents.

Current election rules matter. Wyoming's voter-registration requirements were changed by 2025 legislation, including citizenship and residency documentation. Check the Wyoming Secretary of State for the rules applicable to the election or registration transaction.

Renewing a Wyoming license or ID

Wyoming allows eligible customers to renew in person, through the oneWYO online portal or by mail. State law allows renewal by mail or online once every 10 years for eligible driver's license holders.

Every 10 years, a driver must appear in person for renewal, have a new photo taken and complete a vision screening. Customers who need to update documents or have temporary immigration status may have additional in-person requirements.

WYDOT says current REAL ID documentation may need to be presented during an in-person renewal if the required documents were not previously provided.

Lost, stolen or damaged Wyoming ID

If you lose a Wyoming driver's license or identification card, you can obtain a duplicate at a driver exam office. Eligible customers can also use oneWYO or mail services depending on their previous renewal history.

The current duplicate driver's license or CDL fee is $25. A duplicate motorcycle credential added to a license is $5.

Name and address changes

Wyoming law requires driver's license and ID holders to notify WYDOT within 10 days after changing their mailing address, residence or name.

Address information can be updated online, by mail or through WYDOT's available service options. If you want a new credential showing the new address, you must follow the credential replacement or renewal process and pay the standard applicable fee.

Name changes must be completed in person with legal documentation. Wyoming also requires the new name to be updated with the Social Security Administration before issuing a revised credential.

Wyoming ID requirements for non-U.S. citizens

People who were not born in the United States must provide the applicable immigration and identity documents when applying for a Wyoming driver's license or ID.

Depending on status, Wyoming lists documents such as a Permanent Resident Card, Employment Authorization Card, valid passport with visa and I-94 or other USCIS documentation verifying lawful entry and status.

Wyoming verifies lawful status with USCIS. Temporary or conditional residents can receive credentials whose expiration dates correspond with their immigration documents.

Fake IDs and fraudulent identification in Wyoming

A Wyoming driver's license or identification card is an official government credential. Counterfeit, altered or improperly used identification can create legal consequences separate from ordinary licensing requirements.

For general educational background on counterfeit identification and the distinction between a fake ID and identity theft, see Fake ID vs. Identity Theft: Where the Legal Line Is from FakeIDs.com.

This site does not provide instructions for creating, altering or using fraudulent identification. For a specific legal issue, consult a qualified Wyoming attorney or the relevant government agency.

Wyoming ID law FAQs

How long do new residents have to get a Wyoming driver's license?

Wyoming gives new residents one year from the time they establish residency to obtain a Wyoming driver's license, although certain license holders, including CDL holders and people transferring from specified states, have different requirements.

Is every Wyoming driver's license REAL ID compliant?

WYDOT says current Wyoming driver's licenses and ID cards are REAL ID compliant. Current credentials have a star in the upper-right corner.

How many proof-of-residency documents does Wyoming require?

Two current documents are generally required. They must show your name and current physical Wyoming address and generally must be no more than 45 days old.

Can I get a Wyoming ID card without having a driver's license?

Yes. Wyoming ID cards are available to residents of any age. However, you cannot hold a Wyoming driver's license and Wyoming ID card at the same time.

Does Wyoming require photo ID to vote?

Yes. Wyoming voters voting in person must show an acceptable form of identification. Several documents qualify, and free Wyoming IDs are available for voters who need one.

Can I vote by mail without providing ID?

Wyoming's Secretary of State says voters who mail an absentee ballot are not required to provide an additional form of identification with the ballot.

How often do I need to renew my Wyoming license in person?

Wyoming requires an in-person driver's license renewal once every 10 years. An updated photo and vision screening are required at that in-person renewal.

How quickly must I report a Wyoming address change?

Wyoming law requires license and ID holders to notify WYDOT within 10 days of changing their mailing address, residence or name.

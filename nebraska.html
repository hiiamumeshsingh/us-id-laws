Nebraska ID Laws: What You Need to Know

Nebraska issues driver's licenses, permits and state identification cards through the Department of Motor Vehicles. This guide explains Nebraska ID requirements, REAL ID, proof of residency, voter identification, renewal, replacement, fees and rules for non-U.S. citizens.

Last reviewed: September 26, 2026

Nebraska DMV uses a document-verification system for identity, citizenship or lawful status, Social Security information and address. Applicants should bring original or certified documents that meet the DMV's current requirements. See Nebraska DMV document verification requirements.

Nebraska driver's license requirements

Nebraska's standard passenger driver's license is the Class O Operator's License. Applicants must satisfy identity, citizenship or lawful-status and residency requirements and complete the testing or licensing steps that apply to their age and circumstances.

New applicants

New Nebraska applicants must provide proof of U.S. citizenship or lawful status, identity, principal Nebraska address and their Social Security number. Nebraska requires proof of citizenship one time for new applicants. Once citizenship has been established, it does not generally have to be presented again on later visits, although lawful-status applicants must continue to provide applicable USCIS documentation. citeturn0search2turn2search6

New Nebraska residents

A new resident with a valid out-of-state license must obtain a Nebraska license within 30 days. The applicant must surrender the out-of-state credential and provide the required Nebraska documents. When a valid out-of-state license is surrendered, Nebraska may waive some testing requirements, although a vision test is required and a drive test can be administered at the discretion of licensing staff. citeturn0search1turn2search1

Teen drivers

Nebraska uses a graduated licensing system that includes learner permits and the Provisional Operator's Permit (POP). An applicant generally needs to hold the POP for at least 12 months and meet the applicable driving-record requirements before obtaining a Class O license at age 17. citeturn2search6

Nebraska State ID cards

Any Nebraska resident can apply for a State Identification Card. There is no minimum or maximum age requirement. A person cannot hold a valid Nebraska driver's license and a valid Nebraska State ID at the same time. citeturn0search0turn2search7

Documents for a State ID

Applicants generally provide proof of U.S. citizenship or lawful status and identity, two documents showing their Nebraska principal address and their Social Security number. A qualifying foreign national who is exempt from the SSN requirement can provide the documentation Nebraska DMV specifies for that exemption. citeturn0search0turn0search2

State ID for voting

Nebraska State ID cards are free for U.S. citizens. Voters who need an ID for voting can use the free State ID process through the DMV. If a qualifying voter needs a Nebraska birth certificate to obtain the ID, state law also provides a no-fee process for a certified Nebraska birth record. citeturn0search0turn0search3

Documents needed for a Nebraska ID

Nebraska's document-verification requirements are organized around citizenship or lawful status and identity, residential address and Social Security information. Documents generally must be original or certified copies and meet the DMV's validity requirements. citeturn0search5

Citizenship, lawful status and identity

Accepted documents include a certified U.S. birth certificate, valid U.S. passport or passport card, Certificate of Naturalization, Certificate of Citizenship and qualifying immigration documents. Foreign documents must have a certified English translation attached when required by the DMV. citeturn0search5

Social Security number

Nebraska requires applicants to disclose their valid Social Security number so it can be verified through the Social Security Administration. Certain applicants who are ineligible for an SSN can provide the documentation Nebraska accepts as proof of exemption. citeturn0search0turn0search2

Name changes

If the applicant's current legal name differs from the name on the citizenship, lawful-status or identity document, Nebraska requires documents connecting the former and current names. Examples include a certified marriage certificate, certified divorce decree, court order, amended birth certificate or qualifying naturalization or adoption documents. citeturn0search5

Proof of Nebraska residency

Nebraska generally requires two documents showing the applicant's first and last name and current Nebraska residential address. The documents must come from two separate issuing sources. A P.O. Box by itself is not accepted as proof of principal residential address. citeturn0search5

Common accepted documents

  • Bank or financial institution statement issued within the last 90 days
  • Pay stub or earnings statement issued within the last 90 days
  • Recent federal, state or local government document
  • Valid Nebraska vehicle registration
  • Valid insurance policy or insurance card
  • Mortgage, lease or rental agreement
  • Nebraska voter registration card
  • Real estate tax statement or receipt
  • Nebraska DMV renewal notice
  • Qualifying mail, correspondence, receipt or invoice showing the address

For applicants under 21, Nebraska can accept a parent or guardian's proof of address under the DMV's applicable rules. citeturn0search5

Nebraska REAL ID requirements

Nebraska REAL ID is optional for state purposes. A compliant driver's license or State ID has a star in the upper-right corner. Nebraska's credential design changed in March 2026: new REAL ID credentials use a white star bordered by a black circle, while older credentials issued before March 2026 can remain valid through December 2031. citeturn0search1

What you need for REAL ID

  • Proof of U.S. citizenship or qualifying lawful status and identity
  • Complete Social Security number or qualifying exemption documentation
  • Two Nebraska residential-address documents from separate sources
  • Legal name-change documentation when applicable

Nebraska's approved REAL ID document checklist says documents must be unaltered certified originals or certified copies, valid and unexpired. Photocopies are not accepted. Foreign documents must have a certified English translation attached when required. citeturn0search5

REAL ID is not the same as voter ID. Nebraska's election law separately establishes which photo IDs can be used to vote. A document can satisfy an election requirement without being a Nebraska REAL ID credential.

Nebraska voter identification requirements

Nebraska's voter ID requirement took effect with the May 14, 2024 statewide primary. Registered voters generally must present an acceptable photo ID before casting a ballot in primary, general and special elections. citeturn0search3

Accepted photo IDs

Nebraska's Secretary of State lists the following as acceptable examples:

  • Nebraska driver's license
  • Nebraska State ID
  • Nebraska college or university ID
  • Nebraska political-subdivision ID
  • U.S. passport
  • Military ID
  • Tribal ID
  • Qualifying care-facility ID containing the voter's name and photo

The photo ID can be expired. Nebraska does not accept documents such as a birth certificate, Social Security card, utility bill or bank statement as the photo ID itself. citeturn0search3

Free voter ID

Voters who do not have an acceptable photo ID can obtain a free Nebraska State ID through the DMV. Nebraska also provides a reasonable-impediment process for voters who cannot obtain an acceptable photo ID because of circumstances that make obtaining the required documentation difficult. citeturn0search3

Provisional ballots

A voter who cannot provide an acceptable photo ID may be able to vote provisionally. Nebraska's rules include a reasonable-impediment certification process, and the Secretary of State explains the documentation and curing procedures that apply to provisional ballots. citeturn0search3

Renewing a Nebraska driver's license or ID

Nebraska sends renewal notices before a driver's license or State ID expires. People age 21 and older can generally apply for renewal 90 days before their birthday. A license issued to someone under 21 expires on the person's 21st birthday. citeturn2search0

Online renewal

Eligible Nebraska residents can renew certain credentials online. The DMV also provides in-person renewal. Individuals who are outside Nebraska during the renewal period may be able to renew a standard operator's license by mail under the DMV's out-of-state renewal rules. citeturn2search0

Renewal documents

In-person renewal generally requires one form of identification showing name and date of birth, two address documents when the address has not already been verified or has changed, the applicable fee and any required vision or medical documentation. A new photo is taken at each DMV office visit. citeturn2search0

Replacing a lost, stolen or damaged Nebraska ID

Nebraska allows eligible residents to replace a driver's license, permit or State ID online or at a Driver Licensing Office. A replacement applicant must provide identification showing name and date of birth and, when required, two proofs of address. The replacement credential makes the previously issued credential invalid. citeturn2search2

After an in-person replacement, Nebraska issues a temporary receipt. The permanent license, permit or State ID is mailed after processing. citeturn2search2

Name and address changes

Nebraska requires a resident to update a principal address within 60 days of the change. Two forms of proof of the new principal address are required. Eligible Class O and Class M license holders and State ID holders can update an address online and request a replacement credential when the applicable conditions are met. citeturn0search4

Legal name changes

Name changes require documentation connecting the former and current legal names. Nebraska accepts qualifying marriage certificates, divorce decrees, court orders, amended birth certificates and other documents listed in its verification checklist. citeturn0search5

Nebraska IDs for non-U.S. citizens

Nebraska issues driver's licenses and State IDs to eligible non-U.S. citizens who establish lawful status, identity, Social Security information or an applicable exemption and Nebraska residency. USCIS documents are verified before a license or State ID is issued. citeturn0search2

USCIS verification

Nebraska warns that USCIS verification can take up to 20 federal work days. Applicants presenting USCIS documents should bring those documents whenever the DMV requires them for a transaction. citeturn0search2

Limited-term credentials

A Nebraska credential issued to a person with lawful status is generally valid only during the person's authorized stay. If the authorized stay has no definite end date, Nebraska's driver-licensing manual states that the credential can be limited to a one-year period. citeturn2search6

Counterfeit, altered and borrowed identification

A Nebraska driver's license or State ID is a government-issued credential connected to identity and address information verified by the DMV. A counterfeit, altered or borrowed credential does not become valid identification for the person presenting it.

Nebraska's Secretary of State also addresses suspected fake photo IDs in its voter-ID guidance. When a poll worker believes an ID may be fake or the photo does not resemble the voter, the voter may be directed to the provisional-ballot process and the election office can apply its verification and curing procedures. citeturn0search3

For broader educational information about fake-ID laws and how legal consequences vary by state, see Fake ID vs. Identity Theft: Where the Legal Line Is. The article discusses the distinction between fictitious identification and use of another person's real identifying information.

Nebraska ID law FAQs

Does Nebraska require REAL ID?

No. Nebraska offers both REAL ID-compliant and non-REAL-ID credentials. The REAL ID indicator is a star in the upper-right corner.

How many proof-of-address documents does Nebraska require?

Generally, two documents showing the applicant's name and Nebraska principal residential address are required. They must come from separate sources.

Can I get a Nebraska State ID without a driver's license?

Yes. Any Nebraska resident may obtain a State ID. A person cannot hold a valid Nebraska driver's license and valid Nebraska State ID at the same time.

Is a Nebraska State ID free?

State ID cards are free for U.S. citizens. Non-U.S. citizen ID fees depend on the validity period of the credential.

Does Nebraska require photo ID to vote?

Yes. Nebraska requires registered voters to present an acceptable photo ID for elections, subject to limited exceptions and the state's reasonable-impediment and provisional-ballot procedures.

What IDs can I use to vote in Nebraska?

Examples include a Nebraska driver's license, Nebraska State ID, Nebraska college ID, U.S. passport, military ID, Tribal ID and certain Nebraska political-subdivision or care-facility IDs.

How long do new Nebraska residents have to get a license?

A new resident with a valid out-of-state license must obtain a Nebraska license within 30 days.

Can non-U.S. citizens get a Nebraska driver's license?

Eligible non-U.S. citizens can receive Nebraska credentials after establishing lawful status, identity, Social Security information or an applicable exemption and Nebraska residency. USCIS documents are verified.

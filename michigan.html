Michigan ID Laws: What You Need to Know

Michigan issues driver's licenses and state identification cards through the Secretary of State. This guide explains Michigan ID requirements, REAL ID, Enhanced credentials, residency, voter identification, renewal, replacement, fees and requirements for non-U.S. citizens.

Last reviewed: September 26, 2026

Michigan driver's license requirements

Michigan requires original driver's license applicants to prove identity, Social Security number, U.S. citizenship or legal presence and Michigan residency. At least two documents establishing Michigan residency are required. Photocopies of original identity documents are not accepted. citeturn0search1

Standard driver's license

A standard Michigan driver's license costs $25 for a first-time license, $18 for a renewal and $9 for a replacement or correction. A $7 late fee can apply to a renewal completed after expiration. citeturn0search0

New residents

Michigan requires a person applying for an original Michigan license or ID to establish Michigan residency. An out-of-state credential is surrendered as part of the Michigan issuance process when applicable.

Vision and in-person visits

Michigan drivers complete a vision exam when they visit a Secretary of State office for the periodic in-person renewal requirement. Online and other renewal options are available between required office visits when the applicant is eligible. citeturn0search0

Michigan State ID without a driver's license

Michigan issues a Personal Identification Card to residents who need an official photo ID but do not hold a driver's license. A standard state ID costs $10 for first issuance, renewal or replacement. citeturn0search0

Michigan also offers an Enhanced ID. The standard Enhanced ID fee is $30 for first issuance, renewal or replacement, while people age 65 or older or who are legally blind can receive an Enhanced ID for $20. citeturn0search0

No-fee Michigan ID

Michigan law provides a no-fee ID for qualifying residents, including people age 65 or older, people who are legally blind, people experiencing homelessness, U.S. military veterans, people receiving state aid and people with a documented physical or mental disability that affects their ability to safely operate a vehicle. Eligibility must be established under the Secretary of State's rules. citeturn0search0

Documents needed for a Michigan ID

Michigan's Secretary of State requires original applicants to prove identity, Social Security number, U.S. citizenship or legal presence and Michigan residency. Documents are subject to Department of State approval and some transactions may require an additional visit if a document cannot be verified immediately. citeturn0search1

Identity

Acceptable identity documents are listed on Michigan's Identification Requirements form. The Secretary of State does not accept photocopies of original identity documents for this purpose. citeturn0search1

Social Security number

Applicants must provide proof of a valid Social Security number. If an applicant is ineligible for an SSN, Michigan requires a letter from the Social Security Administration documenting the reason. citeturn0search1

Citizenship or legal presence

U.S. citizens provide proof of citizenship. Applicants who are not U.S. citizens must provide documentation establishing lawful presence in the United States. citeturn0search1

Proof of Michigan residency

Michigan requires at least two documents establishing Michigan residency for an original driver's license or state ID. The Secretary of State reviews the documents for compliance with its residency requirements. citeturn0search1

Examples of residency evidence

Michigan's residency list can include documents such as a utility bill, bank statement, insurance policy, mortgage or lease document, government correspondence or other qualifying records showing the applicant's Michigan residential address.

A P.O. Box is not a substitute for the physical Michigan residence required by the Secretary of State. Applicants should use the current SOS-428 residency list before an appointment because the accepted-document list controls the transaction.

Michigan REAL ID requirements

Michigan issues REAL ID-compliant driver's licenses and Personal Identification Cards. A first-time REAL ID requires an office visit and additional documentation. A person converting a standard Michigan license or ID to REAL ID must provide the current credential and proof of legal presence. citeturn0search0

REAL ID is not the same as Enhanced ID. Michigan offers both. REAL ID is the federal standard for covered federal purposes. Enhanced credentials are a separate Michigan credential with additional border-travel functionality.

Federal uses

Since May 7, 2025, a REAL ID-compliant document or another federally accepted identity document is required for covered federal purposes such as boarding domestic commercial aircraft, entering certain federal buildings and accessing military installations. Michigan confirms this in its current license and ID guidance. citeturn0search0

Converting a Michigan license or ID to REAL ID

Eligible residents can convert a standard credential to REAL ID at a Secretary of State office. Michigan requires the current credential plus proof of legal presence and the additional documents required for REAL ID compliance. citeturn0search0

Michigan Enhanced ID and Enhanced Driver's License

Michigan's Enhanced Driver's License and Enhanced Identification Card are different from REAL ID. Enhanced credentials can be used for certain land and sea travel to Canada, Mexico and some Caribbean countries without a passport in qualifying circumstances. They are not a substitute for a passport in every international travel situation.

The current Michigan fee schedule lists $45 for a first Enhanced Driver's License, $38 for renewal and $24 for replacement or correction. Enhanced ID costs $30 for most applicants and $20 for applicants age 65 or older or who are legally blind. citeturn0search0

Michigan voter identification requirements

Michigan voters generally present photo identification at the polls. The Michigan Secretary of State lists acceptable forms of photo ID including a Michigan driver's license or ID, driver's license or ID from another state, federal or state government-issued identification, U.S. passport, military identification, tribal identification, student identification and other qualifying photo credentials. citeturn1search2

If you do not have photo ID

A voter who does not have acceptable photo identification can still vote. The voter signs an affidavit stating they are not in possession of photo ID and then votes a regular ballot. Michigan's Secretary of State states that voters should not be turned away solely because they do not have photo ID. citeturn1search2

Voter registration

Michigan allows eligible residents to register to vote online, by mail, at a Secretary of State office and through other authorized registration locations. A Michigan driver's license or state ID number can be used as identifying information during registration.

Renewing a Michigan ID or driver's license

Michigan driver's licenses and state IDs expire on the holder's birthday every four years. Eligible credentials can be renewed online, by mail or at a self-service station. Most residents only need an in-person Secretary of State visit every 12 years. citeturn0search0

Driver's license renewal

The standard Michigan driver's license renewal fee is $18. The standard Enhanced Driver's License renewal fee is $38. A late fee of $7 can apply to a standard driver's license renewal. citeturn0search0

ID renewal

A standard state ID renewal costs $10. Enhanced ID renewal costs $30 for most applicants or $20 for qualifying residents age 65 or older or who are legally blind. citeturn0search0

Replacing a lost, stolen or damaged Michigan ID

Michigan offers replacement and correction services for driver's licenses and state IDs. A standard driver's license replacement costs $9, while a standard state ID replacement costs $10. Enhanced driver's license replacement costs $24 and Enhanced ID replacement costs $30 for most applicants. citeturn0search0

If the replacement involves a name change, the Secretary of State requires the current Michigan credential and a legal name-change document, such as a marriage license, divorce decree or court-ordered name change issued in the United States. citeturn0search0

Name and address changes

Michigan allows an address update online, by mail or while completing another license or ID transaction at a self-service station. A corrected address sticker is mailed to the customer for a standard license or ID. An Enhanced credential is replaced with a new card. Michigan voter registration updates automatically when the address is updated with the Secretary of State. citeturn0search0

Name changes must be completed at a Secretary of State office. The applicant must bring the current Michigan license or ID and a legal name-change document. Michigan also says the name should be corrected with the Social Security Administration before the Secretary of State transaction. citeturn0search0

Michigan IDs for non-U.S. citizens

Michigan issues driver's licenses and identification cards to eligible residents who can establish lawful presence in the United States and satisfy the state's identity, Social Security and residency requirements. citeturn0search1

Applicants who are ineligible for a Social Security number must provide a Social Security Administration letter documenting the reason for ineligibility. citeturn0search1

REAL ID for non-citizens

REAL ID applicants who are not U.S. citizens must provide documentation establishing lawful presence. The Secretary of State reviews the immigration or other legal-presence documents before issuing the compliant credential.

Counterfeit, altered and borrowed identification

A Michigan driver's license or state ID is issued by the Secretary of State to a specific person after identity and eligibility information are verified. A counterfeit credential, altered genuine credential or another person's genuine license does not become a valid Michigan ID for the person presenting it.

For general educational background on fake IDs and the difference between counterfeit, altered and genuine credentials, see What Is a Fake ID? Definition, Types and Legal Facts. Michigan-specific identification requirements should be checked against the Secretary of State and Michigan election sources.

Michigan ID law FAQs

Can I get a Michigan ID without a driver's license?

Yes. Michigan issues Personal Identification Cards to residents who need official identification but do not hold a driver's license.

How many residency documents do I need in Michigan?

Michigan requires at least two documents establishing Michigan residency for an original driver's license or state ID.

Is Michigan Enhanced ID the same as REAL ID?

No. They are separate credentials. REAL ID satisfies the federal identity standard for covered federal purposes. Enhanced credentials have additional travel functionality for certain international land and sea crossings.

Do I need a REAL ID to drive in Michigan?

No. A standard Michigan driver's license remains valid for driving. REAL ID affects covered federal purposes.

Does Michigan require photo ID to vote?

Michigan generally asks voters to show photo ID at the polls. A voter without acceptable photo ID can sign an affidavit and vote a regular ballot.

How much does a Michigan state ID cost?

A standard Michigan state ID costs $10. Some residents qualify for a no-fee ID under Michigan law.

Can non-U.S. citizens get a Michigan ID?

Eligible non-U.S. citizens can receive Michigan credentials when they establish lawful presence and satisfy the identity, Social Security and residency requirements.

How long is a Michigan driver's license valid?

A Michigan driver's license expires on the holder's birthday every four years.

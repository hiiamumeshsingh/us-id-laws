Hawaii ID Laws: What You Need to Know

Hawaii's county driver licensing offices issue driver's licenses, permits and Hawaii State Identification Cards. This guide explains the documents, Hawaii residence requirements, REAL ID rules, voter identification, renewal, replacement, fees and requirements for people with temporary or permanent immigration status.

Last reviewed: September 26, 2026

Hawaii's system is administered locally through the four county DMV offices. Some procedures and appointments therefore vary by county even though the underlying state document requirements are statewide.

Hawaii driver's license requirements

Hawaii's REAL ID-compliant driver's license process requires documentary proof of legal name, date of birth, Social Security number, legal presence and Hawaii principal residence address.

Anyone applying for a Hawaii driver's license or instruction permit for the first time must apply in person and provide the required original or certified documents. A photograph is taken at the DMV.

Moving to Hawaii

The same documentary-proof rules apply to people moving to Hawaii from another state or country when they apply for a REAL ID-compliant Hawaii credential. Applicants should bring the required source documents rather than assuming an out-of-state license alone will satisfy every requirement.

Provisional licenses

Hawaii has an instruction-permit and provisional-license process for younger drivers. The age, supervised-driving and testing requirements are separate from the identity-document requirements described on this page.

Hawaii State ID without a driver's license

Hawaii issues State Identification Cards separately from driver's licenses. The State ID can be used as a government-issued photo credential, and a REAL ID-compliant card can be used for federal purposes covered by REAL ID.

For a first-time REAL ID State Identification Card, applicants must apply in person and provide proof of legal name, date of birth, Social Security number, legal presence and Hawaii principal residence address. A photograph is taken during the application.

Hawaii allows only one REAL ID-compliant credential to be held at a time. If you have a REAL ID State ID and later choose your driver's license as the REAL ID credential, you must surrender the REAL ID State ID.

Documents needed for a Hawaii ID

Hawaii's Department of Transportation publishes an acceptable-document list for REAL ID credentials. Documents must generally be valid originals or certified copies. Notarized copies and faxes are not accepted as substitutes for certified copies.

Legal name and date of birth

Documents can include a certified U.S. birth certificate, valid U.S. passport or passport card, Consular Report of Birth Abroad, certificate of naturalization or qualifying immigration documents.

If the names on your documents do not match, Hawaii requires connecting documents that establish the legal name change or the connection between the names.

Social Security number

The DMV requires documentary proof of the Social Security number for a REAL ID credential. The acceptable-document list identifies the documents that can be used for this purpose.

Legal presence

U.S. citizens establish legal presence with qualifying citizenship documents. Non-U.S. citizens must provide immigration documents that establish their lawful status and, when applicable, the authorized period of stay.

Proof of Hawaii residency

Hawaii requires proof of a Hawaii principal residence address for a REAL ID driver's license, permit or State ID.

The acceptable-document list includes different types of records that can establish a Hawaii residential address. Applicants should use the current county DMV checklist because the acceptable combinations can depend on the applicant's circumstances.

The address on the documents must establish the applicant's principal Hawaii residence. A mailing address alone is not necessarily the same as the residence requirement.

Hawaii REAL ID requirements

Hawaii has been REAL ID compliant since 2013. A REAL ID-compliant Hawaii driver's license, permit or State ID is marked with the approved star and can be used for federal purposes covered by REAL ID rules.

For a first REAL ID credential, the applicant must appear in person and provide documentation covering five categories:

  • Legal name
  • Date of birth
  • Social Security number
  • Legal presence
  • Hawaii principal residence address

Renewal after REAL ID verification

U.S. citizens and permanent residents who have already submitted and had their REAL ID documents verified generally do not need to submit the same documents again at renewal unless the information has changed. A new photograph is taken in person at renewal no less frequently than every 16 years.

People with temporary legal presence have different renewal rules and must present proof of continued legal presence in person.

Travel

A REAL ID-compliant Hawaii credential can be used for TSA identity requirements for domestic air travel. A U.S. passport or another TSA-accepted document can also be used.

Hawaii voter identification rules

Hawaii conducts elections primarily by mail. Registered voters receive ballots at the mailing address associated with their voter registration, and ballots are verified through the voter's signature on the return envelope.

Hawaii voter registration can be completed online using a Hawaii driver's license or State ID and Social Security number. A person who does not have either credential can use the paper voter-registration process through the county elections division.

For a person registering to vote in Hawaii for the first time by mail who cannot provide a Hawaii driver's license, Hawaii State ID or Social Security number on the application, additional proof of identification is required. Hawaii lists a current and valid photo ID, utility bill, bank statement, government check, paycheck or another government document showing the applicant's name and address.

At voter service centers, voters can vote in person, register on the same day and update voter registration. A Hawaii driver's license or State ID can still be used to verify identity even if it contains an old address; the address is not used to establish the voting residence by itself.

Renewing a Hawaii ID

Most Hawaii State ID renewals are handled in person at the county DMV. Hawaii's statewide guidance says applicants with temporary legal presence must renew in person and provide documentary proof of continued legal presence.

U.S. citizens and permanent residents who previously completed the REAL ID document-verification process generally do not have to bring the same source documents again unless information has changed. A new photograph is required in person at every other renewal.

Hawaii provides alternative renewal arrangements for some people age 80 or older and for people whose severe physical or intellectual disabilities make an in-person application a serious burden. County DMV offices provide the specific instructions.

Replacing a lost, stolen or damaged Hawaii ID

Hawaii allows U.S. citizens and permanent residents with REAL ID credentials to request a duplicate without resubmitting the complete documentary proof, provided the personal information on the card has not changed.

A person with temporary legal presence must present proof of legal presence in person when requesting a duplicate.

If your name or other personal information changes at the same time as the replacement, Hawaii requires documentary proof of the change. A change to the Hawaii principal residence address should also be reported.

Name and address changes

Hawaii requires connecting documents when the legal name on the credential needs to change. The DMV's acceptable-document list explains which records can establish the link between the old and new names.

A change to the Hawaii principal residence address should be submitted to the county DMV. If other personal information printed on the credential changes, supporting documents must generally be presented in person.

Keeping the address current also matters for voter registration because Hawaii's election system uses the residence information associated with the voter record to determine the appropriate voting district.

Hawaii IDs for non-U.S. citizens

Hawaii issues credentials to non-U.S. citizens who can establish the required identity, Social Security information when applicable, legal presence and Hawaii residence.

Permanent residents

Permanent residents who have completed the REAL ID document-verification process generally do not need to resubmit the same documents at each renewal unless information has changed.

Temporary legal presence

People temporarily authorized to remain in the United States receive limited-term credentials. They must present documentation establishing continued legal presence when renewing or requesting a duplicate.

Temporary-status applicants must renew in person at the county DMV. The expiration period of the credential can be tied to the period of authorized stay.

Hawaii ID fees

Hawaii's identification-card fees are administered through the county DMV offices. The exact amount can depend on the county, transaction and applicant's circumstances.

Hawaii also allows the State ID fee to be waived for qualifying people who are homeless or experiencing documented extreme hardship. The statewide DOT guidance says the waiver can apply to an original or renewal Hawaii State Identification Card when the required documentation is provided.

Because the four counties administer the transactions locally, check your county DMV's current fee schedule before visiting.

Counterfeit, altered and borrowed identification

A Hawaii driver's license or State ID is issued after the DMV verifies source documents and identity information. A counterfeit card, altered credential or another person's genuine card does not carry the same status as a credential issued to the person presenting it.

Hawaii ID law FAQs

Can I get a Hawaii State ID without a driver's license?

Yes. Hawaii issues State Identification Cards separately from driver's licenses.

What documents do I need for a Hawaii REAL ID?

You must establish legal name, date of birth, Social Security number, legal presence and Hawaii principal residence address with acceptable original or certified documents.

Can I have both a REAL ID driver's license and a REAL ID State ID?

No. Hawaii says you can hold only one REAL ID-compliant credential at a time. If you choose the driver's license as your REAL ID credential, the REAL ID State ID must be surrendered.

Does Hawaii require ID to vote?

Hawaii's election system is primarily vote-by-mail. Identification requirements mainly arise during voter registration and certain in-person registration or voting situations rather than through a general polling-place photo-ID requirement.

Can non-U.S. citizens get a Hawaii ID?

Yes. Eligible non-U.S. citizens can receive Hawaii credentials when they establish the required identity, Social Security information when applicable, legal presence and Hawaii residence.

Can the Hawaii State ID fee be waived?

Yes. Hawaii provides a fee waiver for qualifying applicants who can document homelessness or extreme hardship. The statewide guidance says the waiver applies to an original or renewal State ID.

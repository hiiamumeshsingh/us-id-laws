Tennessee ID Laws: What You Need to Know

Tennessee has separate requirements for driver's licenses, ID-only licenses and REAL ID credentials. The state also requires government-issued photo identification for in-person voting. This guide explains Tennessee's identity, Social Security and residency requirements, REAL ID, voter ID, renewal, replacement, fees and non-U.S. citizen rules.

Last reviewed: September 26, 2026

Tennessee driver's license requirements

Applicants for a Tennessee driver's license must provide proof of U.S. citizenship or legal presence, Social Security information and two proofs of Tennessee residency. The documents must be originals or certified copies, and the DMV may verify them with the issuing agency. citeturn0search4turn0search6

Standard Class D license

The standard Class D operator license is generally valid for eight years for people age 21 and older. The current fee table lists $26 for the eight-year license plus a $2 application fee, for a $28 state total. County Clerk partner locations charge an additional $4 administrative fee for services they provide. citeturn0search2

Social Security number

For a non-REAL ID license, an applicant may verbally provide the Social Security number and Tennessee can verify it electronically. If verification fails, additional documentation may be required. REAL ID applicants must provide approved documentation showing the full SSN. citeturn0search1turn0search8

Testing

New drivers must complete the testing applicable to their license class and age. New residents whose out-of-state license has been expired for more than six months must take the Tennessee vision screening, knowledge test and road skills test. citeturn0search4

Teen and graduated licensing

Tennessee has separate teen-driver requirements for applicants under 18, including a graduated licensing process and additional parent or guardian documentation. A minor applying for an ID-only license also needs the required parent or legal-guardian portion of the minor affidavit form. citeturn0search1turn0search4

Tennessee ID-only license requirements

Tennessee offers two types of identification-only licenses: an Expiring Identification License and a Permanent Identification License. Neither is valid for operating a motor vehicle, and no driving test is required to obtain an ID-only license. citeturn0search1

Who can get an ID-only license?

  • A person who needs official identification but does not hold a valid driver's license
  • Children, including infants, who meet the documentation requirements
  • Applicants who provide proof of identity, legal presence and Tennessee residency

Expiration

An Expiring Identification License is issued for a defined period. Temporary ID credentials can expire at the end of the individual's authorized stay. Permanent Identification Licenses are available to qualifying applicants under Tennessee's rules. citeturn0search1

ID-only fees

The current fee table lists an ID-only credential for applicants under 18 at $3 for two years plus a $2 application fee, for a $5 state total. For applicants age 18 and older, the ID-only license cost is $10 for eight years plus a $2 application fee, for a $12 state total. citeturn0search2

Documents needed for a Tennessee ID

Tennessee's document rules are built around three categories: proof of U.S. citizenship or legal presence, proof of Social Security number and two proofs of Tennessee residency. The required documents vary slightly depending on whether the credential is standard or REAL ID and whether the applicant is a U.S. citizen, permanent resident or temporarily present in the country. citeturn0search1turn0search8

Citizenship or legal presence

Examples include a qualifying U.S. birth certificate, valid U.S. passport, Consular Report of Birth Abroad, Certificate of Citizenship, Certificate of Naturalization, Permanent Resident Card, Employment Authorization Document or qualifying foreign passport with a valid U.S. visa and I-94. citeturn0search4turn0search8

Social Security documentation

For REAL ID, acceptable SSN documents include a Social Security card, W-2, 1099 or payroll check stub showing the full number. A sworn affidavit may be used in the limited situation where an applicant has never been issued an SSN. citeturn0search8

Name changes

If the name on the citizenship or legal-presence document differs from the current legal name, Tennessee requires documentation connecting the names. Examples include a marriage certificate, divorce decree or court order. Multiple documents may be needed when there have been several name changes. citeturn0search4turn0search8

Proof of Tennessee residency

Tennessee requires two proofs of residency. Both documents must show the applicant's name and current residential address, or in qualifying cases the name of a spouse, parent or legal guardian together with proof of the relationship. The two documents must come from different sources. citeturn0search6

Current residency documents

  • Utility bill
  • Bank statement
  • Current rental or mortgage contract
  • Employer verification or qualifying employer letter
  • Paycheck, work ID or badge showing the residential address
  • W-2
  • Insurance policy
  • Tennessee vehicle registration or title, using one rather than both
  • Tennessee voter registration card
  • Property-tax receipt
  • Current Tennessee driver's license or ID held by a qualifying parent, guardian or spouse

How recent must the documents be?

Unless an exception is specified, Tennessee considers a residency document current when it was issued within the previous four months. IRS documents must be from the current tax year. Original documents are required and photocopies are not accepted. citeturn0search6

Lease documents

A signed lease can count as one proof when it meets Tennessee's requirements and is accompanied by a qualifying landlord letter when required. A second residency document from an approved, different source is still required. citeturn0search6

Homeless or shelter residents

Tennessee provides a specific residency affidavit for homeless or shelter residents. The affidavit can serve as the secondary proof of residency showing the applicant's principal Tennessee residence. citeturn0search1turn0search6

Tennessee REAL ID requirements

REAL ID is optional in Tennessee. It is not required for ordinary driving, voting, buying age-restricted products or applying for federal benefits. A REAL ID or passport is required for covered federal uses such as boarding domestic commercial flights and entering certain federal facilities. citeturn0search0

REAL ID checklist

  • One proof of U.S. citizenship or legal presence
  • One approved document showing the full Social Security number
  • Two proofs of Tennessee residency
  • Certified legal name-change documents when applicable

All REAL ID documents must be originals or certified copies. Photocopies are not accepted. citeturn0search8

REAL ID application

Federal law requires a REAL ID application to be made in person. Tennessee allows applicants to pre-approve documents online before visiting a Driver Services Center, which can help confirm that the required documents are ready. citeturn0search0turn0search7

REAL ID cost

When a REAL ID is obtained during a normal renewal, Tennessee says the cost is the same as the standard driver's-license renewal. If a REAL ID is obtained outside the renewal period, an applicable duplicate fee is charged. citeturn0search7

REAL ID for non-U.S. citizens

Qualifying non-U.S. citizens can apply for a REAL ID by providing the lawful-presence documents that apply to their immigration status, together with the required SSN and residency documentation. citeturn0search4turn0search8

New Tennessee residents

New or returning Tennessee residents who hold a driver's license from another state must obtain a Tennessee driver's license within 30 days after establishing residency. They must visit a full-service Driver Services Center and surrender the out-of-state license or provide the required driving record. citeturn0search4

Out-of-state license

Tennessee law does not allow a resident to hold more than one valid driver's license or ID. Foreign-country licenses and IDs may be retained under the state's rules. citeturn0search4

New residents from another country

New residents transferring from another country must take the Tennessee vision screening, knowledge test and road skills test. Tennessee allows them to keep the license issued by their foreign country. citeturn0search4

Tennessee voter identification requirements

Tennessee requires voters who vote in person during early voting or on Election Day to present a photo ID issued by the federal or Tennessee state government. The state's current voter information says qualifying IDs may be current or expired. citeturn0search5

Accepted photo ID

  • Tennessee driver's license or ID card with a photo
  • U.S. passport
  • U.S. military photo ID
  • Photo ID issued by the Tennessee Department of Safety
  • Other qualifying federal or Tennessee government photo identification listed by the Secretary of State

The Tennessee Secretary of State maintains the current accepted-ID list and election-specific instructions. citeturn0search5

Non-photo driver's licenses

Tennessee's DMV states that non-photo driver's licenses are not accepted for voting identification under current law. citeturn0search2

Absentee and mail voting

Absentee voting uses separate application and identification rules. Voters should use the Tennessee Secretary of State's current absentee-voting instructions rather than applying the in-person photo-ID rule automatically to every absentee ballot situation.

First-time voter registration

Voter registration is handled separately from the DMV credential process. Tennessee's Secretary of State provides the current registration and identification requirements through GoVoteTN.

Renewing a Tennessee driver's license or ID

A Tennessee driver's license or ID issued to a person age 21 or older is valid for eight years from the date of issuance. The Department of Safety sends a courtesy renewal notice approximately 8 to 10 weeks before expiration, but a notice is not required to renew. citeturn0search2

Online renewal

Eligible customers can renew online. A new photo is required in person every other renewal cycle, which can make a customer ineligible for online renewal for that cycle. If an online renewal is allowed, Tennessee uses the existing photo for the updated credential. citeturn0search2

REAL ID renewal

When renewing, eligible customers can upgrade to REAL ID by completing the required in-person document review. Tennessee states that a REAL ID obtained during renewal costs the same as the standard license renewal. citeturn0search0turn0search7

Commercial driver's licenses

Class A, B and C commercial driver's licenses must be renewed in person at a Driver Services Center. CDL holders must also provide current medical certification when required. citeturn0search2

Replacing a lost, stolen or damaged Tennessee ID

Tennessee provides duplicate credential services for driver's licenses and ID-only licenses. The current fee schedule lists a first duplicate at $8 total for a standard Class D license and a second or subsequent duplicate at $12 total. citeturn0search2

Replacement ID-only license

ID-only licenses have separate fees based on age. An ID-only credential for someone age 18 or older has a $12 state total when first issued, while duplicate transactions use the applicable duplicate fee. citeturn0search2

Lost or stolen credentials

If a credential is lost or stolen and personal information may have been exposed, consider reporting the loss or theft and monitoring accounts for identity misuse. Tennessee's duplicate-credential process is the official method for replacing the state document.

Tennessee name and address changes

Name changes

Tennessee requires documentation connecting the old and new legal names. Depending on the situation, this can include a marriage certificate, divorce decree or court order. Multiple documents may be required when the name changed more than once. citeturn0search4turn0search8

Address changes

Tennessee's residency rules require current documents showing the residential address. The Department of Safety's current services should be checked for the available online or in-person address-change option and any credential-reissue requirement. citeturn0search6

Tennessee IDs for non-U.S. citizens

Qualifying non-U.S. citizens can obtain Tennessee driver's licenses and ID-only licenses by providing proof of lawful presence, Social Security information and Tennessee residency. Temporary credentials can be tied to the person's authorized period of stay. citeturn0search1turn0search4

Permanent residents

Permanent residents can use qualifying permanent-resident documentation such as an I-551 Permanent Resident Card to establish lawful presence. citeturn0search4turn0search8

Temporary lawful presence

Examples of accepted temporary-status documentation include a valid foreign passport with a valid U.S. visa and I-94. F-1 and F-2 students may also need a valid SEVIS I-20, while J-1 and J-2 applicants may need a DS-2019. citeturn0search1

Credential expiration

Temporary ID credentials can expire at the end of the individual's authorized stay. Applicants should check the expiration date and renewal requirements tied to their immigration status. citeturn0search1

Tennessee ID law FAQs

Is REAL ID mandatory in Tennessee?

No. REAL ID is optional. It is required only for covered federal identification purposes when another federally accepted document, such as a passport, is not being used.

How many proof-of-residency documents do I need in Tennessee?

You generally need two current documents showing your Tennessee residential address. The two documents must come from different sources.

How long is a Tennessee driver's license valid?

A license issued to someone age 21 or older is generally valid for eight years. Special age-based rules apply to younger drivers.

Does Tennessee require photo ID to vote?

Yes. In-person voters must present photo identification issued by the federal or Tennessee state government. The state says qualifying IDs may be current or expired.

Can I get a Tennessee ID without a driver's license?

Yes. Tennessee offers ID-only licenses that are not valid for driving. There are Expiring and Permanent Identification License types.

Can children get a Tennessee ID?

Yes. Tennessee says families can obtain ID-only licenses for children, including infants, as long as the required documentation is provided.

How long do new residents have to get a Tennessee license?

New or returning residents with another state's license must obtain a Tennessee license within 30 days after establishing residency.

Can non-U.S. citizens get a Tennessee driver's license?

Yes, qualifying non-U.S. citizens can apply by providing the lawful-presence, Social Security and residency documents required for their status.

How much is a Tennessee driver's license?

The standard eight-year Class D license has a $28 state total before any applicable County Clerk administrative fee.

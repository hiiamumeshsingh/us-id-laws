South Carolina ID Laws: What You Need to Know

South Carolina has separate documentation requirements for standard credentials and REAL ID. The state also requires photo identification for in-person voting. This guide covers driver's licenses, state ID cards, proof of residency, REAL ID, voting, renewals, replacements, fees and non-U.S. citizen requirements.

Last reviewed: September 26, 2026

South Carolina driver's license requirements

South Carolina driver's-license applicants must complete the applicable licensing steps and provide documentation establishing identity, date and place of birth, Social Security information and South Carolina residency. Driver applicants also provide the name of their automobile liability insurance company licensed to do business in South Carolina. citeturn1search3

Standard driver's license

Most South Carolina driver's licenses are valid for eight years and cost $25. The SCDMV checks an applicant's driving history in other states and cancels prior driver's licenses or ID cards from another state or province when a South Carolina credential is issued. citeturn4search4

Social Security number

The SCDMV electronically verifies the applicant's Social Security number with the Social Security Administration. A person who has been denied an SSN can provide an SSA denial letter when permitted by the DMV's documentation rules. citeturn1search3

Automobile insurance

Driver's-license applicants must provide the name and information of an automobile liability insurance company licensed to do business in South Carolina. This insurance information is not required for an identification card or beginner's permit. citeturn1search3

Testing

Depending on the credential and applicant's circumstances, the licensing process can include knowledge, skills and vision testing. The SCDMV's current renewal guidance requires a vision screening when renewing a driver's license in person. citeturn4search5

South Carolina state ID requirements

South Carolina residents can obtain a non-driver identification card if they are at least five years old, live in South Carolina and do not have a valid South Carolina driver's license or beginner's permit. A person with an SC ID does not have driving privileges. citeturn4search1

State ID eligibility

  • At least 5 years old
  • South Carolina resident
  • No valid South Carolina driver's license or beginner's permit
  • Provide acceptable identity documentation

When an SC ID is issued, driver's licenses and ID cards previously issued by another jurisdiction must be surrendered and are cancelled under the DMV's rules. citeturn4search1

ID costs

Original ID cards and renewals are free for people age 17 and older. ID cards for ages 5 through 16 cost $15. A replacement ID costs $10 for all ages, except when an eligible adult is within one year of expiration and the transaction can be processed as a free renewal. citeturn4search1turn4search2

Renewing an ID

An SC ID expires eight years after issuance. Eligible U.S. citizens can renew online when the ID is within 12 months of expiration and has not been expired for more than nine months. Non-U.S. citizens cannot renew an ID online under the current DMV rules. citeturn4search1

Documents needed for a South Carolina ID

South Carolina uses separate checklists for U.S. citizens and international customers. Documents are subject to verification and documents that are not in English must be translated using the DMV's approved translation process. citeturn1search2turn1search3

Identity, date and place of birth

South Carolina accepts specific original documents to establish identity, date and place of birth. The current DMV checklist includes documents such as a U.S. birth certificate, valid U.S. passport and qualifying citizenship or immigration documents. citeturn1search2

Social Security

The applicant's name, date of birth and SSN must match the Social Security Administration's records. If an applicant has been denied an SSN, the SCDMV can accept the applicable SSA letter under its checklist. citeturn1search3

Name changes

If the applicant's legal name has changed, supporting documentation is required. The SCDMV states that a U.S. passport or passport card showing the applicant's legal name may be used in place of certain name-change documents. citeturn4search4

Proof of South Carolina residency

The address requirements depend on whether you choose a standard credential or REAL ID. A standard South Carolina beginner's permit, driver's license or ID requires one proof of South Carolina address. A REAL ID requires two proofs, with each document coming from a different organization and both showing the same South Carolina residential address. A P.O. Box cannot be used as the residential address. citeturn1search2turn1search4

Examples of address documents

  • Utility bill
  • Bank statement
  • Insurance document
  • Lease or mortgage document
  • Government or tax document
  • Other documents listed on the current SCDMV checklist

The exact acceptable-document categories and age requirements can vary by transaction, so applicants should use the current MV-93 or MV-94 checklist before visiting the DMV. citeturn1search2turn1search3

South Carolina REAL ID requirements

REAL ID is optional in South Carolina. A REAL ID has a gold star and can be used for federally regulated purposes such as boarding commercial aircraft and entering secure federal facilities. A standard South Carolina credential remains valid for driving, voting and other state purposes. citeturn1search2

REAL ID checklist

  • Proof of South Carolina address
  • Proof of U.S. citizenship or qualifying lawful status and identity, date and place of birth
  • Social Security documentation
  • Legal name-change documents when applicable
  • Two address proofs from different organizations for a REAL ID credential

The current U.S. citizen checklist says two address proofs are required for a REAL ID beginner's permit, driver's license or ID card. A standard card requires one address proof. citeturn1search2

REAL ID cost

South Carolina's REAL ID license costs the same as the standard eight-year license: $25. The SCDMV's REAL ID FAQ also explains that the first REAL ID cannot be obtained through the $10 duplicate/replacement transaction. citeturn4search4turn2search2

Federal uses

The DMV states that a REAL ID is needed for covered federal identification uses, while a standard card marked "Not for Federal Identification" requires another federally accepted document for those purposes. A standard credential can still be used to drive and vote. citeturn1search2

Central issuance and temporary certificates

South Carolina now uses a central issuance process in which newly issued licenses and ID cards are produced centrally and mailed to customers. The SCDMV says cards are generally processed and delivered within 7 to 15 business days. Customers leave the branch with a temporary driving certificate or temporary identification certificate while waiting. citeturn1search5

New South Carolina residents

When moving to South Carolina, applicants transferring a license from another state must surrender the prior state credential. The SCDMV checks driving history and requires the applicable South Carolina identity, SSN and address documentation. citeturn4search4turn1search3

Out-of-state credentials

An out-of-state driver's license or ID may be used as part of the transfer process, but the South Carolina credential replaces the prior state credential. If the previous credential was lost or stolen, the SCDMV can require a certified driving record from the issuing state. citeturn1search3

South Carolina voter identification requirements

South Carolina requires voters voting in person to show a current and valid photo ID. Accepted forms include an SC driver's license, SC DMV ID card, SC voter registration card with photo, federal military ID, U.S. passport and certain other state-issued photo credentials. citeturn0search0turn0search2

Accepted photo IDs

  • South Carolina driver's license
  • South Carolina DMV ID card
  • South Carolina voter registration card with photo
  • Federal military ID
  • U.S. passport or passport card
  • South Carolina concealed weapons permit
  • Temporary identification certificate, where accepted under current election rules

The South Carolina Election Commission maintains the current list of acceptable voting IDs. citeturn0search0turn0search2

Free voter photo ID

Registered voters who do not have an accepted photo ID can obtain a free photo ID from their county voter registration and elections office or a local DMV office. The state says registered voters can provide their date of birth and the last four digits of their Social Security number at the county office and have their photo taken. citeturn0search0

If you forget your photo ID

A voter who forgets to bring photo ID can vote a provisional ballot. The ballot will count if the voter provides the required photo ID to the county voter registration and elections office before the applicable election certification deadline. citeturn0search0

Reasonable impediment

A voter who cannot obtain photo ID for a reasonable impediment can present a current non-photo voter registration card, sign the required affidavit and cast a provisional ballot. The Election Commission lists examples such as disability or illness, lack of transportation, work conflicts, family responsibilities, lack of a birth certificate and religious objection to being photographed. citeturn0search0

Registering to vote

South Carolina voter registration can be completed online, by mail or in person. Online registration requires an SC driver's license or DMV ID, and the voter registration deadline is generally 30 days before an election. citeturn0search1

Renewing a South Carolina driver's license

South Carolina's standard eight-year driver's-license renewal costs $25. Eligible drivers can renew online, by mail or in person depending on their circumstances. In-person renewal requires payment of the license fee, the applicable insurance information and a vision screening. citeturn4search5

Renewing online

Eligible customers can renew online and receive an eight-year license by mail. The SCDMV uses the photograph and signature already on file. citeturn4search5

Expired licenses

If a South Carolina driver's license has been expired for less than nine months, it can generally be renewed as an ordinary renewal. If it has been expired for nine months or more, the applicant must visit an SCDMV branch and pass the required vision, knowledge and skills tests. citeturn4search5

REAL ID renewal

Customers whose required REAL ID documents are already on file may renew by mail and retain the REAL ID designation. If the documents are not on file, a customer may receive a standard credential unless the required documentation is provided. citeturn4search5

Replacing a lost, stolen or damaged South Carolina ID

A replacement South Carolina driver's license or ID costs $10. The SCDMV allows replacement transactions online for eligible customers and also offers branch service. citeturn4search2

Multiple replacements

If more than one replacement card is requested within a 12-month period, the SCDMV may require additional documentation such as a police report. citeturn4search2

Important REAL ID point

If you have not previously purchased a South Carolina REAL ID, using the $10 replacement transaction does not upgrade the credential to REAL ID. The first REAL ID requires the appropriate transaction and documentation. citeturn2search2

South Carolina name and address changes

Name changes

The SCDMV requires supporting documentation for legal name changes. A U.S. passport or passport card showing the legal name may be used in certain circumstances instead of separate name-change documents. citeturn4search4

Address changes

The residence address on a South Carolina credential must be a current physical South Carolina residence address and cannot be a P.O. Box. The DMV application also allows a separate mailing or temporary address when applicable. citeturn1search4

South Carolina IDs for non-U.S. citizens

South Carolina issues driver's licenses and identification cards to qualifying non-U.S. citizens who meet the state's documentation requirements. International customers use a separate SCDMV checklist from U.S. citizens. citeturn1search3

Lawful presence

The SCDMV verifies lawful presence for qualifying non-U.S. citizens through the federal SAVE system. The applicant must provide the immigration documents applicable to their status. citeturn1search7

Credential expiration

Under current South Carolina law, credentials issued to people who are not lawful permanent residents can have an expiration tied to the person's authorized stay or employment authorization, subject to statutory limits. Certain people pending adjustment of status may receive a one-year extension when they present the required documentation. citeturn4search3

Commercial licenses

The SCDMV's international-customer checklist states that commercial learner's permits and commercial driver's licenses are issued only to U.S. citizens or lawful permanent residents. citeturn1search3

Counterfeit, altered and fraudulent identification

South Carolina's DMV documentation process is designed to verify identity, Social Security information, residence and lawful status where applicable. The state also introduced a more secure centrally issued credential system designed to strengthen the physical card and reduce counterfeiting risk. citeturn1search5

South Carolina ID law FAQs

Is REAL ID mandatory in South Carolina?

No. REAL ID is optional. A standard credential remains valid for driving and voting, but another federally accepted document may be needed for covered federal identification purposes.

How many proof-of-address documents do I need for a South Carolina REAL ID?

You need two proofs of South Carolina address from different organizations, and both must show the same residential address.

How much does a South Carolina driver's license cost?

An eight-year South Carolina driver's license costs $25.

How much does a South Carolina ID card cost?

An original ID and renewal are free for people age 17 and older. An ID for ages 5 through 16 costs $15. A replacement ID costs $10.

Does South Carolina require photo ID to vote?

Yes. In-person voters are asked to show a current and valid photo ID. The state also provides provisional-ballot and reasonable-impediment procedures.

Can I get a free voter ID in South Carolina?

Yes. Eligible voters without an accepted photo ID can obtain a free photo ID from their county voter registration and elections office or a local DMV office.

What happens if I forget my photo ID when voting?

You can cast a provisional ballot. The Election Commission says the ballot can count if the required photo ID is provided to the county election office before certification.

Can non-U.S. citizens get a South Carolina ID?

Yes, qualifying non-U.S. citizens can obtain credentials by providing the identity, Social Security, South Carolina address and lawful-status documents required for their circumstances.

How long is a South Carolina driver's license valid?

Most South Carolina driver's licenses are valid for eight years.

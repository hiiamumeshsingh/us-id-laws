Idaho ID Laws: What You Need to Know

Idaho's Transportation Department handles driver's licenses and identification cards through county driver licensing offices. This guide explains Idaho license and ID requirements, Star Card documents, residency, voter identification, renewal, replacement, fees and rules for lawful presence.

Last reviewed: September 26, 2026

Idaho driver's license requirements

Idaho requires a valid driver's license for anyone who operates a motor vehicle on public roads in the state. The Idaho Transportation Department administers driver's licenses through local driver licensing offices.

For a new Idaho credential, the DMV requires proof of U.S. citizenship or lawful presence, identity, Idaho residency and name when applicable. Applicants must also provide their Social Security number for electronic verification.

New residents

If you move to Idaho, you generally need to apply for an Idaho driver's license or identification card within 30 days of becoming an Idaho resident. Idaho identifies exceptions for certain students attending an Idaho college or university and active-duty military members and their dependents who maintain residency elsewhere and hold a valid out-of-state license.

2026 proof-of-identity update

Effective July 1, 2026, Idaho DMV customers establishing a DMV profile must present valid government-issued photo identification showing their full legal name, date of birth and photograph. Idaho lists acceptable examples including Idaho and out-of-state driver's licenses and ID cards, U.S. and foreign passports, U.S. Department of Homeland Security documents, military identification, Idaho concealed weapons permits and certain Native American, corrections and Mexican Consular identification documents.

Idaho State ID without a driver's license

Idaho identification cards are available to Idaho residents of all ages. The DMV describes the card as a way to show proof of age and identity without serving as a driver's license.

Applicants must provide the required proof of U.S. citizenship or lawful presence, identity, Idaho residency and name when applicable. A person does not need to hold an Idaho driver's license to obtain a standard Idaho ID card.

Free ID for voting

Idaho provides a no-charge identification card for eligible voters who need one for voting. To qualify, the applicant must be at least 18, state that the card is needed for voting and provide proof of U.S. citizenship or have citizenship already documented in the DMV record.

The voting ID is issued for four years. Eligible holders can receive one free replacement and free renewals while they continue to qualify. If an applicant already has a driver's license, Idaho requires the license to be surrendered when applying for the free voting ID.

Documents needed for an Idaho ID

Idaho's DMV separates the document requirements into citizenship or lawful presence, identity, residency, name and Social Security information.

Citizenship or lawful presence

New applicants must provide proof of U.S. citizenship or lawful presence. Idaho accepts documents such as a certified U.S. birth certificate, valid U.S. passport, certificate of naturalization or citizenship, permanent resident card, employment authorization document and qualifying immigration records.

Photocopies and laminated documents are not accepted for the citizenship or lawful-presence documents listed by Idaho DMV. People who are not lawfully present in the United States are not eligible for an Idaho driver's license or identification card.

Proof of identity

Idaho uses photo identity documents to verify identity and age. Examples include an Idaho or out-of-state driver's license or ID card, U.S. passport, qualifying DHS document, military ID, valid foreign passport and certain tribal or school identification documents.

Idaho says photo identity documents may be accepted up to five years after expiration, subject to DMV review and the specific document type.

Social Security number

The applicant's Social Security number is electronically verified with the Social Security Administration and is not printed on the Idaho license or ID. Acceptable documentation can include a Social Security card, W-2, qualifying 1099 or pay statement showing the current legal name and SSN.

If a person has not been assigned an SSN, Idaho requires written documentation from the Social Security Administration confirming that fact. A new confirmation letter is required at each subsequent renewal.

Proof of Idaho residency

Applicants must reside in Idaho to receive an Idaho driver's license or ID. Residency documents must show the applicant's name and principal Idaho residence address and generally must be dated within the last 12 months.

Examples include a signed lease, mortgage or deed, public-utility account statement, bank statement, W-2 or 1099, medical or insurance statement, pay stub or employment verification, school records, qualifying lodging receipt, military Leave and Earnings Statement, shelter certification, residency affidavit, court document, property-tax record or Idaho-based insurance document.

Two proofs for the Star Card

To obtain an Idaho Star Card, applicants must provide two documents from different sources showing their name and principal Idaho residence address. The documents must generally be dated within one year and use the applicant's current legal name.

A P.O. Box does not satisfy the physical-residence requirement for the Star Card.

Idaho Star Card and REAL ID requirements

The Star Card is Idaho's REAL ID-compliant driver's license or identification card. It is optional. A regular Idaho license or ID without the Star remains valid for ordinary state purposes.

Since May 7, 2025, a Star Card or another federally accepted identification document, such as a U.S. passport, is required for commercial air travel and certain federal facilities covered by REAL ID rules.

What you need for a Star Card

  • One acceptable document proving identity, date of birth and lawful status or presence
  • Proof of your Social Security number for electronic verification
  • Two Idaho residency documents from different sources
  • Name-change documents when the current legal name differs from the identity documents

Idaho's DMV provides an Add the Star Tool that creates a personalized document checklist. The exact combination depends on the documents and status of the applicant.

Renewing a Star Card

All applicants can renew a Star Card in person. U.S. citizens and lawful permanent residents may also be eligible for online renewal. Non-U.S. citizens must renew in person to provide documented proof of continued lawful presence.

Idaho voter identification requirements

Idaho requires registered voters to present an approved form of photo identification when voting in person. Current approved forms include an Idaho driver's license or identification card, U.S. passport or federal photo ID, tribal identification card and an Idaho county sheriff-issued concealed weapons license.

If a registered voter cannot present an approved photo ID, the voter can sign a Personal Identification Affidavit affirming their identity. Idaho's Secretary of State describes the affidavit as an alternative when the voter is unable to provide an acceptable photo ID.

Voter registration identification

Idaho also requires identification and proof of residence for voter registration. Approved photo identification includes an Idaho driver's license or ID, U.S. passport or federal photo ID, tribal ID or an Idaho county sheriff-issued concealed weapons license. Proof of residence can include an eligible photo ID with the correct address, insurance documentation, a lease, mortgage, utility bill, bank statement, paystub or other listed records.

Because voter identification rules are administered by the Idaho Secretary of State and county election offices, voters should check the current VoteIdaho.gov identification guidance before an election.

Renewing an Idaho driver's license or ID

An Idaho Class D driver's license can generally be issued for four or eight years depending on age and the option selected. Idaho says people between 21 and 62 can choose an eight-year license.

Eligible drivers can renew online through the Idaho DMV portal. Idaho allows driver's license renewal up to 25 months before the expiration date. Online renewal may qualify for a $5 discount on a driver's license renewal; the discount does not apply to identification cards.

Star Card renewals follow additional REAL ID rules. U.S. citizens and lawful permanent residents may qualify for online renewal, while non-U.S. citizens must renew in person to document continued lawful presence.

Replacing a lost, stolen or damaged Idaho ID

Idaho provides duplicate and replacement transactions through the DMV. The current fee for a duplicate identification card is the same as the original ID fee, while a duplicate driver's license or instruction permit is currently $20.

If the replacement involves a change in name or other identifying information, the DMV may require supporting documents. Applicants should also report an address change and keep their DMV record current.

People who qualify for Idaho's free voting ID program receive one free replacement while they remain eligible. People receiving an initial free ID through Idaho's homelessness program also receive one free replacement.

Name and address changes

If your legal name changes, Idaho requires documents linking the old and new names. Examples include a certified marriage certificate, certified divorce decree, adoption papers or court-ordered name-change documents.

Idaho advises applicants who are changing their name to update the name with the Social Security Administration before making the corresponding change at the DMV.

Address changes can be completed through Idaho's DMV online portal when the applicant is eligible, or through the paper change-of-address process. The DMV's residency rules are based on the applicant's principal physical Idaho residence address.

Idaho IDs for non-U.S. citizens

Idaho issues driver's licenses and identification cards to eligible non-U.S. citizens who can establish lawful presence and satisfy the other identity, residency and Social Security requirements.

Idaho's DMV checks lawful-presence documentation with USCIS. The acceptable documents can include a permanent resident card, employment authorization document, qualifying foreign passport and immigration records.

Temporary foreign nationals

For temporary foreign nationals who are not authorized for employment and do not have an SSN, Idaho says the DMV will verify the applicant's USCIS number. Temporary foreign nationals who are authorized for employment but do not have an SSN are required to obtain and present one.

A credential issued to a person with temporary lawful presence can have different renewal requirements. Non-U.S. citizens must renew a Star Card in person so the DMV can verify continued lawful presence.

Counterfeit, altered and borrowed identification

An Idaho driver's license or ID is issued after the DMV verifies identity, lawful status, residency and other required information. A counterfeit credential, an altered real credential or another person's genuine ID is not equivalent to a credential issued to the person presenting it.

For broader educational context on how fake identification laws differ from state to state, see Fake ID Laws & Legal Consequences. Idaho's own statutes and government guidance control the rules that apply in Idaho.

Idaho ID law FAQs

Can I get an Idaho ID without a driver's license?

Yes. Idaho issues identification cards separately from driver's licenses, and they are available to Idaho residents of all ages.

What is the Idaho Star Card?

The Star Card is Idaho's REAL ID-compliant driver's license or identification card. It is optional and is available through the same driver licensing offices that issue standard credentials.

How many residency documents do I need for a Star Card?

You need two documents from different sources showing your name and principal Idaho residence address. Idaho's accepted-document list gives the specific options.

Does Idaho require a photo ID to vote?

Yes. Registered voters are asked to show an approved photo ID. A voter who cannot present an approved form can sign a Personal Identification Affidavit.

Can I get a free Idaho ID for voting?

Yes. Eligible applicants who are at least 18, need the card for voting and can establish U.S. citizenship can receive a four-year Idaho ID at no charge.

Can non-U.S. citizens get an Idaho ID?

Yes, if they can establish lawful presence and satisfy Idaho's other credential requirements. People who are not lawfully present are not eligible for an Idaho driver's license or ID card.

How soon do new residents need an Idaho ID?

Idaho generally requires new residents to obtain an Idaho driver's license or ID card within 30 days of residing in the state, subject to listed exceptions.

West Virginia ID Laws: What You Need to Know

West Virginia ID laws cover driver's licenses, state identification cards, REAL ID, proof of identity and residency, voter identification, renewal, replacement and fees. This guide explains the main requirements in plain language and links to official West Virginia sources.

West Virginia DMVThe West Virginia Division of Motor Vehicles issues driver's licenses, permits and state identification cards.

West Virginia ID requirements at a glance

Identity

New applicants generally need one acceptable proof of identity. Original or certified documents are used for identity verification.

Social Security

Applicants generally provide one proof of their Social Security number. DMV checks the number against Social Security Administration records.

Residency

New applicants generally provide two proofs of West Virginia residency showing their name and physical address.

REAL ID

West Virginia offers standard "Not for Federal Identification" credentials and REAL ID "For Federal Identification" credentials with a gold star.

Important: West Virginia's REAL ID credential is optional for ordinary driving and state identification. It is required, or an alternative federally accepted document is required, for certain federal purposes such as domestic air travel.

West Virginia driver's license requirements

If you live in West Virginia and drive on public roads, you generally need a West Virginia driver's license unless a statutory exemption applies. West Virginia uses a graduated licensing system for younger drivers and regular Class E and F licenses for other drivers.

New West Virginia residents

New residents who want to drive in West Virginia must apply for a West Virginia driver's license within 30 days. If transferring a valid out-of-state license, DMV requires the current license and the documents applicable to the transaction.

If the previous license is expired or unavailable, DMV may require a certified driving record from the prior state. Applicants may also need vision, written and road testing depending on the circumstances.

First-time applicants

Applicants who have never held a driver's license or ID, applicants transferring from another state and people whose West Virginia license has been expired for more than three years are treated as first-time applicants for documentation purposes.

West Virginia state ID card requirements

West Virginia offers state identification cards for residents who need official identification but do not need a driver's license. A person may choose either a standard "Not for Federal Identification" ID or a REAL ID-compliant "For Federal Identification" card.

For a new ID card, DMV's appointment checklist calls for proof of identity, Social Security information, proof of legal name change when applicable and two proofs of West Virginia residency.

West Virginia also offers youth identification cards and other specialized credential options. DMV determines eligibility and required documents based on the applicant's circumstances.

Proof of identity, Social Security and residency

Proof of identity

West Virginia DMV accepts original or certified U.S.-issued birth certificates, valid unexpired U.S. passports and passport cards and qualifying immigration documents. Foreign-born applicants can use certain DHS documents, including a Permanent Resident Card, employment authorization document, Certificate of Naturalization or Certificate of Citizenship.

Social Security number

Acceptable proof can include a Social Security card, an original W-2 or payroll stub containing the required information or a 1099 with the applicant's full Social Security number. If a person is not eligible for an SSN, DMV requires a Social Security Administration denial letter.

Proof of West Virginia residency

Two proofs of West Virginia residency are generally required. Documents must show the applicant's name and physical West Virginia address. A P.O. Box is not acceptable as the residential address.

Examples include a utility bill, tax record, mortgage or homeowner insurance document, WV W-2, vehicle registration, voter registration card, lease, bank statement, auto insurance card, hunting license, professional license or other documents listed by DMV.

Recurring documents generally cannot be more than 60 days old, and DMV says applicants may not use more than one document issued by the same company.

Name changes

DMV requires documentation connecting a current legal name with the name shown on identity records. Examples include a certified marriage certificate, amended birth certificate, court order or certified divorce decree.

West Virginia REAL ID requirements

West Virginia offers a REAL ID-compliant driver's license or ID card marked with a gold star. The credential is commonly called a "For Federal Identification" card. A standard credential is marked "Not for Federal Identification."

New REAL ID applicants generally need one proof of identity, one proof of Social Security number, two proofs of West Virginia residency and name-change documents when applicable. The DMV also charges an additional $10 REAL ID fee.

Since May 7, 2025, travelers generally need a REAL ID-compliant credential or another TSA-accepted form of identification for domestic air travel. A U.S. passport is one alternative.

REAL ID is optional for ordinary state use. You can keep a standard West Virginia license or ID if you do not need a federally compliant credential, but you will need another federally accepted ID for purposes covered by the REAL ID rules.

Renewing a West Virginia license or ID

West Virginia driver's license renewal fees are based on the number of years remaining until the next applicable age milestone. The DMV's current schedule uses a rate of $5 per year for regular Class E licenses, while certain Class F credentials use a different rate.

Eligible customers can renew online through West Virginia DMV's online services. Customers renewing a REAL ID without changes can generally use the current license or ID card or the renewal application. Address changes require two proof-of-residency documents.

West Virginia uses central issuance. After a transaction, the permanent credential is mailed to the applicant rather than printed at the DMV office. A temporary credential or receipt is issued for immediate use where applicable.

Lost, stolen or damaged West Virginia ID

For a duplicate standard license or ID after loss, theft or destruction, West Virginia DMV generally requires one proof of identity and one proof of West Virginia residency. If the address has changed, two residency documents are required.

For a duplicate REAL ID credential, DMV requires the federal documentation specified for a "For Federal Identification" replacement, including identity, Social Security and residency documentation.

The current duplicate driver's license fee is $7.50. Other credential types may have different fees.

Name and address changes

If your address changes, West Virginia DMV requires documentation showing the new physical West Virginia residence. Two residency documents are required when the address on the credential changes.

A legal name change requires certified documentation connecting the old and new names. DMV may also require the applicant's Social Security record to reflect the current legal name before issuing a new credential.

West Virginia voter ID requirements

West Virginia voters voting in person during early voting or on Election Day must show an approved form of photo identification. The identification must generally be valid and not expired.

Accepted forms include a West Virginia driver's license or state ID, a driver's license from another state, a U.S. passport or passport card, qualifying government employee or student photo IDs, a U.S. military ID and a qualifying voter registration card with a photo.

West Virginia also provides exceptions. For example, a poll worker who has known the voter for at least six months can verify the voter's identity without requiring the normal ID process. A registered voter who cannot obtain an accepted ID can receive a free voter ID card from the county clerk.

Registered voters who do not have acceptable ID and do not qualify for an exception vote a provisional ballot. The Secretary of State says the ballot can be counted after the applicable verification process.

First-time voters

First-time voters who registered in West Virginia or a county and have not previously voted in a federal election in the state must provide identification. Acceptable first-time voter documents can include a valid photo ID, utility bill, bank statement, government check, paycheck or other official government document showing the current name and address.

Voter rules are election rules, not DMV rules. Check the West Virginia Secretary of State before an election for the current identification requirements and exceptions.

West Virginia ID requirements for non-U.S. citizens

West Virginia DMV has separate document requirements for immigrants, non-immigrants and refugees applying for a learner's permit, driver's license or identification card.

Immigrant applicants may need a Permanent Resident Card, qualifying I-551 documentation or another approved immigration document, along with a Social Security card and two proofs of West Virginia residency.

Non-immigrant applicants may need a valid foreign passport with English translation, Form I-94, a visa when applicable and original immigration documents showing status and authorized stay. Some F-1, F-2, J-1, J-2 and work-related applicants have additional verification requirements.

Refugees and asylees have separate documentation paths, including immigration records and proof of West Virginia residency.

Fake IDs and fraudulent identification in West Virginia

A West Virginia driver's license or ID card is an official government credential. Counterfeit, altered or improperly used identification can create legal consequences separate from normal licensing requirements.

For general educational background on counterfeit identification and the legal distinction between a fake ID and identity theft, see Fake ID vs. Identity Theft: Where the Legal Line Is from FakeIDs.com.

This site does not provide instructions for creating, altering or using fraudulent identification. For a specific legal issue, consult a qualified West Virginia attorney or the relevant government agency.

West Virginia ID law FAQs

How long do new residents have to get a West Virginia driver's license?

New residents who want to drive in West Virginia must apply for a West Virginia driver's license within 30 days.

How many proofs of residency does West Virginia require?

New applicants generally need two proofs of West Virginia residency showing their name and physical address. A P.O. Box is not accepted as the residential address.

Is REAL ID mandatory in West Virginia?

No. West Virginia offers both standard "Not for Federal Identification" credentials and REAL ID "For Federal Identification" credentials. A REAL ID or another federally accepted document is needed for certain federal purposes.

What does the gold star on a West Virginia license mean?

The gold star identifies a West Virginia REAL ID-compliant credential that meets federal REAL ID standards.

Does West Virginia require photo ID to vote?

Yes. In-person voters generally must show an approved, valid and unexpired photo ID during early voting or on Election Day, subject to statutory exceptions.

Can I get a free voter ID in West Virginia?

Yes. Registered voters who cannot obtain an accepted form of identification can receive a free voter ID card through their county clerk.

Can a non-U.S. citizen get a West Virginia driver's license?

West Virginia has separate DMV documentation procedures for immigrants, non-immigrants and refugees. The required documents depend on immigration status and may include immigration records, a Social Security card and two proofs of West Virginia residency.

What happens if I lose my West Virginia driver's license?

For a standard duplicate credential, DMV generally requires one proof of identity and one proof of West Virginia residency. Additional documents are required if your address or legal name has changed.

New Jersey ID Laws: What You Need to Know

New Jersey uses a document-based identity verification system for driver's licenses and non-driver IDs. This guide covers 6-point ID, REAL ID, residency, voter identification, renewal, replacement, fees and rules for non-U.S. citizens.

Last reviewed: September 26, 2026

New Jersey driver's license requirements

New Jersey driver's license applicants must establish identity, address and the required legal presence information. The MVC's 6-point system determines whether the identity documents are sufficient. Applicants also complete the licensing and testing steps that apply to their license type and age.

6-point identification system

The standard documentation package requires at least six points, including at least one primary document and at least one secondary document. Applicants cannot use more than two one-point documents. A verifiable Social Security number and proof of New Jersey address are also required. citeturn0search9

New residents

People establishing New Jersey residency must transfer their driver's license to New Jersey and surrender the out-of-state license. The MVC uses the same identity, address and legal-presence framework for the transaction.

Teen drivers

New Jersey's graduated driver licensing system includes a special learner's permit, examination permit and probationary license. Additional supervised-driving, passenger and night-driving restrictions apply to younger drivers.

New Jersey non-driver identification cards

New Jersey issues non-driver identification cards through MVC for residents who need official identification without a driver's license. Applicants must satisfy the MVC's identity verification, address and Social Security requirements.

6-point ID applies

The MVC's 6-point documentation system is used for state-issued identification. Applicants should bring original or certified documents and make sure names are consistent across records. citeturn0search0turn0search9

REAL ID option

A non-driver ID can be issued as a REAL ID-compliant credential when the applicant completes the applicable federal requirements. The applicant chooses whether to obtain a REAL ID or standard credential.

Documents needed for a New Jersey ID

New Jersey's 6-point system assigns different point values to identity documents. The applicant must present at least one primary document and at least one secondary document and reach six points total. citeturn0search9

Primary documents

Examples include a certified U.S. birth certificate, U.S. passport, current New Jersey digital driver's license or digital non-driver ID, military ID, Certificate of Naturalization and Certificate of Citizenship. Qualifying foreign passports and immigration records can satisfy the primary-document requirement for non-citizens. citeturn0search9

Secondary documents

Examples include a certified marriage or civil-union certificate, divorce decree, court order, military dependent or retiree ID, school records, government employee ID, Social Security card, bank statement and certain professional or benefit cards. No more than two one-point documents can be used. citeturn0search9

Social Security number

The MVC requires a verifiable Social Security number for standard driver's license and ID transactions. Certain non-citizens may have additional documentation requirements based on their status.

Proof of New Jersey residency

Applicants must provide proof of a New Jersey address. The MVC's documentation rules distinguish identity documents from address evidence, so applicants should bring separate acceptable proof of residence.

Examples of address evidence

  • Utility or phone bill
  • Bank or credit-card statement
  • Property tax statement or receipt
  • Lease or rental agreement
  • Government correspondence or official document
  • Employment record or other MVC-approved address document

The address must correspond to the applicant's current New Jersey residence. Applicants using a mailing address should review MVC rules to ensure the required physical or residential address is documented.

New Jersey REAL ID requirements

New Jersey allows applicants to choose between a standard credential and REAL ID. A REAL ID-compliant license or non-driver ID is marked with a star and can be used for federal purposes covered by the REAL ID Act, including domestic air travel. New Jersey's rules require proof of identity, date of birth, principal residence and authorized presence. citeturn0search7

2 + 1 + 6 structure

  • At least one primary identity document
  • At least one secondary document
  • Documents totaling at least six points
  • Proof of Social Security number
  • Proof of New Jersey principal residence
  • Proof of authorized presence when applicable

The MVC may require additional documents when names or other information conflict. Documents must generally be originals or certified copies. citeturn0search7turn0search9

REAL ID is optional for New Jersey state purposes. A standard New Jersey credential can still be used for ordinary driving and identification within the state. A passport or another TSA-accepted credential can be used instead of REAL ID for covered federal purposes.

New Jersey voter identification requirements

New Jersey voters are not routinely required to show ID before voting. The state's 2026 election guide says voters may nevertheless be asked to show identification in certain circumstances. citeturn0search8

When ID may be requested

Identification can be requested from certain first-time voters whose registration information could not be verified through the registration process. Acceptable documents can include a New Jersey driver's license, U.S. passport, military or government ID, student or employee ID and various official documents such as a bank statement, utility bill, government check, rent receipt or sample ballot. citeturn0search8

Registering to vote

To register, a person must be a U.S. citizen, at least 17 years old with voting eligibility beginning at 18, a New Jersey county resident for at least 30 days before the election and not serving a disqualifying sentence of incarceration. The registration deadline is 21 days before an election. citeturn0search2

Early voting and provisional ballots

New Jersey provides in-person early voting for primary and general elections. A provisional ballot is used when eligibility cannot be confirmed at the polls or when certain vote-by-mail circumstances apply. citeturn0search3

Renewing a New Jersey driver's license or ID

New Jersey offers online renewal for eligible driver's license holders and requires in-person service when the transaction cannot be completed online. REAL ID upgrades require an MVC visit with the required documents.

What to bring

Applicants who renew in person should bring the current credential and any documentation needed to resolve a name, address or legal-status change. The MVC may require 6-point documentation when identity cannot be verified electronically or when a transaction requires new documentation.

REAL ID renewal

If an applicant already has a New Jersey REAL ID, the credential remains REAL ID compliant when renewed, provided the MVC can verify the record. An applicant upgrading from a standard credential must complete the REAL ID document process.

Replacing a lost, stolen or damaged New Jersey ID

New Jersey provides duplicate driver's license and ID services through MVC. Eligible applicants can use online services for some duplicate transactions. In-person replacement may require identity verification and supporting documents.

If a credential was stolen, reporting the theft and requesting a replacement helps prevent continued use of the missing credential. A replacement credential supersedes the prior card.

Name and address changes

New Jersey requires legal documentation when the name on the credential needs to change. Common evidence includes a certified marriage or civil-union certificate, divorce decree or court order linking the former and current names. citeturn0search9

Address changes

Applicants should update the MVC when their address changes and obtain a replacement credential when they want the new address printed on the card. Address documentation must meet MVC requirements.

New Jersey IDs for non-U.S. citizens

Eligible non-U.S. citizens can obtain New Jersey driver's licenses and non-driver IDs after establishing identity, Social Security information when applicable, New Jersey address and authorized presence under MVC rules.

Immigration documents

Qualifying documents include foreign passports with appropriate USCIS verification and arrival records, permanent-resident cards, refugee or re-entry documents and employment authorization cards. The MVC can require additional federal documentation when needed to verify legal presence. citeturn0search9

Limited-duration credentials

When lawful presence is temporary, the credential's validity can be limited according to the applicant's authorized status. Applicants should use the MVC's current document list because acceptable immigration documents and verification procedures can change.

New Jersey ID law FAQs

What is New Jersey's 6-point ID system?

It is the MVC's identity-document system. Applicants generally need at least one primary document, one secondary document and enough points to total six, along with a verifiable Social Security number and proof of address.

Does New Jersey require REAL ID?

No. REAL ID is optional for state purposes. It is used for covered federal purposes such as domestic air travel.

How many points do I need for a New Jersey license?

You generally need six points, including at least one primary document and at least one secondary document. No more than two one-point documents can be used.

Does New Jersey require photo ID to vote?

Not routinely. Most New Jersey voters do not show ID before voting, although certain first-time voters may be asked for identification.

Can non-U.S. citizens get a New Jersey driver's license?

Eligible non-U.S. citizens can obtain New Jersey credentials after meeting MVC identity, address, Social Security and authorized-presence requirements.

Can I get a New Jersey ID without a driver's license?

Yes. New Jersey issues non-driver identification cards through the MVC.

Can I use a passport instead of REAL ID?

Yes. A valid passport is among the federal identity documents that can be used for covered federal purposes instead of a REAL ID-compliant state credential.

What happens if MVC documents do not match?

The MVC can require additional documentation to resolve discrepancies in names or other information before issuing a credential.

Massachusetts ID Laws: What You Need to Know

Massachusetts issues driver's licenses and Mass ID cards through the Registry of Motor Vehicles. This guide explains Massachusetts identification requirements, REAL ID documents, residency, voting identification rules, renewal, replacement, fees and requirements for non-U.S. citizens.

Massachusetts driver's license requirements

Massachusetts uses Class D for passenger vehicles and Class M for motorcycles. A standard Class D or M license application requires an appointment at an RMV Service Center to present documents proving identity and Massachusetts residency. citeturn1search0

Applicants must also meet the applicable learner's permit, road test and vision requirements. The exact document list depends on the credential and the applicant's circumstances.

Standard Class D license

A standard passenger driver's license is generally valid for five years and costs $50. A Class D/M passenger and motorcycle license costs $65. Licenses can be issued for less than five years when the applicant's lawful presence expires earlier, with the fee prorated at $10 per year. citeturn1search3turn0search2

New Massachusetts residents

People moving to Massachusetts must complete the RMV's out-of-state conversion process and provide the documents required for the new credential. The RMV's current identification guide should be checked before the appointment because document requirements vary by applicant and credential.

Applicants under 18

Massachusetts has additional graduated licensing requirements for younger drivers. Learner's permit applicants and junior operators must follow the RMV's age-specific education, supervision and road-test rules.

Massachusetts Mass ID without a driver's license

A Massachusetts Mass ID is a non-driver identification card. To apply, you must be at least 14 years old, be a Massachusetts resident and not hold a valid driver's license from Massachusetts or another jurisdiction. The application is completed in person at an RMV Service Center. citeturn1search1

A standard or REAL Mass ID costs $25 and is generally valid for five years. If the credential is limited by the duration of lawful presence, the fee is prorated at $5 per year. citeturn1search1turn1search2

Temporary and permanent cards

After an in-person Mass ID transaction, the RMV provides a temporary credential valid for 60 days. The permanent Mass ID is mailed and generally arrives within 10 to 14 business days. citeturn1search1

Documents needed for a Massachusetts ID

Massachusetts uses separate document checklists for standard licenses and IDs and REAL ID credentials. The RMV's identification requirements page should be used for the exact documents before an appointment.

REAL ID documents

For a REAL ID, Massachusetts requires:

  • One document proving the applicant is legally in the United States
  • One document proving the applicant's full nine-digit Social Security number
  • Two documents proving the applicant lives in Massachusetts

Examples include a passport or birth certificate for legal presence, a Social Security card or W-2 for the SSN and documents such as a lease, mortgage or utility bill for Massachusetts residency. citeturn1search0turn1search3

Original documents

REAL ID documents must be originals and cannot be laminated. If the applicant's current name differs from the identity document, the RMV requires documentation showing the legal name change, such as a marriage certificate or court document. citeturn1search0

Proof of Massachusetts residency

Residency documentation is a core part of the Massachusetts RMV application. REAL ID applicants must provide two documents proving they live in Massachusetts. The documents should show the applicant's name and Massachusetts address. citeturn1search0

Common examples

  • Residential lease or mortgage document
  • Utility bill
  • Bank or financial statement
  • Insurance document
  • Government correspondence
  • Other documents listed on the RMV's current residency checklist

The RMV's checklist controls which documents qualify. A document that appears generally useful should not be assumed to qualify for a particular transaction without checking the current list.

Massachusetts REAL ID requirements

A Massachusetts REAL ID is an enhanced driver's license or Mass ID that meets the federal security standard. It has a star in the upper-right corner. A standard Massachusetts driver's license or Mass ID does not have that federal designation. citeturn1search0

To obtain a Massachusetts REAL ID: bring one document proving lawful U.S. status, one proving your full Social Security number and two proving Massachusetts residency. The application must be completed in person at an RMV Service Center or participating AAA location for eligible AAA members. citeturn1search0turn1search3

Do you need a REAL ID?

People age 18 and older need a REAL ID or another TSA-accepted identification document for covered federal purposes such as boarding domestic commercial flights and entering certain federal facilities. A passport or another federally accepted credential can be used instead. citeturn1search0

Upgrading during renewal

You can start a REAL ID upgrade online through the RMV's Online Service Center, but the process must be completed in person with the required documents. An existing REAL ID can generally be renewed online if the applicant is otherwise eligible. citeturn1search3

Massachusetts voter identification requirements

Massachusetts does not generally require every voter to show photo ID at the polls. The Secretary of the Commonwealth lists specific situations in which identification may be requested.

When can a voter be asked for ID?

Identification may be required if the person is voting for the first time in Massachusetts, is on the inactive voter list, is casting a provisional or challenged ballot or a poll worker has a practical and legal reason to request identification. citeturn0search3

What can be used as identification?

The Massachusetts Secretary of the Commonwealth lists a driver's license, state ID, recent utility bill, rent receipt or lease, voter registration affidavit, school dormitory or housing letter and other printed identification showing the voter's name and registered address. citeturn0search3

If you do not have the requested ID

A first-time voter who is asked for identification but does not have it can cast a provisional ballot and return with identification before the polls close. For other circumstances, a voter unable to provide requested identification can cast a challenged ballot under the state's election procedures. citeturn0search3

Renewing a Massachusetts ID or driver's license

Massachusetts allows eligible residents to renew a standard or REAL driver's license online. A license can be renewed up to one year before it expires. Existing REAL ID holders can renew online if eligible, while upgrading a standard credential to REAL ID requires an in-person document check. citeturn1search3

Standard and REAL Class D and Class M driver's licenses are generally valid for five years and cost $50. A Class D/M license costs $65. Limited-term credentials can have shorter validity and prorated fees. citeturn1search3

Mass ID renewal

Standard and REAL Mass IDs can generally be renewed online when the applicant is eligible. The fee is $25 for a five-year ID, with limited-term IDs prorated at $5 per year. citeturn1search2

Replacing a lost, stolen or damaged Massachusetts ID

Massachusetts allows eligible residents to replace a driver's license or ID through RMV services. The current replacement fee for a license or ID card is $25. citeturn0search2

If the replacement also involves a REAL ID upgrade, legal-name change or updated immigration documentation, the RMV may require an in-person transaction and supporting original documents.

Name and address changes

Massachusetts requires your current legal name to match the identity documents used for a REAL ID. If your name has changed, bring documentation proving the legal change, such as a marriage certificate or court document. citeturn1search0

Address and personal-information changes can require an RMV transaction. When a REAL ID application or update is involved, the RMV's current document checklist should be followed rather than assuming an ordinary address document is sufficient.

Massachusetts IDs for non-U.S. citizens

Massachusetts issues driver's licenses and Mass IDs to eligible residents who can satisfy the RMV's identity, residency and lawful-presence requirements. The credential's validity can be limited by the duration of the applicant's lawful presence in the United States. citeturn1search3turn1search1

REAL ID for non-citizens

A non-U.S. citizen applying for a REAL ID must provide documentation proving lawful presence in the United States, the required Social Security information and two Massachusetts residency documents. The RMV determines the acceptable immigration documents based on the applicant's status. citeturn1search0

Applicants without an SSN

The RMV's identification requirements provide an alternative process for applicants who do not have a Social Security number. For example, certain non-U.S. citizen applicants can use an SSN denial notice together with immigration documents when applying for a Liquor ID. Credential-specific requirements should be checked before applying. citeturn1search0

Massachusetts Liquor ID

Massachusetts also issues a Liquor ID for people age 21 or older who do not hold a valid Massachusetts driver's license. It is specifically used as proof of age and identity for alcohol-related purposes and is marked “Not for Federal Identification.” citeturn1search0

The RMV requires a document proving the applicant's Social Security number or qualifying SSN denial documentation and a document proving date of birth. Massachusetts does not issue REAL ID Liquor IDs. citeturn1search0

Counterfeit, altered and borrowed identification

A Massachusetts driver's license or Mass ID is a government-issued credential tied to the person to whom it was issued. A counterfeit card, altered genuine credential or another person's credential does not become a valid Massachusetts ID for the person presenting it.

Massachusetts ID law FAQs

Can I get a Massachusetts ID without a driver's license?

Yes. Massachusetts offers a Mass ID to residents age 14 or older who do not hold a valid driver's license.

How many residency documents do I need for a Massachusetts REAL ID?

You need two documents proving that you live in Massachusetts, along with one document proving lawful U.S. status and one proving your full Social Security number.

Do I need a REAL ID to drive in Massachusetts?

No. A standard Massachusetts driver's license remains available. REAL ID affects covered federal purposes such as domestic air travel and access to certain federal facilities.

Does Massachusetts require photo ID to vote?

Not generally. Identification can be required in specific circumstances, including certain first-time voters, inactive voters and voters casting provisional or challenged ballots.

How much does a Massachusetts Mass ID cost?

A standard or REAL Mass ID costs $25 and is generally valid for five years. Limited-term IDs can have prorated fees.

How much does a Massachusetts driver's license cost?

A standard or REAL Class D or Class M driver's license costs $50. A combined Class D/M license costs $65.

Can non-U.S. citizens get a Massachusetts ID?

Eligible non-U.S. citizens can receive Massachusetts credentials when they satisfy the applicable identity, residency, Social Security and lawful-presence requirements.

Can I renew my Massachusetts license online?

Eligible holders can renew a standard or existing REAL driver's license online. Upgrading a standard credential to REAL ID requires an in-person document check.

Maryland ID Laws: What You Need to Know

Maryland issues driver's licenses and identification cards through the Maryland Motor Vehicle Administration. This guide explains Maryland ID requirements, REAL ID documents, residency, voting identification rules, renewal, replacement, fees and requirements for non-U.S. citizens.

Last reviewed: September 26, 2026

Maryland driver's license requirements

Maryland's standard non-commercial driver's license is generally valid for up to eight years. New applicants must establish identity and age, provide Social Security information, prove Maryland residency and meet the applicable vision and licensing requirements.

For a Maryland license or ID application, the MVA lists proof of age and identity, proof of Social Security number and two documents proving Maryland residency. For a driver's license, the applicant must also satisfy the vision requirement. citeturn0search0

Vision screening

A Maryland driver's license applicant must pass a vision screening. For an out-of-state conversion, the MVA says the vision screening must generally have been completed within the previous 24 months. citeturn0search0

New Maryland residents

People moving to Maryland generally transfer their out-of-state license or ID through the MVA. The MVA requires the current out-of-state credential or, in certain cases, a certified driving record that is no more than 30 days old. Maryland also requires the identity, Social Security and residency documents listed in its current document guide. citeturn0search0

Drivers under 18

Applicants under 18 have additional documentation requirements. Maryland may require proof of parental relationship or dependency, such as a tax return or health insurance documentation, depending on the transaction. citeturn0search0

Maryland State ID without a driver's license

Maryland's non-driver identification card is available to residents who can establish identity, legal presence, Maryland residency and Social Security information. The application is completed in person at an MVA branch. citeturn0search1

The MVA mails the permanent ID card to the address on file rather than issuing the permanent card at the time of the branch transaction. A temporary credential may be provided when applicable. citeturn0search1

Free Maryland ID cards

Certain applicants may qualify for a free Maryland ID. The MVA lists qualifying circumstances that include current homelessness, release from a correctional facility within the previous 60 days, certain youth and Department of Human Services programs, people under the care of the Maryland Department of Health and other qualifying state programs. Documentation proving eligibility is required. citeturn0search0

Documents needed for a Maryland ID

Maryland's MVA uses a document-selector system because the exact list depends on the applicant's age, citizenship or immigration status and the credential being requested.

Proof of identity and age

Common identity and age documents include a U.S. birth certificate, U.S. passport, Permanent Resident Card, Certificate of Naturalization, Consular Report of Birth Abroad or Certificate of U.S. Citizenship. Maryland requires originals or certified copies where specified. citeturn0search0

Proof of Social Security number

Acceptable evidence can include an original Social Security card, a W-2 or pay stub displaying the applicant's full SSN, or an SSA-1099 or Non-SSA 1099 showing the full number. citeturn0search0

Legal presence

Applicants who are not U.S. citizens may need immigration documents establishing lawful presence. The MVA's document guide determines the documents required for the applicant's particular status. A person who is not eligible for a REAL ID may still be eligible for a Standard Maryland ID using alternative documentation. citeturn0search1

Proof of Maryland residency

Maryland generally requires two documents showing the applicant's name and Maryland address. The name on the residency documents must match the name on the proof of age and identity. citeturn0search0

Examples of acceptable residency documents

  • Government agency mail or benefits statements
  • Bank, financial account or collection-agency correspondence
  • Utility account communications
  • Maryland vehicle registration or vehicle title
  • Insurance card or insurance policy
  • Residential rental contract
  • Credit-card statement
  • Telephone, cellular or cable account correspondence
  • Mortgage statement or other proof of home ownership
  • Medical or hospital bill

The MVA's current document guide controls the final list. Applicants should use the guide before an appointment because acceptable documents can depend on the transaction. citeturn0search0

Maryland REAL ID requirements

Maryland has been REAL ID compliant since 2011. A Maryland REAL ID driver's license or identification card displays a star in the upper-right corner. citeturn0search2

Typical REAL ID documents: proof of age and identity, Social Security documentation and two proofs of Maryland physical address. Maryland requires documents to be reviewed in person when the applicant must establish or complete REAL ID compliance. citeturn0search2

Maryland's REAL ID guidance says typical documents include an original U.S. birth certificate or passport, a Social Security card, W-2 or qualifying SSA-1099 and two proofs of Maryland physical address. Photocopies, digital scans and photos of documents do not satisfy the federal document requirement. citeturn0search2

Do you need a REAL ID?

A REAL ID or another federally accepted identity document is required for covered federal purposes such as boarding federally regulated domestic commercial flights and accessing certain federal facilities. A passport or another TSA-accepted document can be used instead.

REAL ID fees

Maryland does not charge an additional REAL ID fee when a customer is renewing a driver's license to obtain REAL ID. If an existing older-style credential must be replaced solely to become REAL ID compliant, the MVA has described a duplicate credential fee. citeturn0search2

Maryland voter identification requirements

Maryland generally does not require voters to present a photo ID at the polls. The state's election guidance explains that most voters can vote without showing identification.

There are exceptions. Maryland election officials may request identification from certain first-time voters or voters whose eligibility cannot otherwise be confirmed. Acceptable identification can include a Maryland driver's license or ID, a current utility bill, bank statement, paycheck, government check or another government document showing the voter's name and address.

Registering to vote

Maryland's voter registration process can use a Maryland driver's license or MVA-issued ID number. Voters who do not have one can use other identifying information through the state's registration process, subject to the applicable requirements.

Election Day and provisional voting

Maryland permits eligible voters to address certain registration or identification issues at the polling place through the election procedures applicable to their situation. A voter who cannot be confirmed in the regular process may be required to use a provisional ballot.

Renewing a Maryland ID or driver's license

Maryland driver's licenses are generally valid for up to eight years. The current MVA fee schedule lists an $64 renewal fee for an eight-year non-commercial driver's license, with fees prorated in some circumstances. citeturn0search14

Eligible residents may renew through MVA online services, at an MVA branch or through other channels available for the credential. Some applicants must appear in person when the MVA requires updated documents, a vision screening or other verification.

REAL ID renewal

If the MVA has notified a customer that additional documents are needed for REAL ID compliance, those documents must be presented and reviewed in person. The MVA's online REAL ID document guide helps determine whether an in-person appointment is necessary. citeturn0search2

Replacing a lost, stolen or damaged Maryland ID

Maryland provides duplicate driver's license and ID services through the MVA. The current fee schedule lists a $30 duplicate driver's license fee. Fees for ID cards vary by credential and age. citeturn0search14

If the replacement transaction also requires a name, address, citizenship or REAL ID document update, the MVA may require additional documentation and an in-person visit.

Name and address changes

When a legal name changes, Maryland requires documentation supporting the new name. The MVA's document guide should be used to determine the exact documents needed for the transaction.

For residency or address updates, Maryland may require documents showing the applicant's Maryland address. The same two-document residency standard is used for many license and ID transactions. citeturn0search0

Maryland IDs for non-U.S. citizens

Maryland issues credentials to eligible non-U.S. citizens who meet the applicable identity, legal-presence, Social Security and residency requirements.

For a REAL ID, applicants must provide documentation establishing lawful presence in the United States. The MVA's document guide identifies the appropriate immigration documents for each status. People who are not eligible for REAL ID may still be eligible for a Standard Maryland ID with alternate documentation. citeturn0search1

Limited-term credentials

Maryland issues limited-term driver's licenses to qualifying applicants whose lawful stay in the United States is temporary. The MVA states that the credential is valid only for the period the person is legally allowed to remain in the United States. citeturn0search14

Counterfeit, altered and borrowed identification

A Maryland driver's license or identification card is issued by the MVA to a specific person after identity and eligibility information is verified. A counterfeit card, altered genuine credential or another person's credential does not become a valid Maryland ID for the person presenting it.

For general educational background on how counterfeit IDs can be identified, see How to Tell if an ID Is Fake: Signs on the Front, Back and Photo. The article is general educational material; Maryland's MVA and Maryland law control Maryland-specific requirements.

Maryland ID law FAQs

Can I get a Maryland ID without a driver's license?

Yes. Maryland offers a non-driver identification card. You apply in person and provide identity, legal presence, Maryland residency and Social Security documentation.

How many residency documents do I need in Maryland?

Maryland generally requires two documents showing your name and Maryland address for a new license or ID.

Does Maryland require REAL ID to drive?

No. REAL ID is a federal identity standard. A standard Maryland credential remains available for ordinary state purposes.

Does Maryland require photo ID to vote?

Generally no. Most registered Maryland voters can vote without showing photo ID. Identification can be requested in specific circumstances, such as certain first-time voters.

Can I get a free Maryland ID?

Yes, some residents qualify for a free ID based on circumstances such as homelessness, recent release from a correctional facility or participation in qualifying state programs.

Can non-U.S. citizens get a Maryland ID?

Eligible non-U.S. citizens can receive Maryland credentials when they satisfy the applicable identity, lawful-presence, Social Security and residency requirements.

How long is a Maryland driver's license valid?

A Maryland non-commercial driver's license is valid for a maximum of eight years, although limited-term credentials can have shorter validity periods.

How much does a Maryland driver's license cost?

The current MVA fee schedule lists $64 for an eight-year renewal, $88 for a new eight-year license for applicants age 21 and older and $30 for a duplicate driver's license.

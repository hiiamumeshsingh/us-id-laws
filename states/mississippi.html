Mississippi ID Laws: What You Need to Know

Mississippi issues driver's licenses and identification cards through the Department of Public Safety Driver Service Bureau. This guide explains Mississippi ID requirements, REAL ID, residency, voter identification, renewal, replacement, fees and requirements for non-U.S. citizens.

Mississippi driver's license requirements

Mississippi's regular passenger driver's license is a Class R credential. For an original license, the state's application requirements call for two proofs of identification, including a Social Security card and certified birth certificate, along with two proofs of Mississippi residency for applicants age 18 and older. citeturn1search6

Residency documents

Each residency document must show a physical Mississippi address. Examples listed by Mississippi include an electric or water bill, bank statement, lease or rent agreement, mortgage document or qualifying employer letter. A P.O. Box does not satisfy the physical-address requirement. citeturn1search6

Applicants under 18

Applicants under 18 have additional requirements. Mississippi requires proof of school enrollment through a Certification of Attendance form that is generally no more than 30 days old. If the applicant is married, a marriage license can be used instead of the school-attendance certification. Applicants under 17 also need the required parent, guardian, employer or responsible-adult signatures. citeturn1search6

New Mississippi residents

A person moving to Mississippi must obtain a Mississippi driver's license within 60 days. The state requires surrender of the out-of-state license and generally requires a certified birth certificate, Social Security documentation and two proofs of Mississippi residency. citeturn3search12

Mississippi State ID without a driver's license

Mississippi offers a state identification card to anyone age 6 or older. Applicants must provide an original birth certificate or another accepted identity document, Social Security documentation and two proofs of Mississippi residency. citeturn3search2turn3search3

ID validity and fees

The Mississippi Driver Service Bureau currently lists a four-year identification card at $17 and an eight-year identification card at $33. Duplicate identification cards cost $11. citeturn3search9

Blind and disability identification cards

Mississippi also issues specialized identification cards for qualifying residents who are blind or have a qualifying disability. The current fee schedule lists separate fees and validity periods for these cards. citeturn3search9turn3search4

Documents needed for a Mississippi ID

Mississippi requires original or certified documents for many identity transactions. The Driver Service Bureau specifically states that photocopies are not accepted for the birth-certificate requirement.

Identity and Social Security

For an original driver's license or permit, the state's published requirements call for a Social Security card and certified birth certificate. Applicants with an out-of-state driver's license must also provide the out-of-state license or a lost-license affidavit. citeturn1search6

For a first-time Mississippi ID card, DPS lists an original or certified birth certificate, Social Security card or official government correspondence showing the full nine-digit SSN and two proofs of residency. citeturn3search2

Name changes

Mississippi accepts specific legal documents for a name change on a driver's license or ID, including a marriage license, divorce decree, adoption order or court order. citeturn3search1

Proof of Mississippi residency

Mississippi generally requires two proofs of residency for an original license or ID. Each document must contain a physical Mississippi address. citeturn1search6turn3search2

Mississippi REAL ID requirements

Mississippi takes a simpler approach to REAL ID than many states. The Department of Public Safety says all Mississippi driver's licenses and identification cards are REAL ID compliant and automatically display the gold star. citeturn3search0turn3search1

What this means: Mississippi residents do not have to choose between a standard noncompliant card and a REAL ID credential. The state's current driver's licenses and IDs are issued as REAL ID-compliant credentials.

REAL ID is a federal identity standard. Since May 7, 2025, adults using a state-issued credential for covered federal purposes such as domestic commercial air travel need a REAL ID-compliant credential or another federally accepted document such as a U.S. passport.

Mississippi voter identification requirements

Mississippi requires voters casting ballots in person at polling places to present an acceptable photo ID. Voters casting absentee ballots in person at a Circuit Clerk's Office also generally must present photo ID. citeturn1search0turn1search5

Acceptable photo ID

The Mississippi Secretary of State lists several acceptable credentials, including a valid Mississippi driver's license, Mississippi state ID, U.S. passport, qualifying government employee ID, qualifying Mississippi pistol or revolver license, tribal photo ID, U.S. military ID, qualifying Mississippi college or university student ID, official Mississippi Voter Identification Card and other qualifying current government-issued photo IDs. citeturn1search0

If you do not have photo ID

A voter who does not have an acceptable photo ID on Election Day can vote by affidavit ballot. The voter then has five business days to present an acceptable photo ID or apply for a Mississippi Voter Identification Card at the Circuit Clerk's Office. citeturn1search0

Free Mississippi Voter ID

Any eligible Mississippi voter who does not have another acceptable photo ID qualifies for a free Mississippi Voter Identification Card. The card is issued through a Circuit Clerk's Office and is valid for voter-identification purposes while the voter remains qualified. citeturn0search1turn1search5

Voter ID exemptions

Mississippi lists exemptions for voters who vote absentee by mail, certain overseas or military absentee voters, voters with a religious objection to being photographed and registered voters who vote at a polling place located inside a state-licensed care facility where they reside. citeturn1search4

Renewing a Mississippi ID or driver's license

Mississippi allows eligible residents to renew driver's licenses and identification cards online. The DPS FAQ says a license may be renewed six months before expiration. citeturn3search1

Mississippi's current fee schedule lists a four-year Class R renewal at $24 and an eight-year Class R renewal at $47. Identification-card renewal is $17 for four years or $33 for eight years. citeturn3search9

Non-U.S. citizen renewals

Non-U.S. citizens cannot renew their Mississippi driver's license or ID online. DPS says they must appear in person within 30 days of the expiration date with the required Homeland Security immigration documents. citeturn3search5

Replacing a lost, stolen or damaged Mississippi ID

Mississippi provides duplicate license and ID services online for eligible customers. The current DPS fee schedule lists $11 for a duplicate driver's license and $11 for a duplicate identification card. citeturn3search9

If a replacement also involves a legal name change or immigration-status update, additional documentation and an in-person transaction may be required.

Name and address changes

Mississippi allows eligible customers to change their address online through DPS services. The Driver Service Bureau also lists address changes among its available online transactions. citeturn3search1turn3search8

Name changes require legal documentation. DPS specifically lists a marriage license, divorce decree, adoption order or court order as acceptable legal documents for changing the name on a Mississippi driver's license or ID. citeturn3search1

Mississippi IDs for non-U.S. citizens

Mississippi issues driver's licenses and identification cards to eligible non-U.S. citizens who provide the required immigration, identity, Social Security and residency documentation. The license or ID is generally valid for the applicant's authorized period of stay or no more than four years. citeturn3search5

Lawful-status verification

DPS verifies immigration documentation through the federal Systematic Alien Verification for Entitlements (SAVE) program. If the status cannot be confirmed immediately, the transaction can be delayed while additional verification is completed. citeturn3search5

Temporary or limited-term credentials

Applicants who are not U.S. citizens, U.S. nationals, lawful permanent residents, refugees or asylees may receive a short-term driver's license or ID tied to the period of lawful presence established by federal immigration documents. citeturn3search5

Mississippi Mobile ID

Mississippi also offers a Mobile ID app that provides a digital version of a Mississippi driver's license or state-issued ID. DPS describes it as a secure digital credential linked to the state's system of record and designed for identity and age verification at participating locations. citeturn3search6

The mobile credential is not a separate driver's license. It is a digital representation of an eligible Mississippi physical credential.

Counterfeit, altered and borrowed identification

A Mississippi driver's license or identification card is issued by the Department of Public Safety to a specific person after identity and eligibility information are verified. A counterfeit credential, altered genuine credential or another person's genuine credential does not become a valid Mississippi ID for the person presenting it.

Mississippi ID law FAQs

Can I get a Mississippi ID without a driver's license?

Yes. Mississippi offers identification cards to people age 6 and older who meet the application requirements.

How many residency documents do I need in Mississippi?

Two proofs of Mississippi residency are generally required for an original license or identification card. Each must show a physical address.

Are Mississippi driver's licenses REAL ID compliant?

Yes. Mississippi DPS says all Mississippi driver's licenses and identification cards are REAL ID compliant and automatically receive the gold star.

Does Mississippi require photo ID to vote?

Yes for most voters voting in person at the polls or in person by absentee ballot at a Circuit Clerk's Office. Mississippi also provides exemptions and an affidavit-ballot process.

Can I get a free Mississippi Voter ID?

Yes. An eligible registered voter who does not have another acceptable photo ID can obtain a free Mississippi Voter Identification Card through a Circuit Clerk's Office.

Can non-U.S. citizens get a Mississippi ID?

Eligible non-U.S. citizens can receive a Mississippi license or ID when they provide the required immigration, identity, Social Security and residency documents.

Can I renew my Mississippi license online?

Eligible residents can renew online. Non-U.S. citizens must renew in person with the required immigration documents.

Does Mississippi have a digital ID?

Yes. Mississippi offers Mobile ID, a digital version of an eligible Mississippi driver's license or state-issued ID.

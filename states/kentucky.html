Kentucky ID Laws: What You Need to Know

Kentucky issues driver's licenses, permits and identification cards through Driver Licensing Regional Offices. This guide explains Kentucky ID requirements, REAL ID documents, residency, voter identification, renewal, replacement, fees and rules for non-U.S. citizens.

Last reviewed: September 26, 2026

Kentucky's current system separates standard and REAL ID credentials. REAL ID upgrades require an in-person visit with the required documents, while eligible routine renewals can use the myDrive online portal, mail or an office visit.

Kentucky driver's license requirements

Kentucky first-time driver's license applicants must complete the required testing and present the documents required for their age and credential type. Testing is scheduled and administered by the Kentucky State Police. citeturn0search8

Under 18

Kentucky uses a Graduated Driver Licensing Program for applicants under 18. Applicants must provide proof of identity, Social Security number and Kentucky residency, plus the school compliance documentation and parent or guardian signature required for the application. For a REAL ID, two proofs of Kentucky residency are required instead of one. citeturn0search11

Age 18 and older

Applicants age 18 and older follow the adult licensing process. First-time applicants provide one proof of identity, one proof of Social Security number and one proof of Kentucky residency for a standard credential. Two residency proofs are required when applying for a REAL ID. citeturn0search12

New Kentucky residents

New residents have 30 days to obtain a Kentucky driver's license. Applicants should bring their valid out-of-state license or permit and the required documents to a Driver Licensing Regional Office. citeturn0search8

Kentucky State ID without a driver's license

Kentucky's personal identification card is a non-driver credential. Applicants age 15 or older can apply for a standard ID at a Driver Licensing Regional Office. Applicants under 18 must have a parent or guardian present. citeturn0search0

Kentucky does not allow a person to hold more than one license or personal identification card at the same time. Since July 15, 2024, a person who currently holds a valid Kentucky instruction permit or operator's license cannot be issued a personal ID card. If a license or permit is suspended or revoked, an ID may be issued, but the ID must be surrendered when the driving credential is reinstated. citeturn0search0

Standard ID fees

A standard Kentucky ID costs $11.50 for four years or $23 for eight years. A REAL ID costs $14 for four years or $28 for eight years. citeturn0search0turn0search2

Documents needed for a Kentucky ID

Kentucky's document requirements depend on whether the applicant wants a standard credential or REAL ID and whether the person is a first-time applicant, a renewal applicant or changing information.

Proof of identity

For a REAL ID, Kentucky requires one original or certified document proving identity. Examples include a certified birth certificate, valid U.S. passport or U.S. citizenship or naturalization certificate. Photocopies are not accepted for the identity document. citeturn0search1

Social Security number

One document showing the full Social Security number is required for a REAL ID. Examples include a Social Security card, SSNAP replacement printout, W-2, 1099 or paycheck statement. Kentucky requires the Social Security card for first-time credentials or out-of-state transfers. citeturn0search1

Name changes

If the applicant's current legal name differs from the name on the identity or lawful-status document, Kentucky requires legal proof of the change. Examples include a certified marriage certificate, certified divorce decree or certified legal name-change order. The name must also be updated with the Social Security Administration before applying. citeturn0search1

Proof of Kentucky residency

Kentucky generally requires one proof of Kentucky residency for a standard first-time adult credential and two proofs for a REAL ID. REAL ID residency documents must generally be dated within the last year when the document type has a date requirement. citeturn0search1turn0search12

Accepted examples include utility bills, telephone bills, U.S. Postal Service postmarked mail, a Kentucky driver's license or ID, lease or rental agreement and mortgage statement. Forwarded mail cannot be used as proof of residency. citeturn0search1

Applicants under 18

If an applicant under 18 does not have residency proof in their own name, a parent or guardian can provide residency proof in the parent's or guardian's name to establish that the applicant lives with them. citeturn0search11

No fixed address

Kentucky has a specific Homeless ID Card program. An applicant without a fixed or permanent address may use a signed letter from a homeless shelter, health care facility or social service agency confirming that the person is receiving services and is a Kentucky resident. citeturn0search0

Kentucky REAL ID requirements

Kentucky offers both standard and REAL ID versions of driver's licenses, permits and identification cards. A REAL ID displays a black star and can be used for federal purposes covered by the REAL ID rules. citeturn0search6

For a Kentucky REAL ID: bring one proof of identity, one proof of Social Security number and two proofs of Kentucky residency. Bring legal name-change documentation when the information does not match. The REAL ID application must be completed in person at a Driver Licensing Regional Office. citeturn0search1turn0search6

Since May 7, 2025, Kentucky's standard credentials are not accepted for covered federal purposes such as boarding U.S. commercial flights or entering restricted federal facilities. A valid U.S. passport or another TSA-accepted document can be used instead. citeturn0search6

Upgrading an existing credential

If a current Kentucky license expires more than six months after the application date, an eligible holder can upgrade to a REAL ID for a $15 fee while keeping the existing expiration date. If the credential is within six months of expiration, the applicant can renew and choose a four- or eight-year REAL ID term. citeturn0search9

After applying, Kentucky issues a 30-day temporary document while the permanent credential is mailed. The temporary document is valid for driving and identification purposes but is not accepted by TSA as a REAL ID. citeturn0search6turn0search9

Kentucky voter identification requirements

Kentucky requires voters to produce identification at the polling place. The Kentucky State Board of Elections defines acceptable proof of identification as a document issued by the United States or Commonwealth of Kentucky that contains the voter's name and photograph, or qualifying photo identification issued by the U.S. Department of Defense, a U.S. college or university or a Kentucky local government. citeturn0search10

Examples include a Kentucky driver's license or ID, U.S. passport, military ID, Kentucky or out-of-state college ID, Kentucky local-government ID and qualifying tribal or other government-issued photo credentials. citeturn0search10

Free Voter ID Card

Voter ID cards are available at no cost at Kentucky Driver Licensing Regional Offices. Applicants must be eligible to vote and either already be registered or register at the time the card is issued. An applicant cannot hold another state-issued credential from Kentucky or another state while receiving the voter ID card. citeturn0search0

Renewing a Kentucky ID or driver's license

Eligible Kentucky driver's license holders can renew online through the myDrive portal, by mail or in person at a Driver Licensing Regional Office. A vision screening is required before renewing a driver's license. citeturn0search14

Standard driver's license renewal costs $21.50 for four years or $43 for eight years. A REAL ID driver's license costs $24 for four years or $48 for eight years. citeturn0search14

Eligible ID-card holders can also renew through myDrive. If the information on the existing ID is still correct, additional documents are generally not required. Name changes, address changes and REAL ID upgrades require the applicable supporting documents. citeturn0search0turn0search9

Replacing a lost, stolen or damaged Kentucky ID

Kentucky allows eligible customers to renew or replace an ID card online through myDrive when the information on the existing credential is unchanged. Additional documents are required when the applicant is changing a name or address or upgrading to REAL ID. citeturn0search0

A standard ID replacement follows the current personal-ID fee structure. Customers should use the myDrive eligibility check or contact a Driver Licensing Regional Office when the credential was lost, stolen or damaged and personal information also needs to change.

REAL ID replacement

If the existing credential is already a REAL ID and the information remains the same, Kentucky says no additional documents are required for a renewal or update. A first-time REAL ID upgrade is different and requires an in-person document check. citeturn0search9

Name and address changes

Kentucky requires legal documentation when the current legal name differs from the identity or lawful-status document. The name must be updated with the Social Security Administration before applying for the Kentucky credential. citeturn0search1turn0search13

For a REAL ID address change, Kentucky requires one proof of Kentucky residency. Address changes may be completed online through myDrive, by mail or in person when the applicant meets the eligibility requirements. citeturn0search9

Gender changes also require legal proof when the gender marker on the current credential differs from the supporting identity document. Kentucky lists a court order or qualifying medical documentation among the possible supporting records. citeturn0search15

Kentucky IDs for non-U.S. citizens

Eligible non-U.S. citizens and permanent residents can apply for a Kentucky driver's permit, driver's license or ID card at a Driver Licensing Regional Office. Permanent residents must present a current Permanent Resident Card or I-551 with photo issued by USCIS when applying for a REAL ID. citeturn0search4

Temporary lawful status

Non-U.S. citizens who are legally present in the United States on a temporary basis may qualify for a Kentucky REAL ID. These credentials are issued for a limited period and cannot be valid beyond the person's authorized stay. If the authorized stay has no specific end date, Kentucky says the credential may be issued for up to one year. citeturn0search4

Temporary-status applicants must provide proof of identity and lawful status plus two proofs of Kentucky residency for a REAL ID. citeturn0search4

Counterfeit, altered and borrowed identification

Kentucky's credential system is built around verifying the identity and residency of the person receiving the credential. A counterfeit card, altered credential or another person's genuine credential is not equivalent to a state-issued credential belonging to the person presenting it.

For general educational context on what can happen after someone is caught with fraudulent identification, see Caught with a Fake ID: Consequences and What Happens Next. Kentucky law and official Kentucky guidance control the rules that apply in Kentucky.

Kentucky ID law FAQs

Can I get a Kentucky ID without a driver's license?

Yes. Kentucky issues personal identification cards. A person cannot hold more than one state-issued license or ID at the same time, and a person with a valid Kentucky permit or driver's license cannot also hold a Kentucky personal ID.

How many residency documents do I need for a Kentucky REAL ID?

Two proofs of Kentucky residency are required for a REAL ID.

Do I need a REAL ID to drive in Kentucky?

No. Kentucky offers both standard and REAL ID credentials. The REAL ID version is needed for covered federal purposes unless you use another federally accepted document.

Does Kentucky require photo ID to vote?

Yes. Kentucky requires identification at the polling place and lists qualifying state, federal, military, educational and local-government photo credentials.

Can I get a free Kentucky ID for voting?

Yes. Eligible voters can receive a Voter ID Card at no cost through a Driver Licensing Regional Office.

Can non-U.S. citizens get a Kentucky ID?

Yes. Eligible non-U.S. citizens and permanent residents can apply for Kentucky credentials when they meet the identity, lawful-status and residency requirements.

Can I renew my Kentucky ID online?

Eligible ID-card holders can renew through myDrive. First-time REAL ID upgrades must be completed in person with the required documents.

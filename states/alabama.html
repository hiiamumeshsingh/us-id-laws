Alabama ID Laws: What You Need to Know

Driver's licenses, non-driver IDs, STAR ID, required documents, residency, voter ID, renewals, replacements and other Alabama identification rules.

Last reviewed: September 26, 2026. Alabama identification requirements can change. Use this page to understand the rules, then verify current details with the relevant Alabama agency before applying.

Alabama driver's license requirements

Alabama driver's licenses are issued by the Alabama Law Enforcement Agency (ALEA). For applicants age 16 and older, ALEA currently requires identification documents, Social Security documentation and, in applicable cases, school enrollment or graduation documentation. The agency also lists two proofs of principal residence for the standard license process. citeturn3search0turn3search5

Applicants who have never been licensed in Alabama, or whose Alabama license has been expired for more than three years, must pass the required driver's examination. ALEA directs applicants to a driver license office for the licensing process. citeturn0search11

  • Age 15: Alabama has a learner-license process with specific document and school requirements.
  • Age 16 and older: Standard driver or learner-license requirements apply based on the applicant's circumstances.
  • First-time applicants: Testing and document requirements can apply depending on prior licensing history.
  • Applicants under 19: ALEA lists school enrollment or graduation documentation for certain first-time applications.

See the official ALEA document and fee requirements for the current checklist.

Getting an Alabama state ID without a driver's license

Alabama issues non-driver identification cards. You do not need a driver's license simply to have an Alabama state-issued identification card.

ALEA currently lists a primary identity document verifying name and date of birth, Social Security documentation and an additional identification document when the primary document is non-photo. The agency also lists two proofs of principal residence for a non-driver identification card.

ALEA currently lists $36.25 as the purchase fee for a non-driver identification card. First-time issuance can also involve a document examination fee. Check the agency's current fee information before applying. citeturn0search0

What documents do you need in Alabama?

Alabama organizes identification documents into primary and secondary categories. The exact combination depends on the credential and the applicant's circumstances.

01

Primary documents

Examples include a certified U.S. birth certificate, an unexpired U.S. passport, an Alabama ID or driver's license, a certificate of naturalization or citizenship and certain immigration documents.

02

Secondary documents

Examples include another state-issued license or ID, military documents, professional licenses, Selective Service cards, VA cards and certain school or insurance IDs.

ALEA publishes the complete primary and secondary document lists. Document-acceptance rules can be specific, so use the Alabama list rather than a generic national checklist.

Proof of residency in Alabama

ALEA currently lists two proofs of principal residence for driver's licenses and non-driver identification cards. Examples include a voter registration card, mortgage contract, current lease, property-tax documentation, vehicle registration, a recent water/gas/electric utility bill, certain court documents, school enrollment documentation, homeowners insurance and government correspondence mailed to the applicant's physical address.

If a utility bill or similar document is in a spouse's or parent's name, ALEA says additional documentation may be required to establish the relationship and residence address. citeturn3search0turn3search6

Alabama STAR ID: the state's REAL ID

Alabama calls its REAL ID-compliant driver's license or non-driver identification card a STAR ID. ALEA developed the STAR ID program to meet federal REAL ID requirements.

A STAR ID is not a separate federal card. It is an Alabama credential that meets the federal standard.

Do you need a STAR ID?

A STAR ID is not required simply to drive in Alabama or to vote. It matters for covered federal purposes. Since May 7, 2025, adults flying domestically have needed a REAL ID-compliant credential or another identification document accepted by TSA. citeturn3search4turn3search3

ALEA states that people with another accepted federal ID, such as a valid U.S. passport, can use that instead of obtaining a STAR ID for applicable federal purposes. citeturn3search4

STAR ID documents

ALEA's STAR ID document list requires documentation covering identity/date of birth, Social Security number and principal residence. The agency says applicants must present four documents across those categories and that photocopies are not accepted. citeturn3search1

Use the official STAR ID document list before visiting an examining office.

Alabama voter ID requirements

Alabama requires valid photo identification for voting in person. The Secretary of State lists accepted forms including an Alabama driver's license or non-driver ID, Alabama Photo Voter ID, certain state or federal IDs, a U.S. passport, qualifying employee or student IDs, military ID and tribal ID. citeturn0search4turn0search5

If a voter does not have an accepted photo ID, Alabama provides a free Photo Voter ID option for eligible registered voters. The Secretary of State also describes a procedure under which a voter without photo ID at the polls can be positively identified by two election officials and vote under the applicable procedure. citeturn0search2turn0search6

Absentee voting has separate identification rules and certain federal-law exceptions. Check the current Alabama Secretary of State guidance for your situation. citeturn0search6

Renewing an Alabama ID or driver's license

Renewal options depend on the credential and the applicant's circumstances. ALEA provides online driver-license services and instructions for renewal and duplicate credentials.

Use the official ALEA online driver license service or start from ALEA's website. Be cautious with third-party sites that add fees. ALEA has warned customers about third-party websites that upcharge for renewal services. citeturn3search9turn3search10

Replacing a lost, stolen or damaged Alabama ID

If your Alabama driver's license or non-driver ID is lost, stolen or damaged, use ALEA's official duplicate or replacement process. Eligibility for online replacement depends on the credential and your circumstances.

If the loss involved suspected identity theft, consider the identity-theft steps that apply to your situation rather than simply ordering a replacement card. A replacement credential does not by itself resolve misuse of personal information.

Changing your name or address

Name changes can require documentation connecting the old and new legal names. The exact process depends on the reason for the change and the credential involved.

For an address change, ALEA provides a DL-99 form for changing the address on an Alabama driver's license or identification card record. ALEA notes that this updates the record and does not itself issue a new license or ID card. citeturn3search12

Non-U.S. citizens and Alabama identification

ALEA publishes separate documentation requirements for non-U.S. citizens. Its current guidance lists a valid foreign passport with an acceptable visa or resident-alien card, Social Security number verification or a Social Security non-eligibility letter and qualifying documentation concerning authorized presence.

Immigration documentation can be situation-specific. If your status or documents are unusual, use ALEA's current requirements rather than relying on a general state ID checklist.

Alabama ID fees

Fees depend on the credential and transaction. ALEA currently lists $36.25 for a standard Alabama driver's license purchase and $36.25 for a non-driver identification card, with separate testing or transaction fees in applicable situations. Credit-card transactions may also carry a convenience fee. citeturn3search0turn3search5

Fees can change, so the official ALEA fee information should be the final check before applying.

Fraudulent, altered or borrowed identification

A government-issued Alabama ID is different from a novelty, replica, counterfeit or altered document. An ID being similar in appearance does not make it an official credential.

Alabama law addresses false statements in driver's-license applications and dealing in false identification documents. Alabama Code § 32-6-18 addresses false statements and material omissions in driver's-license applications, while § 31-13-14 addresses dealing in false identification documents. citeturn2search1turn2search9

If you're researching the terminology around genuine state IDs, counterfeit documents and novelty cards, this explanation of what a fake ID is provides background on the distinction. It is not a substitute for Alabama law or official government guidance.

For an official identification purpose, use the government-issued credential or other identification specifically accepted for that purpose.

Frequently asked questions

Does Alabama have a state ID that isn't a driver's license?

Yes. ALEA issues non-driver identification cards. The document and residency requirements are separate from the privilege to drive.

Is STAR ID mandatory for everyone in Alabama?

No. STAR ID is Alabama's REAL ID-compliant credential. It is not required simply to drive or vote. It matters for covered federal purposes, including domestic air travel for adults, unless another TSA-accepted identification document is used. citeturn3search4turn3search3

How much does an Alabama ID cost?

ALEA currently lists $36.25 for a non-driver identification card and $36.25 for a standard driver's license purchase, with additional fees possible depending on the transaction.

How many proofs of residency does Alabama require?

ALEA currently lists two proofs of principal residence for driver's licenses and non-driver identification cards.

Can I use a passport instead of a STAR ID?

For applicable federal purposes such as domestic air travel, a valid U.S. passport is among the identification documents accepted by TSA. citeturn3search4turn0search5

Where can I get an Alabama Photo Voter ID?

The Alabama Secretary of State provides free Photo Voter ID options through locations including county Boards of Registrars and mobile units. Eligibility and documentation requirements apply. citeturn0search4turn0search6

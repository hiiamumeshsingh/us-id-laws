Maine ID Laws: What You Need to Know

Maine issues driver's licenses and non-driver identification cards through the Bureau of Motor Vehicles. This guide explains Maine ID requirements, REAL ID documents, residency, voting identification rules, renewal, replacement, fees and requirements for people who are not U.S. citizens.

Last reviewed: September 26, 2026

Maine driver's license requirements

Maine BMV branch offices issue driver's licenses. For a standard license application, Maine requires proof of Maine residency, proof of U.S. citizenship or legal presence, two different forms proving identity, and a completed vision screening.

One identity document must show the applicant's date of birth and another must contain the applicant's written signature. If the applicant's Social Security number is not already on file, it must be provided. A person who believes they are ineligible for an SSN must provide an unexpired immigration document, such as an I-94, so BMV can determine eligibility. citeturn2search16

Name changes

If the applicant's legal name has changed, Maine requires documentation supporting the change. Examples include a court document, divorce decree or marriage certificate. citeturn2search16

Vision screening

A completed vision screening is part of the licensing process. Maine's renewal guidance also has age-based vision-screening requirements for certain renewals. citeturn2search14

New residents

A person converting an out-of-state license must follow Maine's conversion process and provide the required documentation. The BMV's current fee schedule lists a $35 application fee plus the applicable license fee for a standard Class C conversion. citeturn1search5

Maine State ID without a driver's license

Maine offers a non-driver identification card to people who can prove they are legally in the United States and have a physical address in Maine. The standard State ID fee is $5. A REAL ID State Identification Card costs $30. citeturn1search7

State ID cards are issued through Maine BMV branch offices and mobile units. The card can also be renewed online or in person when the applicant meets the BMV's renewal requirements. citeturn1search7

Social Security information

If the Social Security number is not already on file with the Secretary of State, the applicant must provide it. Someone who believes they are ineligible for an SSN must present an unexpired immigration document to help BMV determine that ineligibility. citeturn1search7

Documents needed for a Maine ID

Maine's document requirements depend on whether you are applying for a standard credential or REAL ID. Original documents or documents certified by the issuing agency are required for REAL ID.

Identity, date of birth and legal presence

A standard driver's license application requires two different identity documents. One must show date of birth and the other must contain a written signature. The applicant must also show U.S. citizenship or lawful presence. citeturn2search16

For REAL ID, one document must establish identity, date of birth and U.S. citizenship, lawful permanent residence or another lawful status. The current name must match the identity document unless the applicant provides a clear chain of legal name-change documents. citeturn1search8

Social Security number

Maine requires the Social Security number for driver's license and non-driver identification card applications and renewals. The physical Social Security card is not required for a REAL ID if the applicant can otherwise provide the number or evidence of SSN ineligibility. citeturn1search8turn2search14

Proof of Maine residency

Maine distinguishes between standard credentials and REAL ID when it comes to residency documentation.

CredentialResidency proof
Non-REAL ID license or State IDOne document showing a physical Maine address
REAL ID license or State IDTwo documents showing a physical Maine address

Examples of acceptable Maine residency documents include a utility bill, paycheck stub, mortgage statement, Maine vehicle registration, tax bill, tax return, W-2, government-issued document or other qualifying record showing the applicant's physical address. A P.O. Box by itself is not acceptable. citeturn0search2turn1search8

Address changes

Maine does not currently allow an address change through its online BMV system. The Secretary of State says applicants can request an address update by phone, email or the BMV contact form. citeturn2search3

Maine REAL ID requirements

Maine began issuing REAL ID-compliant driver's licenses and State ID cards on July 1, 2019. A REAL ID application requires identity, date of birth, citizenship or lawful status, Social Security information and two proofs of Maine residence. citeturn0search1turn1search8

Important: Your first Maine REAL ID application must be completed in person at a BMV branch office or mobile unit. If you already have a REAL ID, Maine now allows eligible customers to renew it online or at participating locations under the current renewal rules. citeturn0search1turn1search9

REAL ID and federal travel

Since May 7, 2025, a REAL ID or another federally accepted identity document is required for covered federal purposes, including boarding commercial aircraft and accessing certain secure federal facilities. A passport or another TSA-accepted document can be used instead. citeturn1search9

One REAL ID credential

Maine states that a person cannot have both a REAL ID-compliant driver's license and a REAL ID-compliant identification card. The REAL ID regulations limit an individual to one REAL ID credential. citeturn0search1

Maine voter identification requirements

Maine's voting rules are different from its driver's license and State ID rules. If you are already registered to vote, you do not need to show ID to receive a ballot. Maine's Secretary of State states this directly in its voter-rights guidance. citeturn2search7

Registering to vote for the first time

When registering for the first time, Maine allows several forms of identity evidence. These include a government photo ID, a government document without a photo, an official document showing the voter's name and address, a qualifying Maine student photo ID or a verified unique identifier such as a Maine driver's license number or the last four digits of a Social Security number. citeturn1search0turn1search1

Election Day registration

Maine permits same-day voter registration. If a person registers at the polls on Election Day, the person must show ID and proof of where they live. A person who is already registered can vote without showing ID. citeturn2search7

Challenged ballots

Maine's voter-rights guidance says a person who is at the voting place but lacks ID or proof of residence when registering can cast a challenged ballot, subject to the state's election procedures. citeturn2search7

Renewing a Maine ID or driver's license

Maine allows eligible residents to renew driver's licenses and State IDs online or at a BMV branch. A driver's license can generally be renewed as early as six months before expiration. Maine's current renewal guidance says appointments are not required for branch-office renewals, including REAL ID renewals. citeturn2search14

For renewal, BMV may require proof of identification, Maine residency and legal presence. Social Security information must be on file. If the applicant is not eligible for an SSN, Maine accepts an unexpired immigration document or qualifying Social Security Administration statement. citeturn2search0

Online renewal limitations

Maine's online renewal service is available to eligible holders with an active credential. The online service can be unavailable when the person has not previously provided proof of legal presence or residency, has changed their name, has a suspended or revoked license, holds a commercial license or needs a required eye examination. citeturn2search8

Replacing a lost, stolen or damaged Maine ID

Maine offers duplicate driver's license and State ID services online to many eligible customers, as well as through BMV branches, mobile units and mail. A duplicate non-REAL-ID license or ID costs $5, while a duplicate REAL ID license or ID costs $30. citeturn2search1

When applying at a branch, Maine may require two identity documents, with one showing date of birth and the other bearing a written signature. Proof of legal presence and Maine residency may also be required. citeturn2search1

Name and address changes

For a legal name change, Maine requires documentation showing the change, such as a marriage certificate, divorce decree or court document. REAL ID applicants must be able to show a clear trail from the name on the identity document to the current legal name. citeturn2search16turn1search8

Address changes cannot currently be completed through Maine's online BMV system. The BMV provides phone, email and contact-form options for updating the address associated with a license or ID. citeturn2search3

Maine IDs for non-U.S. citizens

Maine allows people who can establish lawful presence in the United States to apply for a driver's license or non-driver identification card. Applicants who are not eligible for a Social Security number must provide an unexpired immigration document, such as an I-94, so BMV can determine their SSN ineligibility. citeturn2search16turn1search7

REAL ID for non-citizens

REAL ID applicants must provide documentation establishing lawful status in the United States. Maine accepts documentation for U.S. citizenship, lawful permanent residence and other lawful status categories listed in the current REAL ID checklist. citeturn1search8

If immigration or other personally identifying information changes, Maine may require the updated documentation to be verified again before the credential can be issued or renewed. citeturn0search1

Counterfeit, altered and borrowed identification

A Maine driver's license or State ID is a government-issued credential tied to the person to whom it was issued. A counterfeit card, altered credential or another person's genuine credential does not become a valid Maine ID for the person presenting it.

For general educational background on how counterfeit documents can be identified, see Fake ID Detector Guide: What Bouncers and Stores Look For. That article discusses document inspection at a general level; Maine's BMV and Maine law remain the relevant sources for Maine-specific requirements.

Maine ID law FAQs

Can I get a Maine ID without a driver's license?

Yes. Maine offers a non-driver State Identification Card to people who can prove legal presence in the United States and a physical Maine address.

How many residency documents do I need for a Maine REAL ID?

You generally need two documents showing your physical Maine residence. A P.O. Box alone does not satisfy the REAL ID residency requirement.

Do I need a REAL ID to drive in Maine?

No. A standard Maine driver's license remains available. REAL ID affects covered federal purposes such as domestic air travel and access to certain federal facilities.

Does Maine require photo ID to vote?

If you are already registered, Maine says you do not need to show ID to receive a ballot. ID and proof of residence are required when registering on Election Day.

How much does a Maine State ID cost?

A standard Maine State ID costs $5. A REAL ID State Identification Card costs $30 under the current BMV fee schedule.

Can non-U.S. citizens get a Maine ID?

Yes, if they can establish lawful presence and meet the applicable identity, residency and Social Security requirements. People who are ineligible for an SSN must provide qualifying immigration documentation.

Can I renew my Maine ID online?

Eligible Maine residents can renew a State ID online. Some circumstances, such as a name change or missing proof of legal presence or residency, require an in-person transaction.

Can I replace a lost Maine ID online?

Many active Maine license and ID holders can order a duplicate online. The standard duplicate fee is $5 and the REAL ID duplicate fee is $30.

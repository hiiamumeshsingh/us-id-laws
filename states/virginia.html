Virginia ID Laws: What You Need to Know

Virginia ID laws cover driver's licenses, non-driver identification cards, REAL ID, proof of identity and residency, voter identification, renewal and replacement rules. This guide explains the main requirements in plain language and links to official Virginia sources.

Virginia DMVDriver's licenses, permits and state identification cards are issued by the Virginia Department of Motor Vehicles.

Virginia ID requirements at a glance

Identity

Virginia DMV requires one proof of identity for a driver's license, learner's permit or ID card. Documents are subject to verification.

Legal presence

One proof of legal presence is required for driver's licenses and ID cards. Additional federal documentation applies to REAL ID.

Residency

Virginia generally requires two proofs of Virginia residency when applying for a license, learner's permit or ID card.

Social Security

If you have been issued an SSN, Virginia requires proof unless DMV can electronically verify the number you provide.

Important: Virginia has both standard and REAL ID credentials. REAL ID is optional, but a standard credential marked "Federal Limits Apply" is not accepted for certain federal purposes such as domestic air travel.

Virginia driver's license requirements

To get a Virginia driver's license, you must be a Virginia resident and at least 16 years and 3 months old. A learner's permit can be obtained beginning at age 15 years and 6 months.

Required documents

When applying for a driver's license, learner's permit or CDL, Virginia DMV requires one proof of identity, one proof of legal presence, two proofs of Virginia residency and proof of your SSN if you have one. If your name differs across documents, you must provide documentation showing the name change.

Virginia DMV says original documents are required and are subject to verification. Temporary documents and photocopies are not accepted.

New Virginia residents

New residents must obtain a Virginia driver's license within 60 days of moving to the Commonwealth. A person holding a commercial driver's license must obtain a Virginia CDL within 30 days.

People who are full-time students and meet Virginia's nonresident student conditions may be able to drive using a valid out-of-state or foreign license. Military personnel stationed in Virginia also have specific exemptions.

First-time drivers

Virginia has age-specific driver education and learner's permit requirements. The DMV has announced additional first-time-driver training rules beginning January 1, 2027 for people who have never held a license, including separate requirements for ages 18–20 and 21 or older.

Virginia state ID card requirements

Virginia residents of any age who do not already hold a Virginia learner's permit or driver's license can apply for a Virginia ID card. A person cannot hold a Virginia driver's license and ID card at the same time.

For a standard ID application, Virginia DMV requires one proof of identity, one proof of legal presence, two proofs of Virginia residency and proof of SSN if one has been issued. Proof of a name change is required when the names do not match.

Virginia also offers a child ID for minors under 15, a hearing-impaired ID and an Identification Privilege Card for eligible applicants.

Proof of identity, legal presence and residency

Proof of identity

Virginia accepts a range of primary identity documents, including an unexpired U.S. passport or passport card, certified birth certificate, Certificate of Naturalization, Certificate of Citizenship and other documents listed in DMV's acceptable-documents guide.

Proof of legal presence

Virginia requires one document proving legal presence. A U.S. passport, certified U.S. birth certificate, Permanent Resident Card and qualifying immigration documents are examples of documents that may satisfy this requirement depending on the applicant.

Proof of Virginia residency

Two proofs of Virginia residency are generally required. Acceptable documents can include a utility bill, mortgage statement, lease, bank statement, insurance document, government correspondence or other documents listed by DMV.

If you do not want your residential address printed on your license or ID, Virginia DMV says you may provide an alternate mailing address, including a P.O. Box, in addition to your residence address.

Social Security number

If you have been issued an SSN, you must provide proof unless DMV can electronically verify the number you know. DMV requires proof of SSN for CDL applicants.

Name changes

If your legal name differs from your identity document, Virginia requires certified documentation connecting the names. Examples include a marriage certificate, divorce decree or certified court order.

Virginia REAL ID requirements

REAL ID is optional in Virginia. A REAL ID-compliant driver's license or ID has a star in the upper-right corner and can be used for federal purposes covered by the REAL ID Act.

For a REAL ID application, Virginia DMV requires one proof of identity, two proofs of Virginia residency, one proof of legal presence, proof of SSN if issued and name-change documentation when applicable. The DMV also charges a one-time $10 REAL ID fee in addition to the normal credential fee.

Virginia requires the first REAL ID application to be completed in person with physical documentation. After receiving a REAL ID credential, eligible customers may renew it online according to the normal renewal schedule.

REAL ID is not required to drive or vote. Virginia's standard credential remains valid for ordinary state purposes. A standard credential may not be accepted for domestic air travel or certain federal facilities where a REAL ID or another federally accepted document is required.

Virginia Driver Privilege Card

Virginia offers a Driver Privilege Card to certain Virginia residents who cannot meet the documentation requirements for a standard driver's license. The card is a driving credential and is not REAL ID compliant.

The Driver Privilege Card is distinct from a standard driver's license and a REAL ID. Virginia's voter identification materials also state that a Driver Privilege Card is not an accepted form of voter identification at the polls.

Virginia voter ID requirements

Virginia voters casting a ballot in person are asked to show one acceptable form of identification. The Virginia Department of Elections lists several accepted forms, including a Virginia driver's license, Virginia DMV-issued ID card, U.S. passport or passport card, U.S. military ID, qualifying student ID, certain government IDs, a Virginia voter ID card and certain tribal IDs.

Some documents without a photo can also qualify, including a current utility bill, bank statement, government check or paycheck containing the voter's name and address, as well as certain current government documents.

A Virginia driver's license or DMV-issued ID can be accepted even when expired. Virginia election officials state that voters who do not present acceptable identification may sign an ID confirmation statement or, if they do neither, vote a provisional ballot.

Election rules are separate from DMV rules. REAL ID status does not determine whether a Virginia credential can be used for voting. Check the Virginia Department of Elections for current election-specific guidance.

Renewing a Virginia license or ID

Virginia driver's licenses are generally issued for eight years for eligible applicants. The standard driver's license fee is calculated at $4 per year, with an eight-year standard license currently costing $32.

Eligible customers can renew a standard credential online. REAL ID applicants must make their first REAL ID application in person, but after receiving the first REAL ID, eligible renewals may be completed online under the normal schedule.

When renewing or replacing a credential, DMV may require updated documentation if your record or credential requires it.

Lost, stolen or damaged Virginia ID

Virginia DMV allows customers to replace eligible driver's licenses and ID credentials. A standard replacement driver's license, Driver Privilege Card, motorcycle license or CDL currently costs $20.

If you are replacing a credential and changing your name, address or credential type, additional documentation or an in-person visit may be required.

Name and address changes

When your legal name changes, Virginia DMV requires documentation showing the connection between your old and new name. Certified marriage certificates, divorce decrees and court orders are common examples.

Virginia also allows an alternate address to be used on a license or ID when the customer does not want the residential address printed on the credential. The DMV still requires the actual Virginia residence address for eligibility and record purposes.

Virginia ID requirements for non-U.S. citizens

Non-U.S. citizens may qualify for Virginia driver's licenses or ID cards depending on their immigration and documentation status. DMV verifies legal presence using the documents required for the applicant's situation.

Some applicants receive credentials with limited duration when their authorized stay is temporary. Virginia also provides a Driver Privilege Card for qualifying residents who cannot establish legal presence under the standard licensing process.

For a REAL ID, Virginia requires documentation establishing legal presence in addition to identity, residency and Social Security documentation.

Fake IDs and fraudulent identification in Virginia

A Virginia driver's license or ID card is an official government credential. Counterfeit, altered or misused identification can create legal consequences that are separate from ordinary licensing requirements.

For general educational background on what a fake ID is and how counterfeit, altered and legitimate identification differ, see Fake ID vs. Identity Theft: Where the Legal Line Is from FakeIDs.com.

This site does not provide instructions for creating, altering or using fraudulent identification. For a specific legal matter, consult a qualified Virginia attorney or the relevant government agency.

Virginia ID law FAQs

How long do I have to get a Virginia driver's license after moving there?

Virginia DMV says new residents must obtain a Virginia driver's license within 60 days of moving to Virginia. Commercial driver's license holders have a 30-day deadline.

How many proofs of residency does Virginia require?

Virginia generally requires two proofs of Virginia residency for a driver's license, learner's permit or ID card application.

Is REAL ID mandatory in Virginia?

No. REAL ID is optional. A standard Virginia license or ID remains valid for state purposes but may not satisfy federal identification requirements such as domestic air travel.

Can I get a Virginia ID instead of a driver's license?

Yes. Virginia residents who do not already hold a Virginia learner's permit or driver's license can apply for a state ID. A person cannot hold a Virginia driver's license and ID card at the same time.

Does Virginia require photo ID to vote?

Virginia asks in-person voters to show an acceptable form of identification. The accepted list includes photo IDs and certain current documents showing the voter's name and address. A voter who does not have acceptable ID can use the process provided by election officials, including an ID confirmation statement or provisional ballot.

Is a Virginia Driver Privilege Card a voter ID?

No. Virginia's Department of Elections lists the Driver Privilege Card as not accepted for voter identification.

Can I renew my Virginia REAL ID online?

After receiving your first REAL ID, eligible customers may renew online according to the standard renewal schedule. The first REAL ID application must be completed in person with physical documentation.

What does Federal Limits Apply mean on a Virginia license?

It means the credential is not REAL ID compliant. You can still use the standard credential for ordinary state purposes, but you need another federally accepted form of identification for certain federal purposes.

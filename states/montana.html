Montana ID Laws: What You Need to Know

Montana issues driver's licenses and identification cards through the Motor Vehicle Division. This guide covers Montana ID requirements, REAL ID, proof of residency, voter identification, renewal, replacement, fees and rules for non-U.S. citizens.

Last reviewed: September 26, 2026

Montana driver's license requirements

Montana's standard noncommercial driver's license is available through the Motor Vehicle Division. New applicants must establish identity, authorized presence and Montana residency. Applicants also complete the applicable licensing and testing steps required for their age and license type.

Proof of identity

Montana generally requires two identity documents: either two primary documents or one primary and one secondary document. Accepted primary documents include a certified U.S. or Canadian birth certificate, valid U.S. passport, qualifying military ID and certain immigration documents. A Social Security card is listed as a secondary document.

Authorized presence

Applicants for a new Montana license must provide documentation establishing that they are a U.S. citizen or legally authorized to be in the United States. Montana lists certified birth certificates, valid U.S. passports and qualifying immigration documents among the accepted evidence.

New residents

People transferring a driver's license from another state must establish identity, authorized presence and Montana residency. A current out-of-state driver's license can be used as one of the accepted identity documents when it meets Montana's requirements.

Teen drivers

Montana uses graduated licensing for younger drivers. Applicants move through learner and restricted licensing stages before becoming eligible for a regular driver's license, with age, supervised-driving and testing requirements applying to the relevant stage.

Montana identification cards

Montana ID cards are available to Montana residents who need an official identification credential but do not need a driver's license. The application is completed in person at an MVD Driver Exam Station. Mail-in applications are not available for an initial ID card.

The current MVD fee schedule lists a standard ID card at $17 and a standard validity period of eight years. A REAL ID-compliant ID card carries an additional REAL ID fee. Montana MVD ID card information

One Montana credential at a time

Montana residents may have only one Montana credential at a time. The MVD states that a person must choose between a driver's license and a state ID card, whether the credential is standard or REAL ID compliant.

Documents needed for a Montana ID

Montana divides its document requirements into identity, authorized presence and Montana residency. The MVD says applicants do not need to show a physical Social Security card for any transaction, but they must know their SSN so it can be verified at the appointment. Montana MVD document checklist

Identity documents

Montana requires two documents to prove full legal name and age. The combination can be two primary documents or one primary and one secondary document. The MVD's primary-document list includes certified birth certificates, passports, qualifying licenses and military documents. Secondary documents include Social Security cards, marriage records and certain school, government and court records.

Social Security number

Unlike states that require a Social Security card as a standard document, Montana says proof of the SSN is not required for transactions. The applicant must know the SSN so MVD can verify it electronically, and the name on file with the Social Security Administration must match the current legal name.

Proof of Montana residency

Applicants must establish a Montana residence. For a standard credential, Montana accepts documents such as an insurance policy or statement, rental agreement, recent bank statement, utility bill, first-class mail, Montana voter registration card, property-tax receipt, tax return or mortgage papers.

Physical address matters

The residency document must establish where the applicant physically resides. If the applicant does not receive mail at the residence, Montana requires a mailing address or P.O. Box in addition to the residence information.

Homeless applicants

Montana has a process for applicants who are homeless. A descriptive address of the location where the applicant actually resides can be used, together with a separate mailing address and a written statement from a social service agency verifying the applicant's homeless status and the descriptive address.

Montana REAL ID requirements

Montana does not require residents to obtain REAL ID. However, since May 7, 2025, a REAL ID-compliant license or ID, or another TSA-accepted form of identification, is required for covered federal purposes such as boarding domestic commercial flights. Montana MVD REAL ID information

What you need for REAL ID

  • One document proving full legal name, date of birth and authorized presence, or the combination of documents Montana allows for those elements
  • Two Montana residency documents from acceptable sources
  • Documentation of every legal name change when the current name differs from the submitted documents
  • Your complete Social Security number for electronic verification
  • An in-person visit to an MVD location

Montana says REAL ID does not transfer state-to-state. Even if an applicant previously held a REAL ID in another state, the applicant must provide Montana's required documentation.

Look for the star. Montana REAL ID driver licenses and ID cards have a star inside a circle in the upper-right corner. Standard credentials issued after January 2019 display “NOT FOR FEDERAL IDENTIFICATION PURPOSES.”

Montana citizenship marker

Montana introduced a separate citizenship credential marker in 2026. The marker is a black eagle-in-flight symbol that identifies qualifying U.S. citizens. It can appear on new or renewed Montana driver's licenses and ID cards issued on or after January 1, 2026.

Acceptable documentation for the marker includes a valid U.S. passport, government-certified U.S. birth certificate, Certificate of Citizenship, Certificate of Naturalization or Consular Report of Birth Abroad.

The citizenship marker is not REAL ID. Montana MVD specifically states that the marker does not replace REAL ID and does not satisfy TSA's REAL ID or passport requirement for domestic travel.

Through September 30, 2026, Montana has announced a temporary fee waiver for certain in-person transactions when the purpose is adding the citizenship marker, subject to the MVD's conditions and available funding. The waiver does not cover new or renewal REAL ID fees. Montana MVD citizenship marker information

Montana voter identification requirements

Montana requires voters to provide identification when voting at a polling place. The Secretary of State says an acceptable ID can be a current photo ID showing the voter's name. If the voter does not have a photo ID, Montana also accepts certain documents showing the voter's name and current address, including a current utility bill, bank statement, paycheck, notice of confirmation of voter registration or government document. citeturn1search7

Other ways to satisfy the requirement

Montana's polling-place procedures allow election officials to validate voter information in the statewide voter registration system in certain circumstances. A voter who cannot provide the required information can return with an acceptable ID or cast a provisional ballot under the applicable rules. citeturn1search8

Common photo IDs

Montana election officials commonly point voters to documents such as a driver's license, passport or Tribal ID. The Secretary of State's detailed rules should be checked before an election because acceptable identification and election procedures can change. citeturn1search0

Voting and address changes

Montana voters are responsible for updating their registration information when their residential or mailing address changes and when their legal name changes. A voter can update registration information through the county election office or during certain MVD transactions. citeturn1search1

Renewing a Montana driver's license or ID

Montana allows eligible driver's license and ID holders to renew online or by mail. Eligibility depends on the credential and renewal history. Montana generally does not allow consecutive mail or online renewals, and a person must renew in person when the remote-renewal conditions are not met. citeturn0search2

Renewal timing

A driver license holder can generally renew when the license will expire within six months or when it has been expired for no more than one year, subject to MVD eligibility rules. In-person renewals are required for people who do not qualify for remote renewal.

When renewing in person, applicants should bring the expiring credential or proof of identity if the credential is unavailable. Name changes require supporting documentation. A temporary license is issued while the new credential is mailed, generally within two to four weeks. citeturn1search4

Replacing a lost, stolen or damaged Montana ID

Montana allows eligible driver's license and ID holders to request a replacement online, in person or by mail for qualifying transactions. The MVD recommends reporting a stolen driver's license or identification card to local law enforcement.

The current replacement fee listed by MVD is $10.30 for a standard driver's license replacement and $10.30 for a standard ID card replacement. A REAL ID transaction can have different fees. citeturn1search2

Name and address changes

Montana requires legal documentation for a name change. Before changing the name on a Montana credential, MVD says the name must first be changed with the Social Security Administration and then the applicant must visit a driver license station.

Acceptable supporting records can include a certified marriage certificate, court order, divorce decree specifying the name change, naturalization certificate or qualifying declaration of marriage. Montana requires documentation connecting each legal name change to the current requested name.

Address changes

Montana allows an electronic address update without a fee. If the applicant wants the new address printed on a standard or REAL ID credential, an in-person appointment and replacement fee are required. citeturn0search2

Montana IDs for non-U.S. citizens

Montana issues credentials to foreign nationals who establish identity, authorized presence and Montana residency. The MVD accepts qualifying foreign passports and USCIS documents as identity and authorized-presence evidence.

Temporary lawful status

Temporary or permanent foreign nationals may be required to provide authorized-presence documents when applying for or renewing a Montana credential. The validity period can be limited by the applicant's authorized stay in the United States.

Montana's required-document rules also apply when a credential has expired beyond the state's grace period. Foreign-national applicants should check the MVD checklist before an appointment because the exact documents depend on immigration status. Montana MVD authorized-presence requirements

Counterfeit, altered and borrowed identification

A Montana driver's license or ID card is a government credential issued after the applicant's identity, authorized presence and residency have been verified. A counterfeit card, altered credential or another person's genuine card does not become valid identification for the person presenting it.

Montana ID law FAQs

Does Montana require REAL ID?

No. Montana makes REAL ID optional for state purposes, but a REAL ID-compliant credential or another TSA-accepted ID is required for covered federal purposes such as domestic air travel.

How many residency documents are needed for a Montana REAL ID?

Two Montana residency documents are required. They must show the applicant's name and current physical address.

Can I get a Montana ID without a driver's license?

Yes. Montana issues state identification cards to residents. However, Montana allows only one state credential at a time, so a person cannot hold both a Montana driver's license and Montana ID card simultaneously.

Does Montana require photo ID to vote?

Montana requires identification at the polls. A current photo ID is one option, but the state also accepts certain documents showing the voter's name and current address.

What is the black eagle on Montana IDs?

The black eagle-in-flight is a citizenship marker introduced in 2026 for qualifying U.S. citizens. It is separate from the REAL ID star and does not replace REAL ID.

Can non-U.S. citizens get a Montana driver's license?

Eligible foreign nationals can obtain Montana credentials after establishing identity, authorized presence and Montana residency. Their credential may be subject to validity limits based on lawful status.

Can I renew a Montana license online?

Some applicants qualify for online or mail renewal. Montana does not allow consecutive remote renewals, and applicants who do not meet the eligibility conditions must renew in person.

How much does a Montana REAL ID cost?

Montana adds $25 to the applicable driver's license or ID fee for REAL ID. The underlying credential fee varies by age and credential type.

Missouri ID Laws: What You Need to Know

Missouri issues driver's licenses and nondriver identification cards through the Department of Revenue. This guide explains Missouri ID requirements, REAL ID, residency documents, voter identification, renewal, replacement, fees and rules for non-U.S. citizens.

Last reviewed: September 26, 2026

Missouri's Department of Revenue publishes separate requirements for REAL ID and non-REAL-ID credentials. The exact documents needed can depend on citizenship, immigration status, name changes and whether the applicant is a new resident. Check the current DOR document requirements.

Missouri driver's license requirements

Missouri's noncommercial driver's license system uses Class F for ordinary passenger driving. Applicants must establish identity, lawful status, Social Security number and Missouri residency before receiving a credential. The Missouri Department of Revenue also requires the applicable written, vision, road-sign and driving tests unless an exemption applies.

New Missouri residents

A person moving to Missouri with a valid out-of-state driver's license or nondriver ID must provide proof of identity, lawful status, Social Security number and Missouri residential address. A REAL ID applicant must provide two acceptable Missouri residency documents from different sources. An out-of-state credential that is expired by no more than 184 days may also be used in the situations described by DOR. Missouri DOR license information

Lost out-of-state license

If an out-of-state license was lost, stolen or destroyed, Missouri DOR allows certain applicants to use a current clearance letter or qualifying lost-license affidavit containing information needed to establish the prior credential and waive Missouri knowledge and skills testing when eligible.

Teen drivers

Missouri uses a graduated licensing system for younger drivers. The process includes an instruction permit, intermediate license and later a full Class F license, with age and supervised-driving requirements applying at each stage.

Missouri nondriver identification cards

Missouri offers nondriver identification cards to residents who need an official photo credential but do not hold a driver's license. The standard Class ND card has no minimum age. Missouri also issues a nondriver license specifically for voting purposes to qualifying applicants age 17½ and older. Missouri DOR fee and credential information

One ID for voting purposes

The Missouri nondriver license for voting purposes is available without a fee to qualifying voters who do not already possess an acceptable photo ID. The Secretary of State directs voters to the Department of Revenue's license offices for this credential.

Validity

Missouri's current fee chart lists the standard Class ND nondriver ID at $24 and the voting-purpose nondriver ID at no fee. The standard nondriver ID may be issued for six years or, in qualifying cases, as a non-expiring card for older residents.

Documents needed for a Missouri ID

Missouri organizes its identity-document requirements into four main categories: identity, lawful status, Social Security number and Missouri residency. Additional documents may be needed for an alternate mailing address or legal name change. See the Missouri DOR document list

Identity and lawful status

For REAL ID applications, documents must be valid and unexpired and must establish both identity and lawful status. A certified U.S. birth certificate is one accepted identity document, while U.S. passports and qualifying immigration documents are among the other documents accepted under Missouri's rules.

Social Security number

Missouri requires documentation showing the applicant's name and Social Security number. A Social Security card must be signed if the applicant is 18 or older and cannot be laminated. Applicants who have never been assigned an SSN can provide the applicable Social Security Administration documentation.

Previously verified SSN

A current Missouri credential holder with a previously verified Social Security number may be eligible to provide the number verbally so DOR can complete an electronic verification inquiry. If verification fails, physical documentation may be required.

Proof of Missouri residency

Missouri's residency requirement depends on whether the credential is REAL ID compliant.

CredentialResidency documentation
Non-REAL-ID driver's license or nondriver IDOne acceptable document showing the applicant's name and Missouri residential address.
REAL ID driver's license or nondriver IDTwo acceptable documents from different sources showing the applicant's Missouri residential address.

A Post Office Box by itself is not accepted as a residential address. DOR's acceptable-document list includes items such as utility bills, bank statements, lease documents and other qualifying records.

Self-certification at renewal

Some current Missouri credential holders may be eligible to self-certify that their residence and mailing address have not changed if the information was previously verified and stored by DOR. If the address has changed, supporting address documents are required.

Missouri REAL ID requirements

Missouri REAL ID is optional for ordinary state purposes, but it provides a credential that meets federal REAL ID standards. A Missouri REAL ID-compliant driver's license or nondriver ID displays a star in the upper-right corner. A noncompliant credential says “NOT FOR REAL ID PURPOSES.” Missouri DOR REAL ID information

What you need for REAL ID

  • One acceptable identity document
  • One document proving U.S. citizenship or qualifying lawful immigration status
  • One document showing the Social Security number
  • Two Missouri residency documents from different sources
  • Additional legal name-change documents when the current name differs from identity or lawful-status documents
REAL ID is not mandatory for every Missouri resident. Missouri allows both REAL ID-compliant and noncompliant credentials. The choice matters when a credential is being used for a federal purpose covered by REAL ID rules.

Missouri DOR says the fees for a REAL ID credential are the same as the corresponding non-REAL-ID credential. Applicants should allow time for processing and mailing of a new REAL ID credential.

Missouri voter identification requirements

Missouri voters who are registered can vote by presenting an acceptable form of identification and signing the poll book. The Secretary of State lists a nonexpired Missouri driver's license or nondriver license, nonexpired military ID, nonexpired U.S. passport or another qualifying photo ID issued by the United States or Missouri. A Missouri driver's or nondriver license that expired after the most recent general election is also accepted. Missouri Secretary of State voter ID rules

If you do not have an acceptable photo ID

A registered voter without one of the listed photo IDs can cast a provisional ballot. The ballot can count if the voter returns to the polling place on Election Day with acceptable photo ID or if the local election authority determines that the signature on the provisional ballot envelope matches the voter's registration record.

Free Missouri voter ID

Missouri provides one nondriver license at no charge to Missourians who want a photo ID for voting and do not already possess an acceptable form of photo identification. The Secretary of State directs eligible voters to DOR license offices for this service.

Absentee voting

Missouri has additional identification rules for some absentee voters, including certain voters who registered by mail and have not previously voted in person. The Secretary of State lists exemptions for overseas voters, active-duty military voters and certain permanently disabled voters and caregivers.

Renewing a Missouri driver's license or ID

Missouri allows eligible applicants to renew remotely through MyDMV. Current DOR rules state that eligible applicants must generally be between ages 21 and 49, have a U.S. citizenship verification indicator on file and meet other renewal conditions. Applicants with USCIS immigration documents must apply in person and submit those documents for review and SAVE verification.

A remote renewal is generally available only when the credential is eligible for renewal and the applicant meets the department's driver-history and vision requirements. Missouri allows a credential to be renewed up to 184 days after expiration under the applicable rules.

Renewal documents

Renewal applicants may need proof of identity, lawful status, Social Security number and Missouri residency. Some current cardholders can self-certify previously verified address information if nothing has changed.

Replacing a lost, stolen or damaged Missouri ID

Missouri provides duplicate driver's license and nondriver ID services. The current DOR fee chart lists duplicate fees that depend on the credential and its prior validity period. A duplicate can also be used for certain eligible personal-information changes.

Missouri's REAL ID guidance also describes a one-time waiver of the duplicate transaction fee for qualifying people who have not previously been issued a REAL ID-compliant credential. Applicable license-office processing fees are not waived.

Name and address changes

Missouri requires supporting documentation when a legal name changes. Examples include a certified marriage license, divorce decree, court order, adoption papers or amended birth certificate that meets DOR's document standards.

If the Missouri residential address changes, the applicant must provide acceptable address verification. Some applicants with previously verified information may self-certify only when the address remains unchanged.

Missouri IDs for non-U.S. citizens

Missouri issues driver's licenses and nondriver IDs to eligible non-U.S. citizens who establish identity, lawful status, Social Security information and Missouri residency. Immigration documents are reviewed as part of the issuance process.

Limited-term credentials

Missouri credentials may be marked LIMITED-TERM when the document used to establish lawful status supports a limited period of authorized stay. The expiration period depends on the lawful-status documentation and Missouri's applicable issuance rules.

SAVE verification

Applicants presenting USCIS-issued immigration documents must apply in person so Missouri can review the documents and complete the required Systematic Alien Verification for Entitlements (SAVE) verification process. Missouri DOR identity and lawful-status requirements

Counterfeit, altered and borrowed identification

A Missouri driver's license or nondriver ID is a government-issued credential tied to the identity information verified during issuance. A counterfeit card, an altered genuine card or another person's genuine credential does not become a valid Missouri ID for the person presenting it.

Missouri ID law FAQs

Does Missouri require REAL ID?

No. Missouri offers both REAL ID-compliant and non-REAL-ID credentials. A REAL ID credential has a star in the upper-right corner.

How many residency documents are needed for a Missouri REAL ID?

Two acceptable documents from different sources showing the Missouri residential address are required.

Can I get a Missouri ID without a driver's license?

Yes. Missouri issues nondriver identification cards and has a separate voting-purpose nondriver license.

Does Missouri require photo ID to vote?

Missouri voters generally present an acceptable photo ID. Registered voters who do not have an acceptable ID can use the state's provisional-ballot process.

Can I get a free Missouri voter ID?

Yes. Missouri provides one nondriver license at no charge to qualifying Missourians who need a photo ID for voting and do not already have one.

Can non-U.S. citizens get a Missouri driver's license?

Eligible non-U.S. citizens can receive Missouri credentials after establishing identity, lawful status, Social Security information and residency. Immigration documents may require in-person review and SAVE verification.

Can I renew my Missouri license online?

Some applicants are eligible for remote renewal through MyDMV. Eligibility depends on age, citizenship verification, prior issuance and other DOR requirements.

Is a Missouri nondriver ID the same as a driver's license?

No. A nondriver ID is an identification credential and does not authorize the holder to operate a motor vehicle.

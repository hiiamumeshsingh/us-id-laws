South Dakota ID Laws: What You Need to Know

South Dakota has specific documentation rules for driver's licenses, non-driver identification cards and federally compliant credentials. The state also requires identification when voting. This guide explains South Dakota's ID requirements, residency documents, REAL ID, voter identification, renewals, replacements, fees and non-U.S. citizen rules.

Last reviewed: September 26, 2026

South Dakota driver's license requirements

To obtain a South Dakota driver's license, applicants complete the application, provide the required documents and pay the $28 application fee. New drivers must also pass the applicable vision, knowledge and driving tests. The knowledge and driving tests require a score of at least 80 percent. citeturn2search1turn2search4

Identity and lawful status

New applicants and people transferring from another state must provide proof of lawful status. South Dakota DPS lists documents such as a certified U.S. birth certificate, valid U.S. passport or passport card, Certificate of Citizenship or Naturalization, Permanent Resident Card, Employment Authorization Card or qualifying foreign passport with U.S. visa and I-94. citeturn2search0

Social Security number

Applicants whose current credential is not already federally compliant may need to provide an original Social Security card, W-2, 1099 or pay stub showing the full Social Security number. South Dakota DPS does not accept photocopies for these documents. citeturn2search0

Testing

First-time applicants must pass a vision screening, knowledge test and driving test when required. The driving test must be scheduled at a South Dakota Driver Exam Station. citeturn2search4

Teen drivers

South Dakota uses a graduated licensing system for younger drivers. Applicants under 18 have additional parental-consent and driver-education requirements, and restricted permits can later be upgraded to an operator's license when the applicable conditions are met. citeturn2search2

South Dakota state ID requirements

South Dakota's non-driver identification card has no minimum age. It is intended for people who need official identification but do not hold a South Dakota driver's license. citeturn1search5

Who can get a South Dakota ID?

  • South Dakota residents who need a non-driver identification credential
  • Applicants who meet the state's identity and lawful-status documentation requirements
  • Applicants who provide two documents showing their physical/residential address

South Dakota does not allow a person to maintain an active driver's license and an active ID card as separate credentials for the same driving identity. When a South Dakota credential is issued after an out-of-state credential is transferred, the prior credential must be surrendered. citeturn1search4

Expiration for younger applicants

If an ID card holder is under 21 and has less than five years remaining until their 21st birthday, the card expires 30 days after the 21st birthday. citeturn1search5

Replacement and renewal

Eligible ID card holders may renew online or by mail. A duplicate or replacement ID card can generally be requested online or by mail once every 10 years, subject to eligibility requirements. citeturn1search5turn1search7

Documents needed for a South Dakota ID

South Dakota DPS maintains a current required-document checklist for driver's licenses and ID cards. New applicants, transfers and non-U.S. citizens must provide proof of lawful status, while all applicants must provide two documents showing their physical/residential address. citeturn2search0

Proof of identity and lawful status

Accepted documents can include a certified U.S. birth certificate, valid U.S. passport, Certificate of Citizenship or Naturalization, Permanent Resident Card, Employment Authorization Card or qualifying foreign passport with U.S. visa and I-94. Only original or certified documents are accepted where the DPS checklist requires them. citeturn2search0

Social Security documentation

If required for the transaction, South Dakota accepts an original Social Security card, W-2, 1099 or pay stub showing the full SSN. Applicants transferring from another state whose current license is not federally compliant may need to provide one of these documents. citeturn2search0

Name changes

Applicants whose legal name has changed must provide a certified marriage certificate, divorce decree or court order. Name-change transactions must be handled at a Driver Licensing location. citeturn2search0turn2search1

Proof of South Dakota residency

South Dakota requires two documents proving a physical or residential address. The documents must show the applicant's name and physical/residential address and generally must be less than one year old. Handwritten documents are not accepted. citeturn2search0

P.O. Box

A P.O. Box can appear on one address document, but the second document must show the applicant's physical/residential address. The state does not treat a P.O. Box alone as the physical residence. citeturn2search0

Minors

Parent or guardian mail can be used as address documentation for applicants under 18. Additional parental-consent requirements may apply. citeturn2search0turn2search2

Full-time travelers

South Dakota has a specific process for full-time travelers and people who use mail-forwarding services. They may need a notarized residency affidavit, proof of a one-night stay in South Dakota within the last year and documentation of a personal mailbox service address. Virtual addresses are not accepted. citeturn1search4turn1search6

South Dakota REAL ID requirements

South Dakota issues federally compliant driver's licenses and ID cards marked with a gold star in the upper-right corner. A federally compliant card can be used for federal identification purposes covered by the REAL ID Act. citeturn2search0

REAL ID checklist

  • Proof of identity and lawful status
  • Social Security documentation when required
  • Two documents proving South Dakota physical/residential address
  • Certified name-change documents when applicable

South Dakota's current documentation page says all applicants must provide two physical-address documents and explains the additional SSN documentation required when a current credential is not already federally compliant. citeturn2search0

Gold-star credential

The gold star in the upper-right corner identifies a federally compliant South Dakota credential. If a person transfers from another state and the current card does not have a compliant marking, South Dakota requires the additional documentation needed to establish federal compliance. citeturn2search0

Renewal eligibility

Applicants with a federally compliant gold-star credential may be able to renew online or by mail if they meet the state's eligibility rules. Online and mail renewal generally require two recent address documents. Only U.S. citizens and permanent residents can use the online renewal process. citeturn1search6turn2search3

New South Dakota residents

People moving to South Dakota who hold an out-of-state driver's license must transfer to a South Dakota credential. The state does not allow a person to remain licensed in two states. citeturn1search4

Transfer documents

New applicants and out-of-state transfers must provide proof of lawful status, Social Security information when required and two documents showing the South Dakota physical/residential address. If the existing credential is not federally compliant, additional documents may be needed for a South Dakota gold-star credential. citeturn2search0

Full-time travelers

South Dakota has special residency documentation for full-time travelers who live in an RV or travel for work. A residency affidavit and proof of a recent South Dakota stay can be required, along with documentation of a personal mailbox service address. citeturn1search4

South Dakota voter identification requirements

South Dakota requires voters to present valid personal identification when requesting a ballot. The Secretary of State lists a South Dakota driver's license or non-driver ID, U.S. government photo ID, tribal photo ID and a current student photo ID from a South Dakota high school or accredited higher-education institution as accepted forms. citeturn0search1turn0search2

Accepted photo ID

  • South Dakota driver's license
  • South Dakota non-driver identification card
  • U.S. passport or other U.S. government photo ID
  • U.S. Armed Forces ID
  • Tribal photo ID
  • Current student photo ID from a South Dakota high school or accredited institution of higher education

South Dakota's 2026 election-worker guidance also states that an expired South Dakota driver's license can still be accepted as voter identification. citeturn0search9

No acceptable ID

A voter who cannot present one of the listed identification documents may sign a personal identification affidavit. The Secretary of State says the voter can then vote a regular ballot. citeturn0search2

Absentee voting

South Dakota generally requires an absentee-ballot application to be notarized or accompanied by a copy of an acceptable photo ID. UOCAVA voters are subject to separate identification rules. citeturn0search1turn0search10

Voter registration and citizenship proof

South Dakota changed voter-registration requirements in 2026. Under SB 175, effective March 26, 2026, new registrants must provide documentary proof of U.S. citizenship to be registered as full-ballot voters. Acceptable documents include qualifying South Dakota or other state credentials showing citizenship has been verified, a tribal identification card or a copy of a birth certificate, U.S. passport, Consular Report of Birth Abroad or certificate of naturalization. If proof of citizenship is not provided, the voter may be registered as a federal-only voter under the current rules. citeturn0search0turn0search6

2026 registration deadline

For the November 3, 2026 general election, South Dakota's voter-registration deadline is October 19, 2026. The state's current election calendar also lists September 18, 2026 as the start of absentee voting. citeturn0search5

Renewing a South Dakota driver's license or ID

A South Dakota driver's license is generally valid for five years. You can renew up to 180 days before expiration, except for the special timing rule around a driver's 21st birthday. citeturn2search1

Online and mail renewal

Eligible U.S. citizens and permanent residents may be able to renew online or by mail. South Dakota says you are generally eligible when you have an unexpired federally compliant credential and did not use online or mail renewal for your last renewal five years earlier. citeturn1search6turn2search3

Renewal after expiration

If a driver's license has been expired for more than 30 days, the driver must visit a Driver Licensing location and pass the required knowledge test. An expired motorcycle endorsement can also require the motorcycle knowledge test. citeturn1search11turn2search3

Age 65 and older

Drivers age 65 or older who renew online or by mail must provide a vision statement completed by an eye-care professional within the previous year. citeturn1search6turn2search3

Replacing a lost, stolen or damaged South Dakota ID

Eligible customers may request a duplicate or replacement driver's license or ID online, by mail or in person. A duplicate driver's license or ID requested by mail currently carries a $15 duplication fee. citeturn1search7

Replacement documents

South Dakota requires two recent physical-address documents for replacement transactions. If the applicant's name has changed, the name change must be handled in person with certified documentation. citeturn1search0turn1search7

Online replacement limits

A replacement or duplicate license or ID can generally be requested online or by mail once every 10 years, subject to eligibility. citeturn1search11turn1search5

South Dakota name and address changes

Name changes

Name changes require a visit to a Driver Licensing location and certified documentation such as a marriage certificate, divorce decree or court order. Applicants also need two documents proving their South Dakota residence under the current name. citeturn2search1

Address changes

Address documentation must show the applicant's name and physical/residential address and generally be less than one year old. If a P.O. Box is used, another document must show the physical residence. citeturn2search0

South Dakota IDs for non-U.S. citizens

South Dakota allows qualifying non-U.S. citizens to obtain driver's licenses and ID cards, but they must apply in person at a state Driver Exam Station and provide proof of lawful status. citeturn2search1turn2search4

Lawful-status documents

South Dakota DPS lists a Permanent Resident Card, Employment Authorization Card, qualifying foreign passport with U.S. visa and I-94, Certificate of Citizenship or Naturalization and other approved documents as evidence of lawful status. citeturn2search0

Renewal

Only U.S. citizens and permanent residents can use the online renewal process. Non-U.S. citizens must provide proof of legal status when renewing at a Driver Licensing location. citeturn1search6turn1search9

Commercial credentials

New commercial driver's licenses and non-U.S. citizen applications must be handled at a state Driver Licensing location rather than a county office. citeturn2search1

South Dakota ID law FAQs

Does South Dakota have REAL ID?

Yes. South Dakota issues federally compliant driver's licenses and ID cards marked with a gold star.

Is REAL ID mandatory in South Dakota?

The state issues both federally compliant and other credentials. A gold-star credential is the federally compliant option for covered federal identification purposes.

How many proof-of-address documents do I need for a South Dakota ID?

South Dakota generally requires two documents showing your name and physical/residential address, normally less than one year old.

How long is a South Dakota driver's license valid?

A South Dakota driver's license is generally valid for five years. Special expiration rules apply to drivers under 21.

Does South Dakota require ID to vote?

Yes. Voters must present an accepted form of personal identification when requesting a ballot, although an identification affidavit can be used when a voter cannot present an accepted ID.

What happens if I do not have an ID when voting?

You can sign a personal identification affidavit and still vote a regular ballot under the current South Dakota rules.

Can non-U.S. citizens get a South Dakota driver's license?

Qualifying non-U.S. citizens can apply in person by providing the lawful-status and other documentation required by South Dakota DPS.

How much does a South Dakota driver's license cost?

The current standard operator's license application fee is $28.

What changed for South Dakota voter registration in 2026?

SB 175 took effect March 26, 2026 and changed proof-of-citizenship rules for new voter registrations. Applicants without the required citizenship documentation may be registered as federal-only voters under the current law.

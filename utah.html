Utah ID Laws: What You Need to Know

Utah has detailed requirements for driver's licenses, identification cards and federally compliant credentials. The state also requires identification when voting in person. This guide covers Utah identity and residency documents, REAL ID, voter ID, renewals, replacements, fees and requirements for non-U.S. citizens.

Utah driver's license requirements

Utah applicants must establish identity, date of birth and residency and provide the Social Security information required for the credential. Non-U.S. citizens must also provide documentation showing lawful status. The Driver License Division can require original or certified documentation and may verify information with the issuing agency. citeturn3search2

Regular Class D license

A regular Class D license allows the holder to operate ordinary personal vehicles within the license class. Utah's current handbook lists the regular Class D fee at $52 for applicants age 21 and older. Applicants under 21 receive a provisional Class D license and the listed fee is $39. citeturn3search2

Minimum age

Utah allows an original regular Class D license beginning at age 16. Applicants younger than 19 generally must complete an approved driver-education course or show qualifying licensing history from another state or country. citeturn3search2

Testing

New drivers complete the applicable vision, knowledge and driving-skills requirements. Teen applicants have additional graduated-licensing restrictions and parent or guardian requirements. citeturn3search2

Driving Privilege Card

Utah also issues a Driving Privilege Card to qualifying people who are not eligible for a regular or REAL ID credential. The DPC is for driving purposes only and is not valid identification or proof of age. It expires annually on the applicant's birthday. citeturn3search2

Utah state identification card requirements

Utah identification cards are available to people who need official identification and do not hold a Utah driver's license. The current Driver Handbook lists the standard ID card fee at $23 and the disability identification card fee at $17. citeturn3search2

Documents

  • Proof of identity
  • Proof of Utah residency
  • Social Security information when required
  • Proof of lawful status for applicants who are not U.S. citizens
  • Legal name-change documentation when applicable

A parent or guardian must sign the application for an applicant under age 16. Utah identification cards are generally valid for five years. citeturn3search4

Replacement

The current handbook lists a $23 duplicate-license fee, while current third-party summaries of Utah's ID-card schedule list replacement ID cards at $23. Applicants should confirm the exact transaction fee with the Driver License Division before payment. citeturn3search2turn3search4

Special free ID situations

Utah law provides fee waivers for certain applicants, including qualifying indigent or homeless adults who have not previously been issued a Utah driver's license. A free ID can also be available when a person must surrender a driver's license because of age or a medical condition. citeturn3search0turn3search4

Documents needed for a Utah ID

Utah's Driver License Division uses a document checklist to establish identity, lawful status, Social Security information and residency. Applicants should use the current DLD checklist for their transaction because acceptable documents vary by citizenship and credential type. citeturn3search2

Identity and lawful status

Common federal identity and status documents include a certified U.S. birth certificate, U.S. passport, Certificate of Naturalization or Citizenship, Permanent Resident Card, Employment Authorization Document and qualifying foreign passport or immigration documents. citeturn3search2

Social Security

Applicants generally provide a Social Security card or another approved document showing the SSN when required. The exact requirement depends on the credential and applicant's status. citeturn3search2

Name changes

Applicants whose current legal name differs from the identity document must provide the legal documents establishing the name change, such as a certified marriage certificate, divorce decree or court order. citeturn3search2

Proof of Utah residency

Utah generally requires two documents showing the applicant's Utah residential address. The documents are used to establish the applicant's current residence and must meet the Driver License Division's accepted-document rules. citeturn3search2

Common examples

  • Utility bill
  • Bank statement
  • Lease or mortgage document
  • Insurance policy
  • Government correspondence
  • Paycheck or employer document
  • Utah vehicle registration
  • Property-tax document

Physical residence

Utah's documentation rules distinguish a residential or physical address from a mailing address. A P.O. Box should not be treated as a substitute for the required Utah residential address.

Address changes

Utah requires drivers and ID holders to update an address after moving. Current DMV guidance allows eligible address changes online, while a new physical credential is not necessarily issued solely for the online address update. citeturn1search9

Utah REAL ID requirements

Utah issues federally compliant driver's licenses and identification cards. A REAL ID-compliant credential is accepted for covered federal identification purposes such as domestic air travel and entry to certain federal facilities. citeturn3search4

REAL ID checklist

  • Proof of identity
  • Proof of lawful status or U.S. citizenship
  • Social Security documentation
  • Two Utah residency documents
  • Legal name-change documents when applicable

Utah's Driver Handbook explains the identity and residency documentation framework, while the DLD's current checklist controls the exact documents accepted for a particular applicant. citeturn3search2

Gold-star credential

Utah's federally compliant driver's licenses and ID cards use the federal REAL ID marking. Applicants who want federal-compliant credentials should bring the full documentation package rather than assuming an existing Utah card automatically has the required federal status.

Federal uses

A compliant Utah credential can be used for covered federal purposes. A person who does not have a compliant state credential can generally use another federally accepted document, such as a U.S. passport, when applicable. citeturn3search4

New Utah residents

People moving to Utah who want a Utah driver's license must complete the Utah licensing process and provide the required identity, residency and Social Security documentation. The Driver License Division's out-of-state process addresses transfer applicants separately. citeturn3search2

Out-of-state license

Applicants transferring a driver's license from another state should bring the current license and required documentation. Utah's licensing system does not allow a person to maintain multiple active state driver's licenses.

New residents and REAL ID

A person transferring an out-of-state credential who wants a federally compliant Utah credential should bring the complete REAL ID documentation package rather than assuming the previous state's credential will transfer as a Utah REAL ID.

Utah voter identification requirements

Utah requires identification when voting in person. A registered voter can use one primary form of ID that is valid, has the voter's name and has a photograph, with an exception for tribal ID cards that do not require a photograph. Alternatively, two valid or recent documents can be used together when they establish the voter's name and current residence. citeturn1search0turn1search24

One primary ID

  • Utah driver's license
  • ID card issued by Utah or the U.S. government
  • Utah concealed-carry permit
  • U.S. passport
  • Tribal ID card
  • Currently valid U.S. military ID

Utah's current voter-ID page specifies that the primary ID must be valid and not expired, contain the voter's name and include a photograph, except for tribal ID. citeturn1search0

Two-document option

Voters can instead provide two forms of ID that together prove name and current residence. Examples include a certified birth certificate, Social Security card, government check, Utah hunting or fishing license, certified naturalization documents, current Utah vehicle registration, employer ID, Utah school ID, Medicaid or Medicare card and certain tribal documents. citeturn1search0

Provisional and same-day registration

Voters who are not already registered and register during early voting or on Election Day must provide two acceptable forms of ID: one establishing identity and one proving residence. Utah's 2026 election information confirms that same-day registration is available on November 3, 2026. citeturn1search1turn1search2

Mail voting

Utah conducts elections primarily through mailed ballots for active registered voters. The state's current election information says ballots for the November 3, 2026 general election are mailed beginning October 13, 2026. citeturn1search1

Renewing a Utah driver's license or ID

Utah's current Driver Handbook lists a standard driver's-license renewal at $52 and a reduced renewal fee of $27 for drivers age 65 and older. A regular Class D license is generally issued for eight years. citeturn3search2

Online renewal

Eligible Utah drivers can use online renewal services when they meet the Driver License Division's requirements. Renewal eligibility depends on the credential, age, previous renewal method and identity-verification history.

ID card renewal

Utah identification cards are generally valid for five years and can be renewed online or in person when the applicant is eligible for online renewal. citeturn3search4

Older drivers

Drivers age 65 and older have a lower renewal fee under the current handbook. Additional vision requirements can apply depending on the renewal method and transaction. citeturn3search2

Replacing a lost, stolen or damaged Utah ID

Utah's current Driver Handbook lists a $23 duplicate driver's-license fee. Replacement identification-card transactions also generally use the $23 standard ID fee, subject to the applicant's circumstances and any applicable fee waiver. citeturn3search2turn3search4

Replacement documents

Applicants should bring identity, residency and Social Security documentation when the DLD requires re-verification. The exact documents depend on the transaction and the applicant's record.

Lost or stolen credentials

If a credential was stolen and personal information may have been exposed, consider reporting the theft and monitoring accounts for possible identity misuse. The Utah Driver License Division's replacement process is the official route for obtaining a new credential.

Utah name and address changes

Name changes

Name changes require in-person documentation establishing the new legal name. A certified marriage certificate, divorce decree or court order can be used when applicable. citeturn3search2turn3search4

Address changes

Utah's voter information site states that a valid Utah driver's license or ID can be used to update a ballot mailing address online, and the driver's-license address can be changed online first. The voter system does not necessarily issue a new physical credential solely for an online address update. citeturn1search9

Ten-day update rule

Current Utah guidance states that drivers and ID holders should notify the DMV of an address change within 10 days. citeturn3search4

Utah IDs for non-U.S. citizens

Utah provides different credential types depending on an applicant's immigration status. Qualifying non-U.S. citizens with verified lawful status can receive limited-term credentials, while certain applicants who are not eligible for REAL ID can qualify for a Driving Privilege Card. citeturn3search2

Limited-term license

A limited-term driver's license is available to a non-U.S. citizen whose lawful status has been verified and who qualifies for a REAL ID credential. The current handbook lists a $32 fee for a limited-term license original or renewal. citeturn3search2

Driving Privilege Card

Utah's Driving Privilege Card can be issued to qualifying people who are not eligible for a REAL ID credential, including certain undocumented immigrants and people with qualifying parole or other status. The DPC is for driving purposes only and is not valid identification or age verification. It expires annually on the applicant's birthday. citeturn3search2

Documentation

Non-U.S. citizens should bring their current immigration documents, identity documents, Social Security documentation when applicable and two Utah residency documents. The DLD determines which documents apply to the person's status.

Counterfeit, altered and fraudulent identification

Utah's licensing process uses identity, residency, Social Security and lawful-status documentation to establish eligibility for state credentials. The state also distinguishes a Driving Privilege Card from a state identification card because the DPC is not valid identification or age verification. citeturn3search2

Utah ID law FAQs

Does Utah have REAL ID?

Yes. Utah issues federally compliant driver's licenses and identification cards that can be used for covered federal purposes.

How many residency documents do I need for a Utah ID?

Utah generally requires two documents showing your Utah residential address.

How long is a Utah driver's license valid?

A regular Utah Class D license is generally issued for eight years. Younger drivers have provisional-license rules.

Does Utah require ID to vote?

Yes. Registered voters voting in person can use one valid primary ID with name and photo, or two acceptable documents that together establish name and current residence.

Can I vote if I do not have a primary photo ID?

Yes. Utah permits voters to use two acceptable documents that together prove name and current residence, subject to the state's current election rules.

Can non-U.S. citizens get a Utah driver's license?

Qualifying non-U.S. citizens can receive limited-term licenses when their lawful status is verified. Utah also offers a Driving Privilege Card to certain people who are not eligible for a REAL ID credential.

How much is a Utah driver's license?

The current standard Class D fee is $52 for drivers age 21 and older. A provisional license for someone under 21 is $39.

How much is a Utah state ID?

The current standard identification card fee is $23. A disability identification card is $17, and qualifying fee waivers are available in certain circumstances.

How quickly must I update my Utah address?

Current Utah guidance says drivers and ID holders should notify the DMV of an address change within 10 days.
